## What is a double BBL?

A double BBL usually means a second BBL, done after the first has healed, to add volume or even out shape. It isn't one surgery that moves twice the fat. None of the sources we rely on defines it as a medical term, so ask any surgeon who uses the phrase exactly what they mean by it.

[ASPS's article on BBL recovery](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/six-things-you-need-to-know-about-recovering-from-a-brazilian-butt-lift) describes both reasons people come back: some return for "another BBL to smooth out any contour irregularities and asymmetry," and "others return seeking a bigger butt."

## Why do people get a second BBL?

Most often because of how much fat stayed, how even the result is, or how much size they wanted in the first place.

- **Some fat doesn't last.** [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/buttock-enhancement/procedure) says patients retain 60% to 80% of the transferred fat on average, in some cases more and in others less. A plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS puts the average "take" at about 60%. Losing some volume is expected, and it doesn't mean your first surgery failed.
- **The shape healed unevenly.** A [2026 meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1097/PRS.0000000000012437) found uneven contours in 2.29% of BBL patients. Our post on [fixing dents after a BBL](/how-to-fix-dents-after-bbl) covers what to do first.
- **The goal needed more fat than one session could safely place.** A [2018 safety advisory from a multi-society task force](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/documents/Patient-Safety/BBL/Gluteal-Fat-Grafting-Safety-Advisory_Jan18.pdf) says that if the goal requires more fat than fits in the layer under the skin, the surgeon should consider staging the procedure rather than injecting deep.
- **Your body changed later.** [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23308-brazilian-butt-lift) says you may need another BBL during your life, and ASPS says results can change as you gain or lose weight.

## When is a second BBL not the answer?

A second BBL isn't always what you need, and an exam should tell you. It may not be the right next step if:

- **Your result is still settling.** Swelling can hide your shape for months, as the next section explains.
- **You don't have enough fat left.** Cleveland Clinic says butt implants may suit you if you don't have enough excess fat.
- **Your concern is loose skin.** More fat doesn't remove skin. [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/buttock-enhancement/candidates) describes a separate buttock lift for significant looseness.
- **Your weight is still changing.** Cleveland Clinic says to maintain a consistent weight to preserve your result.

The [BBL page's options section](/procedures/brazilian-butt-lift-bbl-miami#options) explains how a revision starts: with an exam of what was done before, because some problems are corrected with more fat and some need a different plan.

## How long should you wait between BBLs?

No source we rely on gives a waiting period. You will see specific numbers of months online, but we couldn't find a source for any of them, so we won't give you one.

What the sources do say is when you can judge your first result:

- **Around month 3:** the acute risk of losing grafted fat subsides, according to a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS.
- **Months 3 to 6:** your final shape shows, once swelling has settled, according to [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/butt-lift/aftercare-recovery) and ASPS's recovery article.
- **A year:** ASPS's procedure page says you won't see your final results until a year after surgery.

So a decision made before 3 to 6 months is based on a result that is still changing. Your surgeon decides whether and when a second BBL makes sense after examining you.

## Do you have enough fat for a double BBL?

Maybe not, and that is the first thing to check. A second BBL needs fat again, and your first surgery already took some from your donor areas. Cleveland Clinic says you need [enough excess fat](/blog/do-i-have-enough-fat-for-a-bbl) in other parts of your body. ASPS says a surgeon may remove up to 3 times as much fat as is actually injected.

No source we read gives a minimum amount, so only an exam can tell you. Your surgeon looks at what is left and where it could come from, and tells you what a realistic result would be.

## Is recovery from a double BBL easier?

No. Plan on the same recovery as a first BBL. It is liposuction and fat grafting again, and no source we rely on says a second round heals faster. If you are told a second round is easier, ask why.

| Walking | Up and walking after the second day | The Aesthetic Society |
| Sitting | Not on your buttocks for at least 2 weeks | Cleveland Clinic |

## What are the risks of a double BBL?

They are the risks of a BBL, because it is the same operation. We found no study comparing a second BBL with a first.

The most serious risk is a fat embolism: fat injected into or below the gluteal muscle can enter a blood vessel, which can be fatal. [Florida's rule for office surgery](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/458.328) says fat may only go into the layer under the skin and may not cross the fascia over the muscle, and that applies to a second procedure too. No rule has removed the risk: South Florida recorded 25 deaths from BBL fat embolism between 2010 and 2022, 14 of them after the 2018 guidelines and 12 after Florida's 2019 emergency rule ([Pazmiño and Garcia, Aesthetic Surgery Journal, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1093/asj/sjac224)). In the 11 cases they examined at autopsy, the authors describe fat in the muscle. Those are historical figures from one region, not a measure of today's risk.

The 2026 meta-analysis, of 38 studies and 22,151 patients, found minor complications in 3.58% of BBL patients, including uneven contours in 2.29% and seroma, a pocket of fluid, in 2.03%. Pulmonary embolism, a blood clot in the lungs, was reported separately, at 0.04%.

If you have breathing trouble, chest pain or an unusual heartbeat after surgery, seek medical attention immediately.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many BBLs can you get?

No source we rely on sets a number. Cleveland Clinic says you may need another BBL during your life, but each one needs enough fat to move and a healed result to build on. Each is also a full surgery with its own risks, so your surgeon decides after an exam whether another one makes sense.

### Can both BBLs be done at once?

Not in the sense of doubling the fat in one surgery. A 2018 safety advisory from a multi-society task force says that if the goal requires more fat than fits in the layer under the skin, the surgeon should consider staging the procedure rather than injecting deep. That is why more volume can mean a second surgery.

### Is a second BBL riskier?

We found no study comparing a second BBL with a first, so we can't give you a figure. It is the same operation again: liposuction, anesthesia and fat grafting, with the same most serious risk, a fat embolism. Ask your surgeon how your first surgery and your health change your risk.

### Is a second BBL cheaper?

Don't assume it is. What a second BBL costs depends on your exam, including how much fat is moved and from where. The [pricing section of the BBL page](/procedures/brazilian-butt-lift-bbl-miami#pricing) explains how prices are set, and your surgeon confirms your price at consultation. Financing is offered.

### What if I had my first BBL somewhere else?

Start with an exam of what was done. Bring any records you have from your first surgery, including the date and how much fat was moved. Your surgeon checks how it healed and how much fat you have left, then tells you whether a second BBL makes sense. Some problems need a different plan.
