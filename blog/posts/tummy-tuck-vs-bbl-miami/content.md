## Which is better, a BBL or a tummy tuck?

Neither is better than the other, because they treat different things. A tummy tuck works on the front of the abdomen: [ASPS says](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck) it removes excess fat and skin and, in most cases, restores weakened or separated muscles. A BBL works on shape, with fat taken by liposuction and moved to the buttocks. A BBL won't tighten your stomach, and a tummy tuck won't add curve. Start with what bothers you most. Your surgeon decides after examining you.

## What does each surgery fix?

A tummy tuck is about skin and muscle. A BBL is about volume and shape. This table shows where each one helps and what you need first.

| What bothers you | Tummy tuck | BBL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loose skin on the stomach | Yes | No |
| Separated abdominal muscles | Yes, in most cases | No |
| More volume or shape in the buttocks | No | Yes |
| Stretch marks | Only those on the skin it removes | No |
| What you need first | Good health and a stable weight. ASPS advises postponing it if you may want more children | Enough excess fat elsewhere on your body |
| Scar | Usually hip bone to hip bone, and possibly around the belly button | Small incisions for liposuction, and three to five around the buttocks |

The tummy tuck rows come from [ASPS's candidate page](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck/candidates), [ASPS's tummy tuck page](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck) on future pregnancies and [Cleveland Clinic on the scar](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar). The BBL rows come from [Cleveland Clinic on the BBL](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23308-brazilian-butt-lift).

## How does BBL recovery compare with tummy tuck recovery?

Time off work is about the same. The big difference is sitting. After a tummy tuck you stay bent at the waist at first. After a BBL you keep pressure off your buttocks for weeks.

| | Tummy tuck | BBL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surgery length | 1 to 5 hours, Cleveland Clinic says | 3 to 5 hours under general anesthesia at Alluring |
| Walking | The same day, bent at the waist for 7 to 10 days | Up and walking after the second day |
| Sitting | No pressure rule for the buttocks | Not on your buttocks for at least 2 weeks, a pillow for about 8 weeks |
| Sleeping | Bent at the waist while lying down, for the same 7 to 10 days | On your stomach or side, off your back until your surgeon clears it |
| Back to work | About 2 weeks | 10 to 14 days. An ASPS article says days 7 to 10 |
| Garment | 4 to 6 weeks | All day and night, except in the shower, for the first month, then at least 12 hours a day in month 2, or as your surgeon directs |
| Exercise | No running or lifting for 6 weeks | Normal exercise after 8 weeks, once your surgeon clears you |
| Seeing your result | Up to 3 months, and the scar keeps improving for up to 1 year | Final shape at 3 to 6 months, once swelling settles, though results can keep settling |

The tummy tuck figures are from [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/procedures/11017-tummy-tuck) and from [Cindy Wu, MD, on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/answers-to-common-tummy-tuck-questions). The BBL walking, work and exercise figures are from [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/butt-lift/aftercare-recovery) and Cleveland Clinic. In [an ASPS article on BBL recovery](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/six-things-you-need-to-know-about-recovering-from-a-brazilian-butt-lift), the garment schedule is one surgeon's and the days 7 to 10 line is the article's own. [ASPS's procedure page](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/buttock-enhancement/procedure) says you won't see final BBL results until a year after surgery. Your own surgeon's instructions come first. The [BBL recovery guide](/how-long-to-recover-from-bbl) goes through the BBL timeline in more detail.

## Can you do a BBL and a tummy tuck in one surgery?

Sometimes. Your surgeon tells you whether to do both in one surgery or plan two. Three things shape that answer:

- **How much fat the BBL needs.** A BBL takes its fat from liposuction, so your surgeon looks at how much you have and how much the transfer would use.
- **Where the surgery is done.** [Florida's rule for office surgery](https://www.flrules.org/gateway/readFile.asp?sid=0&tid=28691065&type=1&file=64B8-9.009.doc) says liposuction combined with a tummy tuck in a doctor's office may not exceed 1,000 cc of supernatant fat, meaning the fat alone once the fluid has separated. It also caps all planned procedures in one office surgery at 8 hours. Both limits apply to a doctor's office, not to a hospital or licensed surgery center, so ask where your surgery would be done.
- **Your health and the size of the plan.** In a [large insurance database study of tummy tucks](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26505716/), risk was higher with several procedures at once, at age 55 or older and with a BMI of 30 or more. Those are risk factors, not cutoffs.

If you need two surgeries, we found no source that says which should come first or how far apart they should be. Your surgeon sets the order.

If you do both at once, the recovery instructions pull in two directions. You stay bent at the waist for the tummy tuck and off your buttocks and back for the BBL. Ask exactly how to lie, sit and sleep, and set up your bed before surgery. If you have small children, line up help now: [Shahram Salemy, MD, on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/what-you-need-to-know-about-your-tummy-tuck-recovery) warns against squatting or picking up children for the first few weeks after a tummy tuck.

## BBL vs lipo: what is the difference?

Every BBL starts with liposuction. Liposuction alone removes the fat and stops there. A BBL moves it to your buttocks, which is what brings the sitting rule with it.

After [liposuction](/procedures/liposuction-miami) alone, [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/liposuction/aftercare-recovery) says you will likely return to work within a few days, while [ASPS's timeline](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/liposuction/recovery) says weeks 2 to 3. The Aesthetic Society adds a compression garment for 4 to 6 weeks and no strenuous exercise for 4 to 6 weeks. It also [says liposuction](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/liposuction) removes fat but does not tighten skin.

If you like your buttock shape and want less fat in one area, liposuction alone may be enough. If you want a narrower waist and more curve, the [BBL options section](/procedures/brazilian-butt-lift-bbl-miami#options) explains how a BBL with lipo 360 differs from a traditional or skinny BBL.

## BBL vs mommy makeover: what is the difference?

A BBL is one procedure. A [mommy makeover](/procedures/mommy-makeover-miami) is a plan built from several. [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/mommy-makeover) describes its goal as restoring the shape and appearance of a woman's body after childbearing, and lists tummy tuck, liposuction, breast surgery, labiaplasty and buttock augmentation as possible parts.

If pregnancy changed only your buttocks, a BBL on its own may be the plan. If it changed your stomach and breasts, a mommy makeover without a BBL may be. Your surgeon decides at consultation.

## Mommy makeover with a BBL: what changes?

Adding a BBL makes the plan larger. It doesn't change the rules above:

- **The sitting rule comes with it.** You follow it on top of every other instruction, so plan for help at home. The [mommy makeover recovery timeline](/blog/mommy-makeover-recovery-signs) covers the other parts.
- **The liposuction limit can apply.** If a tummy tuck is in the plan and the surgery is in a doctor's office, Florida's 1,000 cc rule and 8-hour cap apply, so your surgeon tells you whether everything fits in one surgery or needs two.
- **More procedures can mean more risk.** Combining several was a risk factor in the tummy tuck study above.

## BBL vs butt implants: which one suits you?

It mostly comes down to how much fat you have. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23353-butt-implants) says implants may be a good option for people with a smaller, leaner frame who don't have enough fat for a BBL. It also says a BBL tends to look and feel more natural than implants and may have a lower risk of complications.

| | BBL | Butt implants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material | Your own fat | Silicone implants |
| Where it goes | The layer under the skin | Inside or below the muscle |
| Needs extra fat | Yes | No |
| What lasts | ASPS says patients retain 60% to 80% of the transferred fat on average, in some cases more and in others less | Permanent, unless a complication means removing them |
| Back to work | 10 to 14 days | 2 to 3 weeks, using a pillow |
| Exercise | After 8 weeks | After 8 to 10 weeks |
| Risks named by the sources | Fat embolism, uneven contours, seroma | Shifting, infection, seroma, incisions that separate, hematoma, contracture |

Implant figures are from Cleveland Clinic and [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/butt-lift/results). A [2016 systematic review of 44 articles](https://doi.org/10.1097/PRS.0000000000002005) found complications in 9.9% of fat grafting patients and 21.6% of implant patients. That doesn't settle which is safer. It counts how often complications happen, not how serious they are, and a BBL's most serious risk is rare but can be fatal. Ask your surgeon whether implants are an option for you.

## How safe is a BBL compared with a tummy tuck?

Both are surgery, and their risks differ. We found no study comparing the two directly, so read these numbers separately.

For a tummy tuck, major complications followed 3.1% of tummy tucks done alone and 3.8% of those combined with liposuction, in an insurance database of 25,478 patients (Winocour and colleagues, Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery, 2015). Of those complications, 31.5% were hematomas, bleeding under the skin, 27.2% infections and 20.2% suspected or confirmed blood clots. A [2020 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31858207/) found blood clots occur more often after a tummy tuck than after any other cosmetic surgery, and more often still when liposuction is added. A [2019 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30511162/) of 14,061 patients found a seroma, fluid under the skin, in 4.1%.

For a BBL, the most serious risk is a fat embolism: fat injected into or below the gluteal muscle can enter a blood vessel, which can be fatal. Safety guidance says to keep fat under the skin, and [Florida's rule for office surgery](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/458.328) says fat may only go into that layer and may not cross the fascia over the muscle. No rule has removed the risk. South Florida recorded 25 deaths from BBL fat embolism between 2010 and 2022, 14 of them after the 2018 guidelines and 12 after Florida's 2019 emergency rule ([Pazmiño and Garcia, Aesthetic Surgery Journal, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1093/asj/sjac224)). In the 11 cases they examined at autopsy, the authors describe fat in the muscle. Those are historical figures from one region, not a measure of today's risk.

A [2026 meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1097/PRS.0000000000012437) of 38 studies and 22,151 patients found minor complications in 3.58% of BBL patients, including uneven contours in 2.29% and seroma in 2.03%, and pulmonary embolism in 0.04%. Major complications were less common with ultrasound guidance, 0.02% versus 0.08%, which is an association, not proof of cause. The [BBL page's safety section](/procedures/brazilian-butt-lift-bbl-miami#safety) lists questions to ask any surgeon.

After either surgery, if you have breathing trouble, chest pain or an unusual heartbeat, seek medical attention immediately.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a BBL or a tummy tuck more painful?

No source we rely on compares them directly. After a BBL, a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS says most people need some pain medication for the first 4 to 5 days, and Cleveland Clinic says pain eases after 1 to 2 weeks. A tummy tuck adds muscle repair. Our [comparison of BBL and tummy tuck pain](/what-is-more-painful-bbl-or-tummy-tuck) goes through what to expect.

### Can you combine a BBL and a tummy tuck in one surgery?

Sometimes. Your surgeon tells you whether to do both in one surgery or plan two, based on your exam, how much fat the BBL would need and where the surgery would be done. Combining adds some risk, so it isn't the right plan for everyone. Ask what the plan would be if one surgery isn't suitable.

### Does a mommy makeover include a BBL?

Not automatically. A mommy makeover is a plan built from several procedures, and ASPS lists buttock augmentation as one possible part, alongside tummy tuck, liposuction and breast surgery. If you want a BBL in your plan, say so at your consultation. Your surgeon tells you whether it fits in the same surgery.

### Can you get a BBL if you have loose skin?

You can, but a BBL won't remove it, because it moves fat and doesn't cut away skin. Loose skin on your stomach is what a tummy tuck treats. Loose skin on the buttocks is a different question: ASPS describes a separate buttock lift for significant looseness there. Your surgeon tells you at your exam which one applies.

### Which lasts longer, a BBL or a tummy tuck?

Cleveland Clinic calls a tummy tuck permanent and says it can last a lifetime. It calls BBL results semi-permanent, lasting several years and possibly over a decade, while The Aesthetic Society says fat that survives stays for life and ages with you. With either one, weight changes and pregnancy can change your result.
