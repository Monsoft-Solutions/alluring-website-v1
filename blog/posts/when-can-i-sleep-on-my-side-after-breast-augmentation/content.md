## When can I sleep on my side after breast augmentation?

A [UW Medicine patient handout](https://healthonline.washington.edu/document/health_online/pdf/Breast-Augmentation.pdf) says to sleep on your back for the first 3 to 4 weeks and allows your side after that. [University of Utah Health](https://healthcare.utah.edu/plastic-surgery/breast/recovery) says to wait three to four weeks and to alternate sides at first, to help with swelling. Your surgeon may ask you to wait longer, and that instruction comes first.

Until then you sleep on your back, and the rest of this guide shows how to make that bearable. It sits alongside the [breast augmentation recovery timeline](/procedures/breast-augmentation-miami#recovery). After a breast reduction? See [when you can sleep on your side after breast reduction](/when-can-i-sleep-on-my-side-after-breast-reduction).

## How to sleep after breast augmentation in the first weeks

On your back, with your upper body raised. Here is how to set that up:

1. **Raise your upper body.** [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/breast/breast-augmentation/aftercare-recovery) says to sleep on your back at a 25- to 45-degree angle for the first days after surgery, or if you have significant swelling.
2. **Use a wedge, a recliner or a stack of pillows.** The Aesthetic Society suggests wedge pillows or a recliner chair. An [article on the ASPS website by Dr. Paul Vitenas](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/four-tips-for-a-healthy-recovery-after-a-breast-augmentation) says to have plenty of pillows, so you can prop yourself up at a comfortable angle.
3. **Keep your bra on as instructed.** See [when you can sleep without a bra](/when-can-i-sleep-without-a-bra-after-breast-augmentation) for how long.
4. **Put pillows beside your arms and under your knees if it helps.** Some women do this for comfort and to avoid rolling over. No study has tested it.
5. **Walk during the day.** The Aesthetic Society says to walk a few minutes every few hours to reduce the risk of blood clots in your legs.

## Can I sleep on my side 2 weeks after breast augmentation?

Not yet. At 2 weeks you are still inside the 3 to 4 weeks on your back that the UW Medicine handout calls for, and University of Utah Health says to wait three to four weeks before sleeping on your side. Here is the same answer for each week people ask about:

| Time since surgery | Can I sleep on my side? | What the sources say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | No | Rest and sleep on your back, with extra pillows to prop you up (University of Utah Health) |
| 2 weeks | Not yet | Still inside the first 3 to 4 weeks on your back (UW Medicine) |
| 3 weeks | Ask your surgeon | The earliest point in the handouts is 3 to 4 weeks |
| 4 weeks | Usually, with your surgeon's OK | Both handouts allow it by now. Alternate sides at first (University of Utah Health) |
| 6 weeks | Usually, unless your surgeon has said otherwise | Past the 3 to 4 weeks in the handouts |

When you do start, alternate sides, as University of Utah Health advises, so that one side doesn't take all the pressure. Some women tuck a small pillow under the breast for support. That is for comfort, and no study has tested it. If your side hurts, go back to your back for a while and try again later.

## How long do you have to sleep elevated after breast augmentation?

For the first days to about a week, and longer if you are still swollen. The Aesthetic Society says to stay inclined for the first days after surgery, or if you have significant swelling, and University of Utah Health says extra pillows for the first week. You don't have to sit bolt upright, because a slope is all they describe.

No source we read gives a date for lying flat on your back. A sensible approach is to lower the pillows gradually as swelling goes down and lying flatter feels comfortable, while staying on your back for the 3 to 4 weeks. If flat feels bad, stay propped up a little longer.

## When can I sleep on my stomach after breast augmentation?

When your surgeon says so. University of Utah Health says not to sleep on your stomach until your surgeon says it is okay. The UW Medicine handout groups it with side sleeping, saying that after the first 3 to 4 weeks you may sleep on your side or stomach. The two handouts differ, so ask.

## How to get out of bed after breast augmentation

Use your legs and your core, and don't push up with your arms. None of the sources we read spells out a technique, so treat this as practical advice, and ask your nurse to show you before you go home. [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000273.htm), on a page that also covers lifts and reductions, says your nurse will help you begin to move around and warns against overstretching your arms, and The Aesthetic Society says to avoid lifting, pulling or pushing anything that causes pain.

1. **Bend your knees** and slide to the edge of the bed.
2. **Swing your legs over the side** and let their weight help bring your body up, keeping your elbows close to your sides.
3. **Sit for a moment** before you stand, in case you feel lightheaded.
4. **Ask for a hand in the first days.** University of Utah Health says your shoulders may have a limited range of motion at first. A recliner can make getting up easier too.

## How to get ready before surgery

A little setup makes those first nights easier:

- **Pillows:** a wedge and several ordinary pillows, or a recliner you can use.
- **Clothes:** University of Utah Health suggests shirts that are easy to get on and off, such as loose button-downs or zip-up hoodies.
- **Within reach of the bed:** water, your medicine, your phone and a charger.
- **Someone to stay:** [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/11024-breast-augmentation) says to have someone stay with you the first night at least.
- **A test night or two:** if you always sleep on your side, try a few nights on your back beforehand to see which pillows work. No study shows this helps, but it costs nothing.

## After a breast lift with implants

The same back-first approach applies, but ask for your own date. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23298-breast-lift), writing about a breast lift, says some surgeons prefer you to sleep on your back for a few weeks and to check with your surgeon. It gives no side-sleeping date. If you are having a [breast lift](/procedures/breast-lift-miami) too, ask your surgeon before surgery.

## When should I call my surgeon?

Some soreness when you lie down or move is expected. These are not:

| Usually normal | Call your surgeon |
| --- | --- |
| Muscle soreness or sharp, shooting pain for 2 weeks (UW Medicine) | Severe pain your medicine doesn't help (UW Medicine) |
| Tingling or other strange sensations for the first few months (University of Utah Health) | Sudden swelling of a breast (MedlinePlus) |
| Some difference between sides at first (University of Utah Health) | Growing enlargement or firmness with pain and pressure (Cleveland Clinic). Fever, chills, redness or bad-smelling drainage (UW Medicine) |

Sleeping is only one part of recovery. See [what to expect at 5 weeks post-op](/weeks-post-op-breast-augmentation-what-to-expect) for the rest of what is typical around this stage. If you are still planning surgery, the [breast augmentation FAQ](/procedures/breast-augmentation-miami#faq) and a [free consultation](/free-consultation) are good next steps.

## Frequently asked questions

### What happens if I sleep on my side too soon?

We found no study that measures this. The usual reasons to wait are soreness and swelling on that side, and University of Utah Health suggests alternating sides at first to help with swelling. If you wake up on your side, roll back and carry on. Call your surgeon for sudden swelling, severe pain or a change in shape.

### Why do my breast implants hurt when I sleep on my side?

Early on, it is often soreness. A UW Medicine handout calls muscle soreness or sharp, shooting pain normal for 2 weeks, and University of Utah Health describes strange sensations for the first few months. Pain that is new, getting worse, or comes with firmness or a change in shape is different, because the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/breast-implants/risks-and-complications-breast-implants) says [capsular contracture](/blog/capsular-contracture) can be painful. See your surgeon.

### Do I need to wear a bra while I sleep?

Yes, if your surgeon gave you one, for as long as your surgeon says. A UW Medicine handout says to keep a surgical bra on 24 hours a day for 3 to 4 weeks, taking it off only to shower. Going without a bra at night has no sourced date, so your surgeon decides. Our guide to [sleeping without a bra](/when-can-i-sleep-without-a-bra-after-breast-augmentation) has the stages.

### Does it matter if my implants are over or under the muscle?

None of the sources we read gives different sleeping dates by placement. The UW Medicine handout describes implants placed under the muscle, so its 3 to 4 weeks comes from that setting. University of Utah Health doesn't split its advice by placement. Ask your surgeon whether your plan changes anything, and see [over or under the muscle, compared](/blog/breast-implants-over-or-under-the-muscle) if you are still choosing.

### Can I lie face down for a back massage?

Ask your surgeon first. No source we read addresses back massage. Until you are cleared, avoid anything that presses on your chest or has you lying face down, including massage tables and massage chairs. Massaging the implants themselves is a separate question, covered in our guide to [massage after breast augmentation](/how-often-to-massage-breast-after-augmentation).

### How do I sleep after breast augmentation and another surgery, such as a BBL?

Follow the plan your surgeon gives you for the combination, because the rules can pull against each other. Breast implants call for sleeping on your back, and another surgery may call for staying off it. Ask before the operation, so you can have the right pillows ready. The dates in this guide are for breast augmentation alone.
