## What is a tummy tuck without muscle repair called?

It is often called a mini tummy tuck or a skin-only tummy tuck, though neither name is exact. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/procedures/11017-tummy-tuck) also calls a mini a partial abdominoplasty. It treats loose skin and a small amount of fat below the belly button (the navel) through a shorter incision, generally with no cut around the belly button.

Check what yours means. [Samir Rao, MD, on the website of the American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS)](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/understanding-the-different-types-of-tummy-tucks) describes a mini as "removing lax skin and strengthening muscles in the lower stomach", so some surgeons include muscle work under the same name. Ask yours one plain question: will you repair my muscles, and which part?

To see where you might fit, our tummy tuck page explains [how to tell which type of tummy tuck you need](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#which-tummy-tuck) and lists [the price of each type](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#pricing).

## Mini vs full tummy tuck: what is different?

A mini works only below the belly button. A full tummy tuck works above and below it through a longer incision, and [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck) says a tummy tuck "removes excess fat and skin and, in most cases, restores weakened or separated muscles". That muscle work is the main difference, and it is why the two are not interchangeable.

| | Mini tummy tuck | Full tummy tuck |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Area treated | Below the belly button (Rao) | Above and below the belly button (Rao) |
| Scar | About 3 to 6 inches, the length of a C-section scar (Cleveland Clinic) | Hip bone to hip bone, just above the pubic area (Cleveland Clinic) |
| Belly button | Generally no cut around it (Cleveland Clinic) | You may also have a scar around it (Cleveland Clinic) |
| Muscle repair | None when skin-only; some surgeons tighten the lower muscles (Rao) | In most cases (ASPS) |
| Back to work | 1 to 2 weeks (Rao) | Non-strenuous work after a couple of weeks (Rao) |
| More vigorous activities | About 5 to 6 weeks (Rao) | Rao gives no figure |
| Fully recovered | Rao gives no figure | Around 3 months (Rao) |

The scar figures come from [Cleveland Clinic's tummy tuck scar page](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar). The recovery figures come from one surgeon's post, the only source we found that splits recovery by type, so treat them as a guide.

Back to work: 1 to 2 weeks for a mini, a couple of weeks of non-strenuous work for a full. More vigorous activities: about 5 to 6 weeks for a mini. Fully recovered: around 3 months for a full.](IMAGE:sidebyside)

## What about skin removal without muscle repair?

That can mean a mini tummy tuck, or a different surgery called a panniculectomy. Cleveland Clinic lists it among procedures commonly confused with a tummy tuck: it "removes hanging skin and fat from your lower abdominal region" and "doesn't tighten muscles, move your belly button or address fullness in your upper abdomen." If a surgeon offers you skin removal, ask which of the two it is, because they aim at different things.

## Do you need muscle repair?

You may if your abdominal muscles have separated, a condition called diastasis recti. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22346-diastasis-recti) says it affects 6 in 10 women after childbirth and considers a gap wider than 2 centimeters to be diastasis recti. Skin-only surgery doesn't close that gap.

A surgeon can feel for the gap at your exam. We cover [how to check for a gap, when physical therapy comes first and when a tummy tuck repairs it](/blog/tummy-tuck-diastasis-recti-miami) in its own guide.

## Can a tummy tuck without muscle repair flatten your stomach?

It can flatten the area below the belly button when loose skin and fat are the problem. It can't pull in a stretched muscle wall, so a stomach that still rounds out when you stand may stay rounded. Rao puts the trade-off plainly: "its results are limited to the lower abdomen."

It isn't a way to lose weight either. ASPS says a tummy tuck "is not a substitute for weight loss or an appropriate exercise program." If your concern is mostly fat rather than loose skin, compare [a tummy tuck with liposuction](/blog/tummy-tuck-vs-liposuction).

## Who is a good candidate for a mini tummy tuck?

A mini tends to suit someone whose loose skin and extra fat sit mostly below the belly button. Cleveland Clinic describes it as for people with "a small amount of excess fat and tissue beneath their belly button." The rest of this list applies to any tummy tuck, and most of it comes from [what ASPS says about tummy tuck candidates](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck/candidates):

- **Healthy, at a stable weight.** ASPS lists being physically healthy and at a stable weight.
- **Close to your goal weight.** [Paul Vitenas, MD, on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/five-things-to-consider-before-getting-a-tummy-tuck) says to be close to your desired weight for 6 to 12 months first.
- **A nonsmoker.** ASPS lists this too.
- **Not planning a future pregnancy.** ASPS advises women who may be considering future pregnancies to postpone a tummy tuck.
- **Realistic about the result.** A mini changes the lower belly, not the whole stomach.
- **Little or no muscle separation.** A mini that leaves the muscles alone won't fix a gap between them.

## What is recovery like without muscle repair?

Rao says most people return to work 1 to 2 weeks after a mini and resume more vigorous activities in about 5 to 6 weeks, and that a mini "requires less recovery time than a full tummy tuck." We found no study that measures how much easier it is. For the full timeline, see [tummy tuck recovery, week by week](/how-long-to-recover-tummy-tuck). If you are weighing the repair itself, it helps to read about [tightness after a tummy tuck with muscle repair](/how-to-reduce-tightness-after-tummy-tuck).

A mini is still surgery. In an insurance database of 25,478 patients, major complications followed 3.1% of tummy tucks done alone ([Winocour and colleagues, Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery, 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26505716/)). The abstract doesn't separate minis from full tummy tucks, so read that as a figure for tummy tucks in general. Cleveland Clinic also lists scarring, bleeding, pain and swelling, infection, seroma (a buildup of fluid), poor wound healing, blood clots, numbness and uneven healing.

## What do pictures of a tummy tuck without muscle repair show?

Mostly a change below the belly button: less loose skin and fat there, and a short, low scar. Because a mini's results are limited to the lower abdomen, the skin above the belly button should look much as it did. Here is what to look for when you compare photos:

- **Where the change stops.** If the upper belly looks very different too, the photo may show a different type of surgery.
- **The scar.** A mini's is about 3 to 6 inches. A full tummy tuck's runs from hip bone to hip bone, but so can an extended mini's, which has no muscle repair, so a long scar alone doesn't tell you the muscles were repaired.
- **The belly button.** A mini generally has no cut around it. A scar around it usually points to a larger tummy tuck.
- **How long after surgery.** Cleveland Clinic says it can take up to 3 months to see the final result, and the scar keeps improving for up to 1 year. An earlier photo may still show swelling.
- **Which type it is.** Names vary between practices, so ask what was actually done, including whether the muscles were repaired.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a mini tummy tuck include muscle repair?

It depends on the surgeon. Samir Rao, MD, on the ASPS website describes a mini as strengthening muscles in the lower stomach. At Alluring, the mini and the extended mini are listed without muscle repair. If your exam shows separated muscles, your surgeon will explain the options that include it.

### Does a mini tummy tuck move the belly button?

Usually not. Cleveland Clinic says a mini generally involves no cut around the belly button. Some surgeons do detach and reinsert it in a mini, called an umbilical float, which leaves it lower (Borille and colleagues, 2024). In a full tummy tuck, the surgeon makes an opening and moves the belly button into position. If your belly button bothers you, say so at your consultation.

### Is a tummy tuck without muscle repair worth it?

It can be when the problem is loose skin and fat below the belly button and your muscles are intact. If your stomach still bulges when you stand up straight, or your muscles have separated, skin-only surgery leaves that bulge in place and a full tummy tuck may fit better. Your surgeon decides with you at consultation.

### How do I know if I need muscle repair?

A surgeon confirms it at an exam, but you can check first. Cleveland Clinic suggests lying on your back with your knees bent and feeling how many fingers fit in the gap between your abs. If you feel a gap of two or more finger widths, raise it with your healthcare provider, who can confirm it and recommend treatment.

### Does insurance cover a tummy tuck without muscle repair?

Usually not. ASPS says most health insurance plans do not cover tummy tuck surgery or its complications, and a mini is a type of tummy tuck. If a doctor has mentioned a hernia or hanging skin, ask your insurer what its rules are before you decide, and get the answer in writing.

### How long do the results of a mini tummy tuck last?

ASPS says tummy tuck results are technically permanent, but significant swings in your weight can greatly reduce them. Cleveland Clinic adds that your muscles and skin can stretch after pregnancy, which can affect the outcome. None of the sources we read gives separate figures for a mini, so plan around a stable weight and a finished family.
