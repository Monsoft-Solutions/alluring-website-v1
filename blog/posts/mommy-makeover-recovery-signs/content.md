## How long is mommy makeover recovery?

Mommy makeover recovery takes at least 2 weeks and can take longer, depending on your combination of procedures, according to [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/mommy-makeover). Frank Campanile, MD, writing on the [American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS) website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/what-to-expect-during-your-mommy-makeover-recovery), says normal activities usually return around 3 months and the final result can take 6 months to a year.

If you are still deciding, [what a mommy makeover in Miami includes and how it is planned](/procedures/mommy-makeover-miami) is the place to start, because the parts you choose change this schedule. Here is the mommy makeover recovery timeline, week by week, with the source of each figure.

| When | What to expect | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Day of surgery | You may be up and walking within hours. You cannot go home alone. | [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/mommy-makeover/aftercare-recovery) |
| Within 72 hours | You may shower once the dressings are off. | The Aesthetic Society |
| Within 5 days | Your first follow-up with your surgeon. | The Aesthetic Society |
| Week 1 | The hardest week. After a tummy tuck, you stay bent at the waist for 7 to 10 days. | The Aesthetic Society; [Cindy Wu, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/answers-to-common-tummy-tuck-questions) |
| Week 2 | About 2 weeks off work, and someone else in charge of small children for at least 2 weeks. | The Aesthetic Society |
| Weeks 2 to 3 | You may be comfortable enough to drive now, or when your sutures and drains are out and you are off pain medication. | The Aesthetic Society |

## Mommy makeover recovery signs: what is normal, and when should you call your surgeon?

Swelling, bruising, numbness and discomfort are expected after a mommy makeover. The Aesthetic Society says to call your surgeon immediately if swelling, pain, redness, drainage or bleeding increases, or if you develop a fever, dizziness, nausea or vomiting. Chest pain or difficulty breathing is an emergency: go to the emergency room or call 911.

| Usually normal | Call your surgeon |
|---|---|
| Swelling and numbness, which may take months to resolve | More swelling, pain, redness, drainage or bleeding |
| Bruising and tenderness early in recovery | Drainage that turns thick, tan, green or yellow, or smells bad |
| Pain in the first days, managed by pain medication | Pain that is extreme or lasts long |
| Being bent at the waist for 7 to 10 days after a tummy tuck | An incision that looks larger, deeper, dried out or dark |
| Not feeling like yourself for at least 7 to 10 days | A temperature above 100°F (37.7°C) for more than 4 hours |
| Still feeling like you are recovering for 2 to 3 weeks | Dizziness, nausea or vomiting |
| Bandages, compression garments and possibly surgical drains | Redness, warmth, swelling, pain or tenderness in a leg or arm |

The normal column draws on The Aesthetic Society, Frank Campanile, MD, and Cindy Wu, MD. The call column draws on The Aesthetic Society and on MedlinePlus, for [surgical wound care](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000738.htm) and [deep vein clots](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000156.htm).

<CalloutBox type="warning">
Go to the emergency room or call 911 for chest pain, difficulty breathing, coughing up blood, fainting or loss of consciousness. MedlinePlus lists these as emergencies with a deep vein clot, and The Aesthetic Society names shortness of breath, chest pains and an unusual heartbeat as red flags.
</CalloutBox>

## Can you manage a mommy makeover recovery with kids at home?

Yes, if another adult takes over first. The Aesthetic Society says to put someone else totally in charge of small children for at least 2 weeks and to avoid heavy lifting, such as picking up your children, for 4 to 6 weeks. Line up that help before you choose a surgery date.

| At home | When it usually comes back to you | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Looking after small children on your own | Not for at least 2 weeks | The Aesthetic Society |
| Driving them to school | About 2 to 3 weeks, when your surgeon clears you | The Aesthetic Society |
| Picking them up, or carrying laundry | Not for 4 to 6 weeks | The Aesthetic Society |
| Buckling car seats, pushing strollers and bath time | Ask your surgeon: no source we read gives a week or a weight | None found |

After a tummy tuck, Shahram Salemy, MD, on the [ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/what-you-need-to-know-about-your-tummy-tuck-recovery), says squatting or picking up children is strongly advised against for the first few weeks.

A plan that holds names one person for each job: nights in the first week, school runs, baths, meals and laundry. Tell older children ahead of time that you can't lift them for a while, but that they can sit beside you. If you live in another state, you need someone with you in Miami and someone with the kids at home. Start with a [consultation for out-of-state patients](/fly-in-consultation). If your baby is young or you are still nursing, read [how long surgeons suggest waiting after delivery and breastfeeding](/procedures/mommy-makeover-miami#candidate) first.

## Mommy makeover checklist: what should you prepare before surgery?

Prepare your help, your time off and your home. The Aesthetic Society tells mommy makeover patients to line up another adult, someone in charge of small children for at least 2 weeks, meals you can reheat, supplies at counter level and front-opening clothes. Cleveland Clinic says to quit smoking at least 6 weeks before surgery.

![Flat lay of recovery items on pale linen: a button-front shirt, a zip-up hoodie, slip-on shoes, a folded compression garment, a medication organizer, a water bottle, tissues, a phone with its charger and a paperback.](IMAGE:recovery-flat-lay)

- **Someone to help you at home for at least the first 24 hours.** [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/mommy-makeover/aftercare-recovery) also says [you cannot stay home without another adult present](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/mommy-makeover/preparing-your-procedure).
- **Someone in charge of small children for at least 2 weeks.** And a support system for the full recovery, not only the first days.
- **A ride home after surgery.** If you go home the same day, The Aesthetic Society says to have someone stay with you for several days.
- **About 2 weeks off work.** Longer if your job is physical.
- **No smoking for at least 6 weeks before surgery.** That is [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/mommy-makeovers)'s figure, and your surgeon sets yours. The Aesthetic Society says smoking slows healing and raises the risk of serious complications.
- **Your medications reviewed with your surgeon.** Aspirin, anti-inflammatory medications and some vitamins can increase bleeding, The Aesthetic Society says. Fill your prescriptions before surgery day.
- **Meals you can reheat.** High-protein, low-sodium food, fruit, vegetables, caffeine-free drinks and water, The Aesthetic Society says. Common practice adds high-fiber food, because pain medication can constipate. Frank Campanile, MD, says this is no time to diet.
- **Supplies at counter level.** Phone, charger, TV remote, medication organizer, water and tissues by your bed.
- **Pillows for support.** Ask your surgeon how to sleep. Yours may suggest several pillows under your back with your legs slightly bent, The Aesthetic Society says, and some women use a recliner or wedge pillow.
- **Front-opening clothes and slip-on shoes.** Button-down tops and zippered hoodies, so you don't lift your arms overhead or bend.
- **A spare compression garment.** For when you sweat or wash the first. Ask whether your surgeon supplies it.
- **Chores done before surgery.** You should not be lifting, driving, doing laundry or cleaning while you recover. Download shows and books too.
- **Your questions for the surgeon.** [ASPS suggests asking](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/mommy-makeover/recovery) about dressings, drains, showering and follow-up care.

## What should you expect 1 week after a mommy makeover?

The first week is the hardest. The Aesthetic Society says it will be the worst, with discomfort, swelling and numbness, and that recovery can be intense the first few days. Expect a follow-up visit within 5 days and to be walking around the house by the end of the week, though you still can't drive.

- **Walking.** After a tummy tuck, walking on the day of surgery keeps the risk of blood clots in the legs low, Cindy Wu, MD, writes on the ASPS website.
- **Garments.** ASPS says an elastic bandage or support bra will minimize swelling and support the breasts, and a compression garment may also be used to control swelling in the abdomen, waist and buttocks. The Aesthetic Society says you will need to wear an abdominal binder after a tummy tuck. The [compression garment guide](/blog/mommy-makeover-compression-garments-guide) covers how long.
- **Drains.** When drains are placed, they come out once the fluid slows, usually in 1 to 2 weeks (Cindy Wu, MD) or 7 to 10 days, sometimes 2 weeks or longer ([Paul Vitenas, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/five-things-to-consider-before-getting-a-tummy-tuck)), both on the ASPS website. Ask your surgeon whether you will have them.
- **Mood.** Feeling tired or low this week doesn't mean something is wrong. Tell your surgeon or someone close to you if it doesn't lift.

## When can you go back to work and drive after a mommy makeover?

The Aesthetic Society says to plan about 2 weeks off work, depending on the physical demands of your job, and that you may be comfortable enough to drive in 2 to 3 weeks, or when your sutures and drains are out and you are no longer taking pain medication.

Frank Campanile, MD, says most women plan about 2 weeks away from work. The Aesthetic Society adds that you may still feel like you are recovering for 2 to 3 weeks, and that traditional sutures usually come out within 1 to 2 weeks. A breast lift has its own line: [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/breast/breast-lift/aftercare-recovery) says no lifting, driving, laundry or cleaning for the first 2 weeks. Your surgeon clears you to drive, and [when you can drive after a tummy tuck or mommy makeover](/how-long-after-mommy-makeover-can-i-drive) has the full answer.

## When can you lift your children and exercise again after a mommy makeover?

The Aesthetic Society says no heavy lifting, such as picking up your children or baskets of laundry, for 4 to 6 weeks, and that you will probably need to wait 6 weeks before any strenuous activities. Cleveland Clinic says surgeons often recommend no heavy lifting or strenuous exercise for the first 4 to 6 weeks.

After a tummy tuck, [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar) says heavy lifting and strenuous exercise wait until your provider clears them, usually 4 to 8 weeks, and Cindy Wu, MD, says running and lifting are avoided for 6 weeks. No source we read gives a weight limit in pounds or a week for core exercise after muscle repair, so ask your surgeon, and ask about car seats and strollers by name. Walking comes first, and [exercise after a mommy makeover](/blog/mommy-makeover-recovery-exercises) goes through what follows it.

## How long does swelling last, and when will you see your final results?

After a tummy tuck, swelling keeps going down until about 3 months, Cindy Wu, MD, writes on the ASPS website. Frank Campanile, MD, also on the ASPS website, says it can take 6 months to a year to see your final mommy makeover results. The Aesthetic Society says fading and flattening of scars take 6 months to 2 years.

- **Numbness.** The Aesthetic Society says swelling and numbness may take months to resolve.
- **Scars.** The Aesthetic Society says you will likely have scars hidden in your bikini line or under your bra. A tummy tuck scar becomes thinner and lighter in color at around 12 to 18 months, Wu writes. See [tummy tuck recovery](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#recovery) and [breast lift recovery](/procedures/breast-lift-miami) for each part.
- **Sun and water.** Cleveland Clinic advises sunscreen with SPF 30 on the scar for 12 to 18 months after a tummy tuck if you must be in the sun. Pool and beach plans wait until your incisions have closed and your surgeon clears you.
- **Scar care and massage.** Ask your surgeon before adding silicone sheets or lymphatic massage. The sources above set no schedule for them.
- **Keeping the result.** The Aesthetic Society says pregnancy or weight gain after these surgeries may compromise your results.

## Why is recovering from several surgeries at once different?

The surgeries share one recovery, which may be more intense, The Aesthetic Society says. Risk also rises with what is added: in an insurance database of 25,478 patients, major complications followed 3.1% of tummy tucks done alone and 4.6% of those with liposuction and a breast procedure ([Winocour and colleagues, Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery, 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26505716/)).

| Part of your plan | What it adds to recovery | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tummy tuck | The most uncomfortable part; bent at the waist for 7 to 10 days; an abdominal binder | The Aesthetic Society; Cindy Wu, MD |
| Breast lift | No lifting, driving, laundry or cleaning for the first 2 weeks | The Aesthetic Society |
| Liposuction | A compression garment over the treated areas for 4 to 6 weeks | [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/liposuction/aftercare-recovery) |

- **Blood clots.** In 129,007 patients having cosmetic surgery, blood clots within 30 days followed 0.20% of combined procedures and 0.04% of single ones, and 0.28% of combined body and breast procedures, the highest rate the study lists ([Winocour and colleagues, Aesthetic Surgery Journal, 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28207041/)). The study calls the overall rate relatively low.
- **Time in surgery.** In a study of complex plastic surgery at one US medical center, each extra hour of surgery raised the odds of a complication by 21%, and risk rose once surgery passed 3.1 hours ([Hardy and colleagues, Aesthetic Surgery Journal, 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24696297/)). It was not limited to mommy makeovers.
- **The newest review.** A 2026 review of 8 studies found that a tummy tuck combined with breast surgery carries a complication profile comparable to a tummy tuck alone and higher than breast surgery alone, and concluded that it is generally safe in carefully selected patients ([Skorochod and Wolf, Aesthetic Plastic Surgery, 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42089979/)). It rates its own evidence as moderate to very low certainty.

These are published figures, not one practice's rates. Use them to ask how long your surgery will take, whether your plan should be one surgery or two, and [how a plan is priced part by part](/procedures/mommy-makeover-miami#pricing).

## Frequently asked questions

### How painful is recovery from a mommy makeover?

The Aesthetic Society says the recovery period is significant and can be intense the first few days, and that the tummy tuck is the most uncomfortable part. Pain medication can manage it, and you can ask your surgeon about a pain pump. If the pain is extreme or lasts long, contact your surgeon.

### What follow-up appointments should I expect after a mommy makeover?

The Aesthetic Society says your first follow-up is usually within 5 days, and that your surgeon can remove drainage tubes at that visit if you have them. Your surgeon sets the rest of the schedule, so ask when you return for follow-up care, one of the questions ASPS suggests asking.

### What are the signs of infection after a mommy makeover?

MedlinePlus says to contact your surgeon about more redness or pain around an incision, drainage that increases or becomes thick, tan, green or yellow or smells bad, or a temperature above 100°F (37.7°C) for more than 4 hours. The Aesthetic Society says to call immediately if swelling, redness, drainage or bleeding increases, or if you develop a fever.

### What are the most common complications during mommy makeover recovery?

In an insurance database of 25,478 patients having a tummy tuck, 31.5% of major complications were hematomas (bleeding under the skin), 27.2% infections and 20.2% suspected or confirmed blood clots (Winocour and colleagues, Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery, 2015). Those are shares of the complications, not of patients, from data for 2008 to 2013.

### Will I stay overnight after a mommy makeover?

The Aesthetic Society says it depends on your medical issues, the number of procedures, how long surgery takes and your caregiver at home, and your surgeon guides the decision. After cosmetic surgery in a doctor's office, [Florida requires](https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=64B8-9.009) that you be discharged within 24 hours of arriving. Either way, you cannot go home alone.

### How long should I stay in Miami after a mommy makeover?

It depends on your plan and your first follow-up visits, so no single number fits everyone. At Alluring, Dr. Karlinsky tells you at your consultation how many nights to plan in Miami before you are cleared to fly home, and your surgery, pre-op and follow-up dates are confirmed in writing.
