## Do breast lift scars go away?

Breast lift scars don't go away completely, but they fade a lot. [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-lift/procedure) says incision lines are permanent but "in most cases will fade and significantly improve over time", and [Cleveland Clinic's surgery center](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/cosmetic-plastic-surgery/procedures/breast-lift) says the most fading comes at around 1 year.

A scar is the trade every lift makes: [what a breast lift does](/procedures/breast-lift-miami#what-is-a-breast-lift) is remove stretched skin, and that takes an incision.

## Where are the scars for each type of breast lift?

The scars sit around the areola, on the lower breast, in the breast crease, or in a combination of those, depending on the type of lift. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23298-breast-lift) says almost all of them are hidden in the borders of the areolas, the breast crease and the natural contours of the breast, though you may see some on the surface.

| Type of lift | Where the scar sits | Usually for | Scarring |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crescent | Along the top edge of the areola | Minor sagging; less common | Short |
| Donut (periareolar) | A ring around the areola | Mild sagging or uneven areolas | Among the least |
| Lollipop (vertical) | The ring, plus a line down to the breast crease | Moderate sagging | Moderate |
| Anchor (inverted T) | The lollipop, plus a line along the crease | Severe sagging, or a lift with a reduction | The most |

These groupings come from [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/breast/breast-lift/detailed-procedure-information), and the crescent's short scar from [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK610682/). Our guide to [the types of breast lift](/blog/types-of-breast-lift) explains how surgeons choose, and you can see [the four lift patterns, drawn](/procedures/breast-lift-miami#types).

## How do breast lift scars change, month by month?

Breast lift scars start red or pink, stay that way for many months, and do most of their fading by about 1 year. We found no source with a month-by-month schedule for lift scars, so this table combines what each source says, and yours may differ.

![Timeline of how breast lift scars change. First 2 weeks: redness, swelling and bruising. First 2 months: red or pink lines that may look raised or firm. Up to month 3: itching around the incisions eases. Months 3 to 12: pink for many months, then paler and flatter. About 1 year: most of the fading is done. Scars are permanent, and timing may differ. Sources: Cleveland Clinic, The Aesthetic Society and the ASPS website.](IMAGE:timeline)

| When | What you may see | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First 2 weeks | Redness, swelling and bruising. Dressings or bandages go on after surgery; ask when they come off | Cleveland Clinic; The Aesthetic Society; ASPS |
| First 2 months | Red or pink lines that may look raised or firm. If a thick, raised scar forms, it usually develops 1 to 2 months after surgery | The Aesthetic Society; ASPS website; Cleveland Clinic |
| Up to month 3 | Itching around the incisions eases | The Aesthetic Society |
| Months 3 to 12 | Pink for many months, then paler and flatter. The first signs of a keloid can appear now or later | The Aesthetic Society; ASPS website; American Academy of Dermatology |
| About 1 year | Most of the fading is done. The scar is permanent | Cleveland Clinic |
| After 1 year | A faded line that can still darken in the sun | ASPS website |

The flattening comes from [Daniel Maman, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/how-to-maintain-minimal-scarring-after-breast-augmentation), writing on the ASPS website about augmentation scars. The raised-scar timing is [Cleveland Clinic's](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/21466-hypertrophic-scar) for hypertrophic scars, and the keloid timing, 3 to 12 months or longer, is from [the American Academy of Dermatology](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/a-z/keloids-symptoms).

To see results, look through real [breast lift before and after photos](/gallery/breast-lift), and ask how long after surgery each photo was taken.

## What do breast lift scars look like at 3 weeks?

At 3 weeks, breast lift scars are usually red or pink and may look raised, firm or darker than your skin, and that is expected. [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/breast/breast-lift/aftercare-recovery) says incisions stay red or pink for many months, and Cleveland Clinic says they may take several months to heal fully, so a closed line at 3 weeks is not a finished scar.

For now, follow your surgeon's wound care, ask whether it is time to start silicone, keep the scars out of direct sun and avoid stretching them. [Breast lift recovery, week by week](/blog/breast-lift-recovery-guide-miami) covers lifting, bras and exercise.

## When should you call your surgeon about a scar?

Call your surgical team right away for signs of infection or a wound problem. Cleveland Clinic's list is:

- Either breast becomes red, hot, swollen or painful
- Bleeding, drainage, redness or swelling at an incision
- Chest pain or dizziness
- Fever higher than 100.5°F
- Severe pain not helped by medicine
- Nausea or vomiting
- An incision that opens up (Daniel Maman, MD, writing on the ASPS website)

Call 911 or go to the emergency room for new or severe chest pain or trouble breathing.

Book a follow-up visit for slower changes. Daniel Maman, MD, names thick, raised scars and scars that stay red, dark or itchy for months. A scar growing past the edges of the incision may be a keloid, which [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/keloid-scar) says doesn't fade over time.

## What helps breast lift scars fade?

Sun protection has the clearest advice behind it, and silicone has been studied in at least 20 trials, though the results are mixed. Start either only once the incision has closed and your surgeon agrees. Neither erases a scar.

| Scar care | What the evidence says | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sunscreen, once healed | [The American Academy of Dermatology](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/injured-skin/burns/wound-care-minimize-scars) says it may reduce red or brown discoloration and help a scar fade faster | Yes: SPF 30 or higher, reapplied often |
| Silicone sheets or gel, once healed | [Expert guidelines](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24888226/) call it first-line; a [Cochrane review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24030657/) of 20 trials calls the evidence weak | Reasonable if your surgeon agrees |
| Massage | A [2012 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22093081/) of 10 publications calls the evidence weak, though surgical scars seemed to respond better | Only as your surgeon directs |
| Vitamin E on the skin | In a [1999 trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10417589/) of 15 patients after skin cancer surgery, no benefit, and 33% got a skin reaction | Skip it |
| Onion extract gel | In a [2006 trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16442038/) of 24 patients, on other surgical scars, no better than petroleum ointment | Don't expect more than ointment |
| Steroid shots or laser | Cleveland Clinic lists both for hypertrophic scars | Ask your surgeon or a dermatologist |

On sun: [Josef Hadeed, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/fun-in-the-sun-protecting-your-plastic-surgery-scars), quoted on the ASPS website, says sun on a fresh scar can turn a thin, pink line into "a dark brown or purple mark that may become permanent", and [he advises](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/planning-your-plastic-surgery-timeline-for-summer) avoiding direct sun on incisions for the first year. Clothing isn't always enough: he puts a white cotton T-shirt at about UPF 5.

On silicone, breast studies point both ways. [In 20 women after breast reduction](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8905040/), a sheet worn 12 hours a day for 2 months left 25% of scars hypertrophic (raised) at 2 months, against 60% of untreated scars. [In 129 women after breast reduction](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9810992/), neither silicone sheeting nor gel prevented hypertrophic scars, and the silicone scars did worse than those supported with paper tape, so ask your surgeon about tape too.

## What makes a breast lift scar worse?

Nicotine and stretching can make a scar thicker or wider, and some skin is simply more prone to raised scars.

- **Nicotine.** [Heather Furnas, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/how-nicotine-sabotages-plastic-surgery), writing on the ASPS website, lists delayed wound healing and "thick, wide scars", whether it comes from cigarettes, vapes, gum or patches. Your surgeon may ask you to quit three to six weeks before surgery through three to six weeks after.
- **Tension.** Daniel Maman, MD, says heavy lifting, vigorous exercise or sudden arm movements "can stretch the incisions and widen scars". The Aesthetic Society advises no lifting, driving, laundry or cleaning for the first 2 weeks, and [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-lift/recovery) says to wear an elastic bandage or support bra as you heal.
- **Your own skin.** Cleveland Clinic says keloids are more likely with a relative who gets them, a dark skin tone, or African, Asian or Hispanic descent, and the American Academy of Dermatology lists the chest among common sites. If you have had a raised scar or a keloid, tell your surgeon before surgery. A [2014 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4236363/) says the anchor's larger scar is a point to weigh if you tend to form them.

## How often do scar problems happen?

A [2019 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30527707/) of 34 studies and 1,888 patients who had a lift without implants found complications in 10.4%, most often scar problems (3%) and nipple and areola problems such as asymmetry or reduced sensation (2.9%). Its studies ran from 1980 to 2016.

## When can you ask about breast lift scar revision?

You can ask at any time, but many surgeons wait; [Karen Horton, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/an-exploration-of-scar-revision-surgery-applications), for one, waits up to a full year, or longer if healing was delayed, before considering surgical revision (quoted on the ASPS website). She waits typically 12 to 18 months, for full scar maturity, before laser or microneedling. Emily Sespaniak, NP, in the same article, says energy-based treatment on an immature scar can cause darkening that may be irreversible.

Raised scars often settle with time, and Cleveland Clinic says a provider may wait a few months or up to a year before treating one. In the study of 129 women, 64.3% had a hypertrophic crease scar at 3 months, 56.6% at 6 months and 35.3% at 1 year. Those were breast reduction scars, so it isn't a rate for lifts.

[ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/reconstructive-procedures/scar-revision) says "a scar cannot be completely erased", and [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24670-scar-revision) says revision surgery can leave a new scar.

## Are the scars different with a lift and implants?

An implant can go in through a lift incision, so it may not add a scar of its own. StatPearls describes this for a vertical lift, and a [1990 paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2185619/) for a ring around the areola. Ask your surgeon where yours would go, and see our guide to [a breast lift with implants](/blog/breast-lift-implants-miami).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you have a breast lift without scars?

No. A lift removes skin through an incision, so every surgical lift leaves a scar. The Aesthetic Society says even its least common, smallest-scar options "require incisions of some sort". Treatments with no incision don't remove stretched skin, and our guide to [non-surgical options](/blog/non-surgical-breast-lift) covers what they can change.

### Do breast lift scars ever go away completely?

No. [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/scars.html) says scars in general usually fade over time but never go away completely. Cleveland Clinic says almost all breast lift scars are hidden at the areola's border, in the crease or along the breast's contours.

### What do breast lift scars look like after one year?

Usually paler, flatter and softer than in the first months, but still there. Cleveland Clinic says most of the fading has happened by about 1 year, and Karen Horton, MD, says it can take up to a full year or more for scars to mature fully. Keep protecting them, since even mature scars can darken in the sun.

### Are lollipop scars better than anchor scars?

A lollipop scar is shorter, because it has no line along the crease, but The Aesthetic Society recommends it for moderate sagging, while an anchor suits severe sagging. The 2014 review says patients rate vertical scars higher but may need more revisions. StatPearls adds that the vertical line is more apparent, while the anchor's crease line is usually well hidden.

### Will breast lift scars show in a bikini?

It depends on the line and the top. The ring around the areola and the crease line usually sit under a top that covers the areola and lower breast. The vertical line runs down the lower breast, so a small top may show it, and the 1990 paper notes a crease scar shows when you lie down. Josef Hadeed, MD, speaking of tummy tuck scars, says sunscreen still goes under swimwear, as UV rays can penetrate thin, wet fabrics.

### When can I start silicone sheets or gel after a breast lift?

Only once the incisions have closed and your surgeon agrees. [The American Academy of Dermatology](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/a-z/keloids-treatment) says never to apply a silicone sheet to a scab or open wound. In the smaller breast reduction study above, women started when the stitches came out, but your surgeon may set a different schedule.
