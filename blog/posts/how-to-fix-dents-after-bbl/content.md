## Why Do Dents Happen After a BBL?

While Brazilian Butt Lifts (BBLs) have become increasingly popular for enhancing the buttocks' shape and size, many patients aren't prepared for the possibility of dents or irregularities after the procedure.

No source we rely on gives a dent-specific cause. The closest number is for contour irregularities: 2.29% of cases in a [2026 meta-analysis of 38 studies and 22,151 patients](https://doi.org/10.1097/PRS.0000000000012437), with minor complications overall at 3.58% (Elsaftawy and colleagues, *Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery*, 2026). That counts contour irregularities as those studies reported them, so it can't tell you how many women notice a dent in the mirror.

## What Happens After BBL Dent Correction?

Recovering from BBL dent correction requires patience and careful adherence to your surgeon’s guidelines. A revision is a new surgery, so ask your surgeon which of these limits apply again.

During the initial phase, expect some swelling and bruising. A plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS says compression garments help alleviate swelling.

Avoid sitting directly on your buttocks for at least two weeks post-procedure, [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23308-brazilian-butt-lift) says, then keep sitting to about 10 minutes at a time for the first 6 weeks, according to a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS. Cleveland Clinic says most people cannot sit without a BBL pillow for about 8 weeks. We'll schedule regular follow-up appointments to monitor your progress.

The Aesthetic Society says normal activities and exercise can resume after 8 weeks, once your surgeon clears you, and Cleveland Clinic puts full recovery at 2 to 3 months and notes that it can take up to 6 months. However, strenuous exercise should wait until your surgeon provides clearance.

Your diligent post-operative care strongly influences your final outcome, so following all instructions carefully is essential to achieve a good resolution of your BBL dents.

## How Can You Prevent Dents in the Future?

Long-term success following your BBL dent correction depends largely on preventive measures and ongoing maintenance.

Strict adherence to post-operative care guidelines matters, particularly your compression garment: a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS advises wearing it around the clock, except in the shower, for the first month, then at least 12 hours a day in the second month, or as your surgeon directs.

It helps alleviate swelling, according to a [plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/six-things-you-need-to-know-about-recovering-from-a-brazilian-butt-lift).

Regular follow-up appointments allow us to identify and address potential issues early. Cleveland Clinic also advises keeping a consistent weight to preserve your result.

## When Should You See a Surgeon for BBL Dents?

While most BBL procedures heal beautifully over time, knowing exactly when to seek professional evaluation can greatly impact treatment outcomes.

Raise any persistent dent at your next follow-up. Swelling can hide your results; it sometimes takes 3 to 6 months to subside (The Aesthetic Society), so ask again once it has settled.

However, don't hesitate to seek evaluation earlier if you notice significant irregularities during recovery. Uneven fat distribution or asymmetry that affects your self-confidence deserves professional attention.

During regular follow-up appointments, discuss any concerns with your surgeon. They can assess whether simple solutions or more extensive interventions will address your specific concerns.

A firm lump that hurts, grows or drains should be checked now. That is our own conservative advice. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/surgical-wound-infection) lists a noticeable odor from the incision, pus, warmth, redness, pain when you touch the area, or a fever above 101°F (38.4°C) as signs of a surgical wound infection. If you have shortness of breath, chest pain or an unusual heartbeat, [ASPS says to seek medical attention immediately](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/buttock-enhancement/recovery).
