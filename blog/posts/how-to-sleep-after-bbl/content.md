## Why Is Sleep Position Crucial After BBL?

Although many patients focus primarily on post-surgical pain management, we can't overstate how critical proper sleep positioning becomes for BBL surgery success.

When you apply pressure on the buttocks during sleep, you directly compromise the survival of grafted fat cells that need ideal conditions to establish blood supply.

During recovery, these newly transferred cells remain extremely fragile and vulnerable to damage.

Sleeping on your stomach or side becomes essential for protecting your investment: a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS says "the fat is very susceptible to pressure necrosis, or pressure that kills off the fat cells."
