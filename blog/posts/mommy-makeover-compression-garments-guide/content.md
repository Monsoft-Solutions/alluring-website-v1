## Do You Need Compression Garments After a Mommy Makeover?

Most likely, yes. The [American Society of Plastic Surgeons](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/mommy-makeover/recovery) (ASPS) says an elastic bandage or support bra will minimize swelling and support the breasts, and a compression garment may also be used to control swelling in the abdomen, waist and buttocks. [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/mommy-makeover/aftercare-recovery) says you will need to wear an abdominal binder after a tummy tuck.

If you are still weighing surgery, [what your mommy makeover plan combines](/procedures/mommy-makeover-miami) decides what you wear in the first weeks. Many Spanish speakers call the garment a faja.

## Why Mommy Makeover Compression Garments Matter for Your Recovery

Mommy makeover compression garments provide gentle, consistent pressure to surgical sites, which helps control swelling in the abdomen, waist and buttocks, ASPS says. The Aesthetic Society says to wear yours as directed to reduce the likelihood of loose or sagging skin.

Daniel Maman, MD, writing on the [ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/the-importance-of-compression-after-plastic-surgery), also credits garments with less bruising, softer scars and better circulation, which he says reduces the risk of blood clots and infection, though his post cites no study. A [2023 review in Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery Global Open](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37753333/) (Ormseth and colleagues) found the strongest support, after breast and abdominal procedures, for reducing pain, and no effect on seroma rates (fluid pockets under the skin), and it weighs any benefit against possible harms such as slowed blood flow in the veins. A [small randomized trial in Aesthetic Surgery Journal](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36282895/) (Fontes de Moraes and colleagues, 2023) of 32 women after a tummy tuck, all of whom also had lymphatic drainage massage, found less swelling under the skin after 24 days in those who wore no garment. That is a reason to ask your surgeon why they chose your garment, not a reason to skip it.

### How Compression Supports Tissue Healing

Maman writes that constant pressure on the surgical site helps prevent excess fluid from building up under the skin and encourages your body to reabsorb it. Your skin also needs support as it redrapes over your new contours: after liposuction, [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/liposuction/aftercare-recovery) says the garment is there to control swelling and promote skin contraction.

### What Goes Where After a Mommy Makeover

Each part of the surgery has its own piece, according to ASPS, The Aesthetic Society and Cleveland Clinic. Your surgeon decides which ones you wear:

- **Breasts:** an elastic bandage, surgical bra or support bra.
- **Abdomen and waist:** an abdominal binder or compression garment.
- **Liposuction areas and buttocks:** a compression garment.

![Clean line drawing of a faceless woman in a sleeveless top and long pants, with labels showing what goes where after a mommy makeover: an elastic bandage, surgical bra or support bra for the breasts; an abdominal binder or compression garment for the abdomen and waist; a compression garment for liposuction areas and buttocks.](IMAGE:what-goes-where)

Always select medical-grade options recommended by your surgeon for breathability and fit. See also [tummy tuck recovery](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#recovery), [Lipo 360 recovery](/procedures/liposuction-miami#recovery) and [what lipo foam and boards are for](/what-is-lipo-foam).

## How Long to Wear Mommy Makeover Compression Garments: Week-by-Week Timeline

Expect to wear a garment day and night for the first 1 to 3 weeks, then at night for several weeks once your surgeon approves, says Daniel Maman, MD, on the ASPS website. [Cindy Wu, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/answers-to-common-tummy-tuck-questions), also on the ASPS website, says 4 to 6 weeks in a garment after a tummy tuck. No source gives one schedule for a whole mommy makeover.

### Weeks 1-3: Full-Time Compression (Except Showering)

Wear yours continuously, removing it only to shower or bathe. Maman says the exact time varies with your body, your procedure and your surgeon. The Aesthetic Society says you may shower once the dressings are off, usually within 72 hours, and that your first follow-up with your surgeon is usually within 5 days.

Many patients find sleeping in compression garments awkward at first. The Aesthetic Society says your surgeon may suggest several pillows under your back and your legs slightly bent at the hips. See [how to sleep in your garment after liposuction](/how-to-sleep-after-liposuction).

![A neat stack of folded clothing on the linen seat of an oak chair in a bright bedroom: two beige compression garments and a soft bra with a front zip.](IMAGE:garment-on-chair)

Keep a second garment on hand for rotation. This allows proper washing while maintaining constant compression.

### After That: Beginning the Transition

Maman says your surgeon will tell you after your first follow-up when you can begin taking the garment off, and for how long, and that you can then expect to wear it at night for several weeks. We found no society or surgeon source for a set number of part-time hours a day, so ask your surgeon. You may also hear "stage 2" for a lighter garment some patients move to later. No source we read defines it, so ask whether you need one and when. Our guide to [exercise after a mommy makeover](/blog/mommy-makeover-recovery-exercises) covers walking and what comes next.

### Weeks 4-6: The Range for the Abdomen and Liposuction Areas

Those 4 to 6 weeks in a garment are each one part's figure, not a rule for a whole mommy makeover. Some swelling after garment removal is normal: after a tummy tuck, swelling keeps going down until about 3 months (Cindy Wu, MD, on the ASPS website). If swelling increases, The Aesthetic Society says to call your surgeon immediately.

### Beyond 6 Weeks: As Your Surgeon Directs

We found no source that sets an end date past these ranges, and some procedures may require longer compression periods. Trust your medical team's guidance over general timelines.

## What Should You Wear After a Mommy Makeover?

Choose clothes you can put on without lifting your arms or bending: front-opening tops, a zip-up hoodie, soft high-waist pants and slip-on shoes. Your garment and a soft bra go underneath. An [ASPS article on garments after breast surgery](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/finding-comfort-after-breast-surgery-a-guide-to-compression-bras-and-postsurgical-garments) says many patients prefer front closures, which are easy to take on and off without help.

![Flat lay of clothes to wear after a mommy makeover: a front-opening linen shirt, a zip-up hoodie, soft drawstring pants, slip-on shoes, a robe and a soft front-zip bra with no underwire.](IMAGE:what-to-wear)

- **Tops:** front-opening shirts, a zip-up hoodie or a robe.
- **Pants:** soft, loose and high-waist, so nothing rubs your incision. After a tummy tuck, you stay bent at the waist for 7 to 10 days (Cindy Wu, MD, on the ASPS website).
- **Shoes:** slip-ons, so you don't have to bend.
- **Bras:** soft, with no underwire. The bra question below has the timing.

These are practical tips, not study findings.

## Managing Mommy Makeover Compression Garments in Miami's Humidity

Miami's warm, humid climate can intensify sweating during recovery. What follows is comfort advice, not a medical claim: we found no source that says humidity changes how you heal.

### Fabric Selection for Tropical Climates

Look for garments labeled as moisture-wicking or quick-dry. These materials pull sweat away from your skin and allow it to evaporate. Kelly Killeen, MD, says in the ASPS article on garments after breast surgery that it's important not to have rough seams against the skin. Ask your surgeon which brands they recommend.

### Staying Cool During Recovery

Stay in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible, and limit outdoor time to cooler mornings or evenings. After liposuction, The Aesthetic Society says to wait a minimum of 6 weeks before exposure to sun and heat, including sunbathing, because of the possibility of swelling.

### Hygiene Tips for Humid Conditions

Maman writes that your garment must always be clean to prevent infection at the surgical site, and that it is fine to take it off periodically for cleaning. Hand-wash with mild soap and air dry completely before wearing, unless your surgeon's office says otherwise. Keep your skin clean and dry beneath the garment.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid with Mommy Makeover Compression Garments

The common ones are stopping before your surgeon says so, wearing a garment that no longer fits or isn't fully dry, and following a schedule from the internet instead of your surgeon's.

### Garment Rotation and Hygiene Errors

It helps to own two, so one can be washed while wearing the other. Never wear a garment that hasn't fully dried.

### How Tight Should a Compression Garment Be?

Snug, but not painful. Daniel Maman, MD, on the ASPS website, says a garment should fit snugly enough to do its job but not so tight that it is uncomfortable or restricts your breathing. If it hurts, talk to your surgeon. If it gets loose as swelling goes down, ask about a smaller size.

![Card titled Is your garment the right fit? Right fit: snug and even, comfortable, and you can breathe freely. Too tight: it hurts or restricts your breathing, new numbness or tingling, skin turning pale or dark, deep grooves that don't fade; take it off and call your surgeon. Too loose: slipping or bunching, loose after the swelling goes down; ask to be fitted for a smaller size.](IMAGE:fit-check)

- **Right fit:** snug and even, comfortable, and you can breathe freely.
- **Too tight:** it hurts or restricts your breathing, new numbness or tingling, skin turning pale or dark, or deep grooves that don't fade. Take it off and call your surgeon.
- **Too loose:** slipping or bunching, or loose after the swelling goes down. Ask to be fitted for a smaller size.

The 2023 Ormseth review lists discomfort, skin damage and slowed blood flow in the veins among the consequences of ill-fitting garments. [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/what-you-should-know-about-compression-socks), writing about compression socks rather than surgical garments, says to take them off and call your provider for pain, numbness, tingling or skin discoloration. No source we read lists these signs for garments after a mommy makeover, and deep grooves are common practice only, but don't wait on any of them.

Get properly fitted at your post-op appointments. Your body changes rapidly during recovery, and garment fit should be reassessed regularly.

### Rushing the Transition

Don't stop garment wear before surgeon approval, even if you feel better. Maman says the goal is to wear it as consistently as possible.

## When to Contact Your Surgeon During the Compression Garments Phase

Call about more redness, pain, swelling or bleeding at an incision, drainage that increases or turns thick, tan, green or yellow or smells bad, or a temperature above 100°F for more than 4 hours, [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000738.htm) says. Severe pain, new numbness, or skin color changes under your garment also need a call right away.

The Aesthetic Society also lists dizziness, nausea or vomiting, and calls shortness of breath, chest pains and unusual heartbeat red flags.

[MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000156.htm) lists swelling, pain or tenderness, warmth and redness in a leg or arm as signs of a deep vein clot. With a clot, chest pain, coughing up blood, difficulty breathing or fainting mean going to the emergency room or calling 911.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long should I wear compression garments after a mommy makeover?

No society publishes one schedule for a whole mommy makeover. Daniel Maman, MD, on the ASPS website, says a garment is worn day and night for 1 to 3 weeks, then at night for several weeks once your surgeon approves. Cindy Wu, MD, also on the ASPS website, says 4 to 6 weeks in a garment after a tummy tuck.

### How do compression garments help with swelling and bruising after a mommy makeover?

Daniel Maman, MD, on the ASPS website, says steady pressure helps prevent fluid from building up under the skin, and that garments can help keep blood cells from moving toward the skin's surface, which minimizes bruising. His post cites no study for either, and a small 2023 trial after tummy tucks found less swelling under the skin after 24 days without a garment (Fontes de Moraes and colleagues).

### What are the risks of not wearing compression garments after surgery?

The Aesthetic Society says to wear yours as directed to reduce the likelihood of loose or sagging skin. How much a garment changes complications is less settled: a [2024 systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39021663/) (Arkoubi, JPRAS Open) found only 5 trials totaling 130 patients and called the evidence weak. Ask your surgeon before you stop.

### Can I sleep without my compression garment after a mommy makeover?

Not in the first weeks. Daniel Maman, MD, on the ASPS website, says the garment is worn day and night for 1 to 3 weeks, then at night for several weeks once your surgeon approves. Take it off at night only when your surgeon says so.

### When can I wear a normal bra after a mommy makeover?

It depends on your surgery and your surgeon. Cleveland Clinic says women can usually wear a regular bra about 8 weeks after a breast lift. In an ASPS article, Kelly Killeen, MD, says most women return to normal bras in about 1 month, and Karen Horton, MD, advises no underwire for at least 3 months.
