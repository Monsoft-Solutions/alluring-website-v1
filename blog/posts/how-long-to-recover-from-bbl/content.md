## How long does BBL recovery take?

Most people go back to a desk job 10 to 14 days after a Brazilian butt lift (BBL), sit normally and exercise again at about 8 weeks, and see their final shape 3 to 6 months after surgery. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23308-brazilian-butt-lift) puts full recovery at two to three months and notes that it can take up to six. [ASPS's procedure page](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/buttock-enhancement/procedure) says final results take a year, so your shape can keep settling after that.

BBL recovery takes this long because grafted fat is fragile. A [plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/six-things-you-need-to-know-about-recovering-from-a-brazilian-butt-lift) says the fat is very susceptible to pressure necrosis, pressure that kills off fat cells, which is why most BBL recovery rules are about how you sit, sleep and move.

## What happens in the first two weeks after a BBL?

The first two weeks are the strictest part of BBL recovery: no sitting or lying on your buttocks, a compression garment around the clock and short walks to keep your blood moving. Cleveland Clinic says you won't be able to sit or lie on your buttocks for at least two weeks, so you stand, walk or lie on your stomach or side instead.

Arrange help at home before surgery. The Aesthetic Society notes that you won't feel like yourself for one or two weeks, and says not to do anything strenuous, including lifting, for four to six weeks.

### How much does a BBL hurt in the first week?

The first several days after a BBL are often the most painful, Cleveland Clinic says, and most people need some pain medication for the first four to five days, according to a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS. The areas where fat was removed tend to feel tender and bruised. Cleveland Clinic says the pain should ease after one to two weeks.

Short walks start early even so. The Aesthetic Society says you should be able to get up and walk after the second day, which helps prevent blood clots.

### When can you sit, and how should you sleep?

You can't sit directly on your buttocks for at least two weeks after a BBL, according to Cleveland Clinic. After that, surgeons allow short periods on a BBL pillow, which The Aesthetic Society describes as a soft pillow placed beneath your hamstring muscles, not touching your buttocks. A plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS keeps sitting to about 10 minutes at a time for the first six weeks, and Cleveland Clinic says most people can't sit without the pillow for about eight weeks. An [ASPS article by surgeon Kamran Azad, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/recovering-from-a-brazilian-butt-lift) is stricter: no sitting directly on your buttocks for at least eight weeks. Our guide to [how long after a BBL you can sit](/how-long-after-bbl-can-i-sit) covers sitting in more detail.

Sleep on your stomach or side, as Cleveland Clinic advises, and stay off your back until your surgeon clears it. The Aesthetic Society says to avoid direct pressure on the buttocks for at least eight weeks, so back sleeping can wait that long. Pillow setups and positions are in [how to sleep after a BBL](/how-to-sleep-after-bbl).

## What should you expect at 2, 4, 6 and 8 weeks after a BBL?

BBL recovery loosens in steps: desk work around two weeks, a lighter garment schedule and fast walks after the first month, and normal sitting and exercise around eight weeks.

### 2 weeks post-op

Two weeks after a BBL, most people are back at a desk job or close to it, and the worst pain is behind them. The Aesthetic Society says you can return to work, drive or go to the movies after 10 to 14 days, as long as you use a pillow when you sit. You're still wearing the compression garment around the clock and still avoiding heavy lifting.

### 4 weeks post-op

Four weeks (one month) after a BBL, the round-the-clock garment phase ends for many patients, and Cleveland Clinic's one-month limit on heavy lifting and strenuous activity is up; ask your surgeon before restarting either. You're still sitting only briefly, on a pillow. Swelling is going down and some of the grafted fat is reabsorbed, so your buttocks usually look smaller than they did in week one. That's expected, and it's too early to judge the result.

### 5 and 6 weeks post-op

Five and six weeks after a BBL, you're in the second month of compression, wearing the garment at least 12 hours a day, and sitting on a BBL pillow in short stretches. Light activity such as fast-paced walking is fine after the first month, according to an ASPS article by surgeon Kamran Azad, MD, but workouts that load your buttocks still wait. Swelling keeps easing and your shape keeps changing for months.

### 8 weeks post-op

Around eight weeks after a BBL, most day-to-day limits lift. Cleveland Clinic says you won't be able to sit without your pillow for approximately eight weeks, and The Aesthetic Society says normal activities and exercise can resume after eight weeks. Surgeons differ on how hard to start: Cleveland Clinic describes light exercise at this point, so get cleared before you go back to squats, running or heavy weights.

## How long do you wear a compression garment after a BBL?

Wear your compression garment around the clock, except in the shower, for the first month after a BBL, then at least 12 hours a day in the second month, or as your surgeon directs. That schedule comes from a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS; Dr. Azad's ASPS article recommends eight weeks of wear, and The Aesthetic Society says you will likely wear one for six to eight weeks. The evidence behind any exact duration is thin: a [2023 review in *Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery Global Open*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10519563/) found limited high-quality evidence supporting compression garments, so your surgeon's schedule is the one to follow.

Two practical points matter in hot weather. A [plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS about BBL smell](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/is-bbl-smell-real-heres-what-you-need-to-know) says garments should be changed and washed regularly, ideally every 24 hours, so keep two garments and ask your surgeon how long it can be off for a change. If a garment rolls, pinches or leaves deep marks, ask your surgeon about sizing rather than loosening it yourself. The [BBL compression garment timeline](/blog/bbl-compression-garment-timeline) covers garment stages and fit.

## When can you go back to work, drive and exercise after a BBL?

Cleveland Clinic says you should be able to go back to work 10 to 14 days after a BBL, depending on your job, and advises a standing desk until you've completely healed if you usually sit for long periods. The Aesthetic Society says you can return to work and drive after 10 to 14 days if you use a pillow when you sit. Some patients go back sooner: an ASPS article says patients return to work between days 7 and 10. A job that involves lifting needs a longer break, so ask your surgeon for a return date before you book time off.

Exercise after a BBL comes back in stages:

1. **From day 1 or 2:** short walks. The Aesthetic Society says walking after the second day helps prevent blood clots.
2. **First month:** no heavy lifting or strenuous activity, per Cleveland Clinic. The Aesthetic Society says four to six weeks for anything strenuous, including lifting.
3. **After a month:** light activity such as fast-paced walking.
4. **Around 8 weeks:** normal exercise, once your surgeon clears you. Ask specifically about squats, lunges and running before you restart them.

The [exercise timeline after a BBL](/blog/exercises-after-bbl-timeline) lists which workouts to add and when.

## Which BBL recovery mistakes cost you grafted fat?

The BBL recovery mistakes to watch for put pressure on your buttocks too early. Some fat loss is expected even with good aftercare, so the aim is to avoid losing more.

- **Sitting on your buttocks in the first two weeks.** An ASPS article says sitting too soon puts you at risk for asymmetrical or quickly diminishing results. If it happens by accident, tell your surgeon.
- **Sitting too long, even on a pillow.** Keep to short stretches, about 10 minutes at a time for the first six weeks by the rule of a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS, and stand up between them.
- **Sleeping on your back before you're cleared.** Lying on your back is pressure on the graft, just like sitting.
- **Skipping or loosening the garment** in the first month.
- **Staying in bed.** The Aesthetic Society says walking after the second day helps prevent blood clots.
- **Going back to the gym early.** No heavy lifting or strenuous activity for the first month, and no normal workouts until you're cleared at around eight weeks.
- **Smoking or vaping.** The Aesthetic Society says not smoking improves healing, and [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/surgical-wound-infection) says smoking, including e-cigarettes, increases your risk of surgical wound infection.
- **Judging the result too early.** The Aesthetic Society advises waiting at least three months before shopping for new clothes, because swelling hides the final shape. That is also the closest guidance on [when you can wear jeans after a BBL](/when-can-i-wear-jeans-after-bbl).

Two other questions come up often: [how many massages you need after a BBL](/how-many-massages-after-bbl), where there is no agreed number, and [what to eat after a BBL](/how-to-feed-the-fat-after-bbl).

Plan to stay near your surgeon for at least four to five days after a BBL, and longer if you can. Sitting for long periods during travel raises the risk of blood clots: the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/blood-clots/risk-factors/travel.html) says the longer you are immobile, the greater your risk. On the way home:

- Get up and walk for 5 to 10 minutes for every hour you sit, the advice of a plastic surgeon quoted by ASPS in that article.
- Stay well hydrated.
- Wear compression stockings if your surgeon recommends them.
- Ask your surgeon how to sit on the flight.

## How safe is a BBL in 2026?

The risk is lower when the fat stays under the skin, above the gluteal muscle. In a [2019 safety advisory](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/documents/Patient-Safety/BBL/Gluteal-Fat-Grafting-Safety-Advisory_Jun19.pdf), plastic surgery societies said research findings suggest mortality risk can be minimized if the entire procedure is confined to that space. The main danger is a fat embolism: Cleveland Clinic explains that the buttocks contain many blood vessels and nerves, and if the cannula strikes them, it can lead to injury, a fat embolism or death.

- **Florida's rule for office surgery.** Under [Florida Statutes §458.328](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/458.328), a physician performing a BBL in an office surgery setting may inject fat only into the space under the skin and may not cross the fascia over the gluteal muscle. The same rule requires ultrasound guidance, or other technology authorized under board rule, while placing and moving the cannula; a ratio of one physician to one patient from the start of anesthesia until extubation; an in-person exam no later than the day before surgery; and that the physician perform the fat removal and injection personally.
- **What happened in South Florida.** South Florida recorded 25 deaths from BBL fat embolism between 2010 and 2022, according to a [2023 study by Pazmiño and Garcia in *Aesthetic Surgery Journal*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9896146/): 14 after the 2018 guidelines from the Aesthetic Surgery Education and Research Foundation, and 12 after Florida's 2019 emergency rule. In the 11 cases they examined at autopsy, the authors describe fat in the muscle. These are historical figures, not today's risk.
- **What surgeon surveys show.** Surveys estimated the BBL death rate at 1 in 3,448 in 2017 and 1 in 14,952 in 2019, after surgical safety guidance changed ([Rios and Gupta, *Aesthetic Surgery Journal*, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1093/asj/sjaa098)). Those figures are self-reported and historical, so read them as a trend rather than today's odds.
- **Complications overall.** A [2026 meta-analysis of 38 studies and 22,151 patients](https://doi.org/10.1097/PRS.0000000000012437) found minor complications in 3.58% of BBL patients, including uneven contours in 2.29% and seroma, a pocket of fluid, in 2.03%. Pulmonary embolism, a blood clot in the lungs, was reported separately, at 0.04%. Major complications were less common with ultrasound guidance: 0.02% versus 0.08% (Elsaftawy and colleagues, *Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery*, 2026). That is an association, not proof of cause.
- **How common BBLs are.** ASPS estimates that 25,662 BBLs were performed in the US in 2025, 5% fewer than in 2024, according to its [2025 Plastic Surgery Statistics Report](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/documents/news/statistics/2025/plastic-surgery-statistics-report-2025.pdf).

Ask any surgeon you consider, including us, where they place the fat, whether they use ultrasound and how many BBLs they run at the same time. You deserve a clear answer to each. The [BBL safety section](/procedures/brazilian-butt-lift-bbl-miami#safety) explains why fat placement matters.

## Which symptoms mean you should call your surgeon now?

<CalloutBox type="warning">
Seek medical attention immediately, and call 911, for shortness of breath, chest pain, a fast or unusual heartbeat, coughing up blood, dizziness or fainting. [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/buttock-enhancement/recovery) and [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/17400-pulmonary-embolism) list these signs. After a BBL they can mean a blood clot or fat in the lungs, and they need emergency care, not a callback.
</CalloutBox>

Call your provider right away or go to the emergency room for a swollen, painful leg with warmth or red skin, especially after travel. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/16911-deep-vein-thrombosis-dvt) lists these among the signs of a blood clot in a deep vein and says not to wait to see if they go away.

Call your surgeon the same day for:

- **Signs of infection**, which typically appear three to seven days after surgery: a noticeable odor from an incision, pus, redness, warmth, pain when you touch the area, or a fever above 101°F (38.4°C), according to Cleveland Clinic. Our post on [whether BBLs smell](/why-do-bbl-stink) explains where odor comes from and when to call.
- **A soft, swollen pocket that feels like it holds fluid**, which can be a seroma.
- **A lump in the buttocks that hurts or grows.** [Fat necrosis](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/24187-fat-necrosis), where fat tissue dies, can feel like a lump or a hard node and usually doesn't hurt, Cleveland Clinic says. Have any lump that hurts, grows or drains checked.
- **Pain that gets worse instead of better** after the first week.

## When will you see your final BBL results?

You'll see your final BBL shape 3 to 6 months after surgery, once swelling has settled, by the timing The Aesthetic Society and a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS give. ASPS's procedure page says final results take a year, so small changes after that aren't unusual. The acute risk of losing grafted fat subsides around month 3, according to a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS, and another says that during months three to six the shape should further refine and the curve should become more apparent.

ASPS says patients retain 60 to 80% of the transferred fat on average, in some cases more and in others less. A plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS puts the average "take" at about 60%, and [another](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/seven-things-you-need-to-know-about-a-brazilian-butt-lift) says 20 to 40% of the injected fat is absorbed by the body during healing. Cleveland Clinic advises keeping your weight consistent to preserve the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take to recover from a BBL?

Most people return to desk work 10 to 14 days after a BBL, sit normally and exercise again at about 8 weeks, and feel recovered at 2 to 3 months. Your shape shows at 3 to 6 months, and Cleveland Clinic notes that full recovery can take up to 6 months.

### When can I sit after a BBL?

Not directly on your buttocks for at least 2 weeks, according to Cleveland Clinic. After that, sit only briefly on a BBL pillow, about 10 minutes at a time through week 6 by the rule of a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS, and expect to need the pillow for about 8 weeks.

### How long do I wear a compression garment after a BBL?

Around the clock, except in the shower, for the first month, then at least 12 hours a day in the second month, or as your surgeon directs. The Aesthetic Society says you will likely wear one for six to eight weeks, and evidence for any exact duration is limited.

### How should I sleep after a BBL?

On your stomach or side, as Cleveland Clinic advises. Stay off your back until your surgeon clears it. The Aesthetic Society advises avoiding direct pressure on the buttocks for at least 8 weeks, so ask before you go back to sleeping on your back.

### When can I go back to work after a BBL?

At about 10 to 14 days for a desk job, according to Cleveland Clinic, and The Aesthetic Society says to use a pillow when you sit. An ASPS article says patients return between days 7 and 10, and a job that involves lifting needs longer, so ask your surgeon for a date.

### When can I exercise after a BBL?

Walk from the first day or two. Avoid heavy lifting and strenuous activity for the first month, add light activity such as fast walking after that, and return to normal exercise at about 8 weeks once your surgeon clears you.

### What should I expect 5 weeks after a BBL?

At 5 weeks you're past the worst pain, in the second month of compression (at least 12 hours a day), still sitting only briefly on a pillow and doing light activity. Swelling keeps easing, and your shape keeps refining until 3 to 6 months.

### How much of the transferred fat survives a BBL?

ASPS says patients retain 60 to 80% of the transferred fat on average, in some cases more and in others less. A plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS puts the average "take" at about 60%, and the acute risk of losing grafted fat subsides around month 3, according to a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS.

### Is recovery faster after an awake BBL?

No source we rely on compares recovery after an awake BBL with recovery after general anesthesia. The sitting, sleeping and compression rules protect the grafted fat, so ask your surgeon whether they change at all, and which parts of the first few days depend on the anesthesia used.
