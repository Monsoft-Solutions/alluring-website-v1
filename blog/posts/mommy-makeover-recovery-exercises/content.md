## When can I exercise after a mommy makeover?

You can walk on the day of surgery and add movement in stages from there. [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/mommy-makeover/aftercare-recovery) says you will probably need to wait 6 weeks before any strenuous activities, with no heavy lifting for 4 to 6 weeks. Abdominal exercises start only when your surgeon clears them.

If you are still deciding, plan around two things: how long you would be out of the gym, and who lifts the kids meanwhile. A [mommy makeover in Miami](/procedures/mommy-makeover-miami) or anywhere else combines surgeries, so the limits of each part apply together. This guide covers exercise only. For pain, swelling and time off work, see [week-by-week mommy makeover recovery](/blog/mommy-makeover-recovery-signs).

## What are safe exercises after a mommy makeover?

Walking comes first, because it keeps blood moving and you can start on the day of surgery. Deep breathing and ankle circles fit the first days. A stationary bike and light lower-body work come next, with your surgeon's approval. Strength, running and core exercises wait for clearance, and your combination of procedures sets the order.

Light movement starts early, while full workouts wait for tissue stability. Rushing strains incisions and increases swelling.

Why does this matter? Your body is healing internally even when external incisions look good. Muscle repairs, especially from tummy tuck procedures, need time to strengthen. Blood vessels are rebuilding, and tissues are settling into their new positions. Pushing too hard disrupts this process and can compromise your results.

Always secure approval at follow-ups. The Aesthetic Society says recovery time varies greatly among individuals.

## Week-by-week exercises after a mommy makeover: your workout plan

Healing progresses in phases, so focus on low-strain moves first. Remember, these are guidelines; your surgeon's instructions take priority. The exercises below are common starting points, not a protocol from any society, and no source we read sets reps or holds for them, so do them only as your surgeon directs. Bring the list to your consultation and ask what your surgeon would change.

### Weeks 1-2: focus on gentle mobility

Prioritize rest while introducing basics to boost blood flow. Your body is working hard to heal, so think of movement as medicine rather than exercise.

Start with short walks around your home or neighborhood. Keep the pace slow and stop immediately if pain increases. [Cindy Wu, MD, on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/answers-to-common-tummy-tuck-questions), describes walking three times on the day of surgery, which keeps the risk of blood clots in the legs low. The Aesthetic Society expects you to be walking around the house frequently by the end of the first week.

A [2020 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31858207/) found that blood clots occur more often after a tummy tuck than after any other cosmetic surgery, and more often still when liposuction is added (Mittal and colleagues, Aesthetic Plastic Surgery, 2020). You also stay bent at the waist for 7 to 10 days after a tummy tuck (Cindy Wu, MD, on the ASPS website), so these first walks look nothing like a workout.

Deep breathing exercises support healing without abdominal strain. Inhale deeply through your nose, letting your chest expand. Exhale slowly through your mouth. This engages your diaphragm gently. Take a few slow breaths several times a day, as your surgeon directs.

Avoid lifting or bending at the waist. Lists online quote pound limits, but no source we read gives a weight limit, so ask your surgeon. [Compression garments after a mommy makeover](/blog/mommy-makeover-compression-garments-guide), detailed in our guide, support this phase by reducing swelling and providing comfort.

### Weeks 2-6: build endurance with low-impact cardio

If cleared at your follow-up, extend your activity gradually. Your energy levels should improve, but don't mistake feeling better for being fully healed.

Increase your walking time gradually. Try shaded paths in the morning for motivation and cooler temperatures. Maintain a conversational pace: if you can't talk comfortably, slow down.

Ankle circles keep blood moving in your lower extremities. If your surgeon approves seated leg lifts, sit in a chair, slowly extend one leg, then lower it, as many times as your surgeon directs.

A stationary bike offers low-impact cardio when used correctly and when your surgeon clears it. Set resistance to low. Keep your heart rate moderate; you should feel slightly warm but not breathless.

Avoid twisting motions or upper-body weights during this phase. [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/mommy-makeovers) says surgeons often recommend no heavy lifting or strenuous exercise for the first 4 to 6 weeks.

### Week 6 onward: progressive strength building

Surgeon approval at your follow-up unlocks more variety. Wu says running and lifting are avoided for 6 weeks after a tummy tuck, and [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar) says heavy lifting, strenuous exercise and stretching wait until your provider clears them, usually 4 to 8 weeks after a tummy tuck. Your combination of procedures decides which applies to you.

With your surgeon's clearance, light weights enter the picture. Start with dumbbells well below what you used before surgery for leg exercises like squats and lunges. The Aesthetic Society advises easing back into your normal routine gradually rather than all at once.

Resistance bands add lower-body work. Seated leg presses against a band build quad strength. Side steps with a band around your ankles activate glutes. Ask your surgeon how much to do, and focus on controlled movements rather than speed.

Upper body work begins cautiously. Light bicep curls and shoulder raises help rebuild arm strength. Avoid chest exercises or anything requiring you to lie flat and press weight upward until your surgeon clears them.

Gentle yoga poses suit this stage, once stretching is cleared, when modified appropriately. Child's pose stretches your back. Cat-cow movements, performed gently with minimal range, mobilize your spine. Seated forward folds stretch hamstrings while keeping core engagement minimal. Avoid any deep twists or poses requiring significant core activation.

Running and higher-intensity cardio come with clearance. Start with intervals of jogging and walking rather than sustained running, and build duration over several weeks.

### Around 3 months: return to normal activities

[Frank Campanile, MD, on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/what-to-expect-during-your-mommy-makeover-recovery), says that around 3 months you can usually return to your normal activities, and that it can take 6 months to a year to see your final mommy makeover results. Full gym access becomes possible with your surgeon's clearance, though you'll ramp intensity gradually rather than jumping back to pre-surgery levels. Track your progress weekly.

## Core exercises after a tummy tuck: when can I work my abs after muscle repair?

Start core exercises after a tummy tuck only when your surgeon clears you. [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck) says a tummy tuck, in most cases, restores weakened or separated muscles, and no source we read gives one safe week for abdominal exercises after that repair: not ASPS, not The Aesthetic Society, not Cleveland Clinic. Clinic websites publish their own numbers, and they disagree.

Tummy tuck procedures usually tighten abdominal muscles, making core exercises after a tummy tuck a careful progression. Rushing this could strain the repair and compromise your results. The figures above cover strenuous exercise and heavy lifting in general, and none of those pages names sit-ups, crunches or planks. At your consultation, ask whether your plan includes [tummy tuck muscle repair](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#what-is-a-tummy-tuck) and how your surgeon decides when core work can start.

Once approved, these are common starting moves. No source we read sets reps or holds for them, so do only what your surgeon directs:

![Clothed illustration of four gentle core moves on an exercise mat: heel slide, glute bridge, dead bug and modified plank. A note says to do them only when your surgeon clears them, and to stop and call your surgeon for a bulge along the middle of your abdomen, sharp pain or pulling at the incision.](IMAGE:core-moves)

- **Pelvic tilts:** These work well seated or lying down. Gently rock your pelvis forward and back to activate deep core stabilizers.
- **Heel slides:** Lie on your back with knees bent. Slowly slide one heel along the floor until your leg extends, then draw it back, with no crunching.
- **Glute bridges:** From the same position, press through your heels to lift your hips, squeeze your glutes at the top, then lower slowly.
- **Dead bugs:** Extend opposite arm and leg slowly, with controlled breathing, while keeping your low back flat against the floor. The challenge comes from maintaining stability, not from crunching. Ask your surgeon before you try this one.
- **Modified planks:** Try planks on your knees rather than toes. Ask your surgeon before you try this one, and how long to hold it. Stop immediately if you feel pulling at your incision or bulging in your abdomen.

These movements are meant to work your transversus abdominis, a deep abdominal muscle.

Avoid traditional crunches, sit-ups, or any exercise that causes your abdomen to "dome" outward. This can mean too much pressure on a healing repair. Stop and call your surgeon for a bulge along the middle of your abdomen, sharp pain or pulling at the incision.

Learn more about tummy tuck healing in our [tummy tuck recovery guide](/blog/tummy-tuck-recovery-time-miami) and our post on [exercises after a tummy tuck](/blog/exercises-after-tummy-tuck-miami).

## When can I do chest and arm exercises after a breast lift or implants?

Chest and arm exercises come later than leg work. After a breast lift, [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/breast/breast-lift/aftercare-recovery) says no lifting, driving, laundry or cleaning for the first 2 weeks, and [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23298-breast-lift) says lifting and exercising may not be cleared for up to 6 weeks. After implants, [it says](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/11024-breast-augmentation) no heavy lifting for at least 4 weeks.

If you play sports, Cleveland Clinic says it may take up to 6 weeks after breast augmentation to return to them. Push-ups and chest presses come only when your surgeon clears them. For arms above your head, ask your surgeon: Cleveland Clinic says your team may ask you not to raise them after a breast lift. Read more on [push-ups after breast augmentation](/when-can-i-do-push-ups-after-breast-augmentation) and [breast lift recovery](/procedures/breast-lift-miami).

## How do I get fit before a mommy makeover?

Build a routine you can keep and reach a weight you can hold. [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/mommy-makeover/candidates) describes candidates as at their ideal body weight, and Nneamaka Nwubah, MD, [quoted by ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/mommy-makeovers-what-women-should-know-about-postpartum-surgical-procedures), says, "These are not weight loss surgeries." [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/mommy-makeover/preparing-your-procedure) says your surgeon may ask you to exercise at least 30 minutes a day, several days a week, before surgery.

Kristy Hamilton, MD, quoted by ASPS, tells patients to wait at least 6 months after delivery, and says that if you are still losing weight and toning up, "you want to push it as far as you can." Be close to your goal weight for 6 to 12 months before a tummy tuck ([Paul Vitenas, MD, on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/five-things-to-consider-before-getting-a-tummy-tuck)). ASPS adds that a tummy tuck is not a substitute for weight loss or an appropriate exercise program. Wondering if you're ready? Read [who is a good candidate for a mommy makeover](/procedures/mommy-makeover-miami#candidate).

## Safe workouts post mommy makeover: Miami-specific tips

No source we read says heat or humidity changes when you can exercise or how you heal, so treat these as comfort tips for South Florida, not medical rules.

<Figure src="IMAGE:walking" alt="A woman in a loose long-sleeve top and soft joggers walks slowly along a shaded path in early morning light, holding a water bottle." width="1600" height="1067" caption="Model shown. Not a patient." />

- **Timing matters:** Schedule outdoor walks for early morning or evening, when temperatures stay cooler. Avoid peak sun hours.
- **Hydration:** Wu calls adequate hydration and walking important for lowering the risk of blood clots. No source we read gives a daily amount. Carry water with you and sip frequently rather than gulping large amounts at once.
- **Indoor alternatives:** Stationary bikes and yoga studios with air conditioning let you maintain your routine without heat exposure.
- **Breathable fabrics:** Lightweight, moisture-wicking clothing keeps you comfortable. Avoid tight waistbands that press on abdominal incisions.
- **Sun:** Cleveland Clinic says it's extremely important to stay out of the sun as your wound heals. It advises sunscreen with SPF 30 on the scar for 12 to 18 months after a tummy tuck if you must be in the sun.

## Common mistakes to avoid in mommy makeover recovery exercises

Eagerness to return to normal leads to setbacks. Campanile puts it simply: "don't push your body to do too much too soon."

- **Ignoring clearance requirements:** This creates increased risk of prolonged swelling and complications. Your surgeon's approval isn't arbitrary. It's based on how your body is healing internally, and what looks healed outside may still need time inside.
- **Rushing core work:** Crunches or sit-ups before your surgeon clears them strain muscle repairs. The wait feels long, but protecting your results is worth it.
- **High-impact activities too early:** Running, jumping or HIIT workouts before you are cleared stress incisions. Your tissues need time to regain strength before absorbing impact forces.
- **Skipping rest days:** Even when you feel good, your body needs recovery time. Alternate activity days with rest days, especially in early weeks.
- **Comparing your progress to others:** Every body heals differently based on age, procedure scope, overall health and genetics. Focus on your own plan.

Listen to your body and log your sessions for follow-up discussions. Notes about what felt good and what caused discomfort help your surgeon guide your progression.

## Signs to watch during exercise recovery: are you doing too much?

Pain that lasts after you rest, new or worse swelling and any change at an incision mean stop and call your surgeon. The Aesthetic Society says to call immediately if swelling, pain, redness, drainage or bleeding increases. Mild muscle soreness after new activities is normal. Sharp, stabbing or burning pain is not.

Pause your activity and call your surgeon right away if you notice any of these, from MedlinePlus on [surgical wounds](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000738.htm) and [deep vein clots](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000156.htm):

- More redness, pain, swelling or bleeding at the incision
- Drainage that increases or turns thick, tan, green or yellow, or smells bad
- A temperature above 100°F for more than 4 hours
- One leg or arm swollen, painful, warm or red

Go to the emergency room or call 911 for chest pain, coughing up blood, difficulty breathing or fainting. MedlinePlus lists these as emergency signs with a deep vein clot, and The Aesthetic Society names shortness of breath and chest pains as red flags after a mommy makeover. When in doubt, reduce intensity and contact your surgeon.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long after a mommy makeover can I lift weights?

Not for several weeks. The Aesthetic Society advises no heavy lifting for 4 to 6 weeks after a mommy makeover and waiting 6 weeks for strenuous activities. Cleveland Clinic says heavy lifting after a tummy tuck usually waits 4 to 8 weeks. Start well below your old weights once your surgeon clears you.

### How long should I wait before resuming high-impact activities?

Plan on at least 6 weeks for running or jumping: Cindy Wu, MD, on the ASPS website, says running and lifting are avoided for 6 weeks after a tummy tuck. Full clearance from your surgeon comes first, and breast surgery adds its own limits.

### When can I start core exercises after a tummy tuck in a mommy makeover?

Only when your surgeon clears you, because a tummy tuck usually repairs the abdominal muscles and no source we read gives one safe week for everyone. That includes sit-ups and crunches. Begin with gentle movements like pelvic tilts or heel slides, and stop if you see a bulge along the middle of your abdomen or feel pulling at the incision.

### Can I swim after my mommy makeover?

Only once your incisions have fully closed and your surgeon clears you. MedlinePlus says soaking a wound could cause it to reopen or become infected, so showers come before pools or the ocean. Swimming laps is also exercise, so The Aesthetic Society's 6 weeks for strenuous activities applies too. Start with shallow water and gentle movements.

### How long after a breast lift can I work out?

Cleveland Clinic says you may not be cleared for vigorous activities, such as lifting things and exercising, for up to 6 weeks after a breast lift. It says many women go back to gentle activities such as walking about a week afterward. Chest exercises such as push-ups come last, when your surgeon clears them.

### How do I maintain my results long-term after recovery?

Consistent exercise and a stable weight help keep the result. ASPS says the outcome of a tummy tuck can be greatly diminished by significant fluctuations in your weight, and [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/procedures/11017-tummy-tuck) says you may need to adjust your lifestyle to maintain the results. Establishing healthy habits during recovery makes long-term maintenance easier.
