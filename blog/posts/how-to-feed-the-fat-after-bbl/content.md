## Do you need to feed the fat after a BBL?

No. We found no study in people showing that any food, a high-fat diet or extra calories makes grafted fat survive. What food can do is help you heal, so eat enough, get your protein and keep your weight steady. You don't need to overeat to protect your result.

## Does eating fat help grafted fat survive?

Not that anyone has shown in people. [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/buttock-enhancement/procedure) says patients retain 60% to 80% of the transferred fat on average, in some cases more and in others less. A plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS says [20% to 40% of the injected fat](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/seven-things-you-need-to-know-about-a-brazilian-butt-lift) is absorbed by the body during healing. The ASPS articles we read on BBL recovery don't mention diet at all.

The closest evidence comes from mice. In a [2022 study in Frontiers in Cell and Developmental Biology](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8968084/), 54 mice with fat grafts ate a normal, high-carbohydrate or high-fat diet for 1 month. Both the high-fat group, on 60% of calories from fat, and the high-carbohydrate group had more graft volume at 90 days than the normal-diet group. The authors themselves say "further clinical research is warranted." A mouse on a lab diet tells you little about a woman recovering at home.

We also searched PubMed on November 4, 2026, for fat-grafting papers that mention diet or nutrition. It returned 20 results, and none tested a diet in patients.

What the sources do link to fat survival is pressure. [A plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/six-things-you-need-to-know-about-recovering-from-a-brazilian-butt-lift) calls the fat "very susceptible to pressure necrosis" while it establishes a blood supply, and the same article says the amount that takes depends on following recovery guidelines. So keeping pressure off is the part of recovery the sources actually tie to the graft. Here is [how long to stay off your buttocks](/how-long-after-bbl-can-i-sit).

What grafted fat needs:

- A new blood supply
- Time without pressure on it
- A body with enough protein and calories to heal

No food is shown to make grafted fat survive in people.

## What should you eat after a BBL?

Regular meals built around protein, with fruit, vegetables, whole grains and plenty of fluids. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23308-brazilian-butt-lift) says recovery may take up to 6 months, and that during this time you may have to eat a high-protein diet to assist your healing.

How much protein? No source gives a BBL number. A [2009 review in ePlasty](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2642618/) says a healthy adult needs about 0.8 g per kilogram of body weight a day, and that patients under the stress of surgery or a wound need about 1.5 g. At 150 pounds (68 kg), that is about 54 g and about 102 g a day. The review covers wound healing in general, including far larger wounds than a BBL, so treat this as a range to discuss, not a target. [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/002467.htm) notes that a 2,000-calorie diet could include about 100 g.

How many calories? We found no calorie target for a BBL. The same review says metabolic rate rises 20% after elective surgery, which is a reason not to diet, not a number to eat to. Your surgeon or a dietitian can set your target, especially if you have a medical condition or have had weight-loss surgery.

A few things that help day to day, which come from common practice, not studies:

- **Cook ahead.** Stock the freezer before surgery, because you won't feel like standing at a stove.
- **Go small if you're not hungry.** Smaller meals are easier when your appetite is low.
- **Eat standing, or on your pillow.** [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/butt-lift/aftercare-recovery) says many surgeons who restrict sitting early on make room for eating, but a counter works too.

Fiber and fluids matter for a practical reason. MedlinePlus says some medicines can make you constipated, and advises 8 to 10 cups (2 to 2.5 L) of liquids a day, mainly water, along with more fiber. It says to contact a provider if you haven't had a bowel movement in 3 days. Constipation is also hard on a sitting limit.

## What should you avoid after a BBL?

Crash diets, alcohol while you're on medication, smoking and supplements your surgeon hasn't cleared. No source we read names a food that destroys grafted fat, so you don't need a long list of forbidden foods.

- **Crash dieting.** You need protein and calories to heal, and ASPS says transferred fat can shrink as you lose weight.
- **Alcohol with your medication.** The [National Institute on Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism](https://www.niaaa.nih.gov/publications/brochures-and-fact-sheets/harmful-interactions-mixing-alcohol-with-medicines) warns that alcohol with opioid pain medicines can cause drowsiness, dizziness, slowed or difficult breathing and a higher risk of overdose, and lists liver damage with acetaminophen. Ask your surgeon when it is safe to drink.
- **Smoking.** The Aesthetic Society says being in good health, not smoking and having help after surgery will increase your healing and improve your results.
- **Anything you haven't cleared.** Show your surgeon every supplement and medication you take. [ASPS's preparation page](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/buttock-enhancement/preparation) says to avoid aspirin and certain anti-inflammatory drugs because they can increase bleeding. The ePlasty review says zinc beyond correcting a deficiency has no added benefit for wound healing, and MedlinePlus says vitamin C above 2,000 mg a day isn't recommended.
- **Eating extra on purpose.** No source we read supports it, and any weight you gain doesn't go only to the graft.

## What should you do about your weight after a BBL?

Keep it steady. Cleveland Clinic says that to preserve the effects of a BBL, you should maintain a consistent weight. ASPS says your results may change as the transferred fat can grow or shrink, just as any other fat in your body.

That cuts both ways. Losing a lot can shrink the result, and gaining a lot adds fat everywhere. A plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS says the acute risk of losing grafted fat subsides around month 3, but nothing we read measures diet in that window, so treat these months as sensible eating and not as a graft treatment.

If you take a weight-loss medication such as a GLP-1, or plan to start one, tell your surgeon. We didn't find BBL-specific guidance on them.

## Frequently asked questions

### What foods help grafted fat survive after a BBL?

None that has been shown in people. We found one mouse study in which a high-fat diet improved graft retention, and its authors say clinical research is still needed. Eat protein and enough calories to heal, keep your weight steady, and keep pressure off the grafted area, which is what the sources do link to fat survival.

### Can I diet or restrict calories after a BBL?

It's better not to diet through recovery. Cleveland Clinic says you may have to eat a high-protein diet to assist healing, and to keep a consistent weight to preserve your result. ASPS says transferred fat can shrink as you lose weight. If you want to lose weight later, ask your surgeon when and how.

### How long do I need to feed the fat after a BBL?

You don't need to feed the fat, so there is no end date to reach. No source sets a diet period. A plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS says the acute risk of losing grafted fat subsides around month 3, and Cleveland Clinic ties its high-protein advice to a recovery of up to 6 months.

### Does alcohol kill fat cells after a BBL?

No source we read says that. The clearer concern is your medication. The National Institute on Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism warns that alcohol with opioid pain medicines can cause drowsiness, dizziness, slowed or difficult breathing and a higher risk of overdose. Ask your surgeon when it is safe to drink again.

### What vitamins should I take after BBL surgery?

No source we read recommends a particular vitamin for BBL results. MedlinePlus says vitamin C helps heal wounds and zinc plays a role in wound healing, and both come from food. A 2009 review says zinc beyond correcting a deficiency has no added benefit for healing. Ask your surgeon before you take any supplement.

### How much water should I drink after a BBL?

No source we read ties water to fat survival or gives a BBL target. MedlinePlus advises 8 to 10 cups (2 to 2.5 L) of liquids a day, mainly water, when you are dealing with constipation. That is a useful starting point. If your surgeon has given you a fluid limit, follow that.

### Will gaining weight make my BBL bigger?

It can change your result, but not in a controlled way. ASPS says the transferred fat can grow or shrink as you gain or lose weight, just as any other fat in your body, so weight you gain goes elsewhere too. No source we read recommends gaining on purpose, and Cleveland Clinic says to maintain a consistent weight.
