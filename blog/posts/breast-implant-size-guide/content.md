## What size breast implant should I get?

Breast implants in the 300 to 400 cc range are the most commonly chosen, going by [ASPS's 2025 survey of member surgeons](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/documents/news/statistics/2025/plastic-surgery-statistics-report-2025.pdf). That tells you what is common, not what fits you: the right volume and profile depend on the width of your breast, how much tissue covers the implant and the look you want.

## Your Breast Implant Size Guide: Understanding How Sizing Works

Breast implants are measured in cubic centimeters (cc) rather than cup sizes, because a volume is a fixed number and a cup is not. Three measurements describe an implant. Volume is how much it holds, diameter (also called base width) is how wide it sits on your chest wall, and projection is how far forward it comes.

![Diagram of low, moderate and high profile breast implants at the same 300 cc volume: as the profile rises, base width narrows and projection increases.](IMAGE:geometry)

Profile is the word that ties these together. [An article on the ASPS website by Dr. Cameron Craven](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/all-about-breast-implant-profiles) describes low, moderate, moderate plus, high and ultra-high profiles, and explains that implants of the same cc volume with different widths produce different levels of projection. So the same cc volume can create a different silhouette depending on the profile you choose. At the same 300 cc volume, base width goes from wider to narrower and projection from lower to higher as the profile rises. Knowing this helps you say clearly what you want at your consultation.

A moderate profile sits on a wider base for a gentler slope, and that article says moderate implants tend to give the most natural-looking results. Higher profiles narrow the base and bring the implant forward, for more fullness at the top. The range of sizes is wide too: [Mentor's MemoryGel catalog](https://www.jnjmedtech.com/es-419/product/mentor/mentor-memorygel), published by J&J MedTech, lists smooth round implants from 125 cc to 800 cc. Other makers' ranges differ.

That is the foundation: volume, width and projection, matched to your body and your routine.

## What Factors Affect Choosing Breast Implant Size?

Your body frame sets the stage, and one measurement comes up in every sizing system we read: the width of each breast. Narrow chests often pair with high profile implants: Dr. Craven's article says high profile implants are often ideal for petite women with a narrow chest wall, and low profile implants for women with wider chests. That is one surgeon's rule of thumb. Your own measurements come first.

Breast base width (the width of each breast, not the chest) guides the implant's diameter, to help avoid a mismatch with your tissue. For scale, Mentor's 300 to 500 cc smooth round implants run from 11.1 to 14.1 cm across in the two profiles compared below.

![Card titled Breast Implant Size Selection: What Surgeons Measure, with a front-view line drawing of a torso in a plain high-neck top and two dashed gold lines, one across the width of one breast and one running down the chest from the collar. Breast base width guides the implant's diameter. Skin stretch shows how much room the skin gives. Nipple to breast fold is measured under stretch. Tissue cover is how much hides the implant. Your goal is the look you want. Sources: Tebbetts 2002 and Bletsis and van der Lei 2025.](IMAGE:selection)

Surgeons who use these systems measure base width, skin stretch and the distance from the nipple to the breast fold, and those numbers feed into the implant's diameter and volume. Ask which measurements your surgeon takes.

Existing breast tissue plays a crucial role in your final result. Fuller natural tissue hides an implant more, and thin tissue shows more. [Sientra's patient labeling](https://sientra.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/MDC-0010-R6-Sientra-Patient-Educational-Brochure-Augmentation-1.pdf) warns that if skin is stretched too thin over the implant, you may be able to feel or see its edges. [A 2002 paper on implant selection (Tebbetts)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11964998/) lists what can go wrong when a size doesn't suit your tissue: skin stretch and thinning, implant edges you can see or feel, and [visible rippling and bottoming out](/blog/breast-implant-rippling-bottoming-out).

Lifestyle matters too. If you run, lift or swim, tell your surgeon, and read [when each kind of exercise comes back after surgery](/blog/when-can-you-work-out-after-breast-augmentation). We found no study that gives a size for a given sport, so this is a judgment the two of you make together.

Aesthetic goals round it out, whether you prefer a subtle change or fuller curves. Trends lean smaller right now: in the same ASPS survey, 36% of member surgeons saw patients choosing smaller implants than in previous years, 12% saw a shift to larger ones and 52% saw no change. It is worth getting right the first time. [The FDA says](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/what-know-about-breast-implants) many patients have more operations to change implant size. In Sientra's 10-year study, the most common reason implants came out after a first augmentation was the patient asking for a different size or style: 49.1% of the implants removed.

## Breast Augmentation Implant Chart: Sizes and Cup Increases

While cup sizes vary by band size and brand, approximate increases still make a helpful breast augmentation implant chart.

![Chart of implant volume and estimated cup change: 200–250 cc about +1 cup, 250–300 cc about +1 to +1.5, 300–350 cc about +1.5 to +2, 350–400 cc about +2, 400–500 cc about +2 to +3. Estimates vary by band size, breast width, tissue elasticity and implant profile.](IMAGE:cc-chart)

[A 2017 study in Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery (King and colleagues)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28445356/) measured five cup sizes from three bra brands and looked at 79 women who had augmentation. The step between cups averaged 135 cc in the lab and about 138 cc in the patients, so the authors advised that 130 to 150 cc equals one cup size: 130 cc on narrower bands and 150 cc on wider ones. Earlier reports used 100 cc. Here is the breast implant size chart, CC to cup size, from that rule:

| Cup sizes added | Narrower band: about 130 cc per cup | Wider band: about 150 cc per cup |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 cup | about 130 cc | about 150 cc |
| 2 cups | about 260 cc | about 300 cc |
| 3 cups | about 390 cc | about 450 cc |
| 4 cups | about 520 cc | about 600 cc |

The table is that study's figure multiplied out, per breast. Treat it as a rough rule. The same study concluded that "there is no standardization within the bra manufacturing industry", so a D in one brand is not a D in another. It measured five cup sizes in one group of patients. The calculator above uses the same figures, with 130 cc at band 32 and below and 150 cc at band 38 and above. Those band cutoffs and the width of the range it shows are our reading of the study, not the study's own numbers.

If you came here for the kitchen conversion, a US measuring cup is about 0.24 liters, or 240 cc ([NIST](https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/approximate-conversions-us-customary-measures-metric)). That has nothing to do with a bra cup.

Results depend heavily on skin elasticity and existing tissue. The same implant "will look completely different on two different women with different chest types", Dr. Craven writes. Working closely with your surgeon keeps expectations realistic.

## Popular Implant Sizes in CC for Different Frames

Implant volumes from 300 to 400 cc remain the most used, and by the rule above they add about 2 to 3 cup sizes. The survey shows what surgeons use, not what suits a particular frame. No published chart we read gives a volume by frame, so be careful with any that does.

If you are petite with a narrow chest wall, you may have less width to work with. In the sizing systems we read, width limits size and height does not come into it. A narrower breast base points to a narrower implant, and a higher profile holds more volume at the same width: in Mentor's catalog, a 400 cc high profile implant (12.2 cm) is about as wide as a 300 cc moderate plus (12.0 cm).

## 300cc, 400cc and 500cc Implants: Cup Size and Dimensions

A 300cc implant adds about 2 cup sizes, a 400cc about 3 and a 500cc about 3.5, by the 130 to 150 cc rule. What they look like depends on how wide they are. This table uses one maker's published sizes for smooth round silicone gel implants, from [Mentor's catalog](https://www.jnjmedtech.com/system/files/pdf/2018_Cat%C3%A1logo%20para%20HCP_Xtra.pdf), to show how much a profile changes the shape. Other makers' dimensions differ, and this is not a statement of which implants we use.

| Volume | Rough cup sizes added | Moderate Plus: width × projection | High Profile: width × projection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 250 cc | a little under 2 | 11.3 × 3.4 cm | 10.5 × 4.3 cm |
| 300 cc | about 2 | 12.0 × 3.6 cm | 11.1 × 4.5 cm |
| 350 cc | about 2.5 | 12.5 × 3.9 cm | 11.7 × 4.8 cm |
| 400 cc | about 3 | 13.1 × 4.0 cm | 12.2 × 5.0 cm |
| 450 cc | about 3 to 3.5 | 13.6 × 4.2 cm | 12.8 × 5.1 cm |
| 500 cc | about 3.5 | 14.1 × 4.3 cm | 13.2 × 5.3 cm |

### What cup size is a 300cc implant?

There is no fixed cup. A 300cc implant adds about 2 cup sizes to what you have now, because 300 divided by 130 to 150 cc is a little over 2. An A cup would move toward a C, if the rule holds for your bras. It sits at the bottom of the 300 to 400 cc range surgeons report using most, and it is 11.1 to 12.0 cm wide in these two profiles.

### What cup size is a 400cc implant?

About 3 cup sizes on the same rule, so a B cup would move toward a DD. It sits at the top of the range surgeons report using most. At 12.2 to 13.1 cm wide, it needs more breast width than a 300cc implant in the same profile.

### What cup size is a 500cc implant?

About 3.5 cup sizes. That is above the range most surgeons in ASPS's survey ranked highest. At 13.2 to 14.1 cm wide, the question for your surgeon is whether your breast width and skin can carry it, since the 2002 paper links a size that doesn't suit your tissue with skin stretch, thinning and visible rippling.

## Can you choose breast implant size based on height and weight?

Not directly. We found no study that sets an implant's cc by height and weight. What does guide it is your breast and your tissue. The 2002 Tebbetts system relies on three tissue measurements: the base width of the breast tissue, how far the skin stretches, and the distance from the nipple to the breast fold under stretch. [A 2025 paper by Bletsis and van der Lei](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40230475/) records the distance from the top of the breastbone to the nipple, the base width of the breast and the tissue covering the implant. The 2017 cup study ties cc per cup to band width, which is why the calculator above doesn't ask for your height or weight. Ask which measurements your surgeon takes.

Height and weight can matter indirectly, through your frame and your tissue. Two women of the same height and weight can have very different breast widths and skin. Charts that promise an ideal size for your height and weight don't cite a source we could find.

Weight does come up for a different reason: safety and stability. Our guide to the [BMI range for breast augmentation](/bmi-calculator#breast-augmentation) covers that, and if you are above the range, it suggests choosing your implant at the weight you'll keep.

## Breast Implant Profile Types: Comparing High vs. Moderate

At the same volume, a high profile implant is narrower and comes further forward, and a moderate profile is wider and flatter. The profile you choose affects how your result looks from every angle.

Moderate profiles suit a softer blend. Their wider bases spread volume across the chest, with less projection. Dr. Craven's article says they tend to yield the most natural-looking results.

High profiles sit on narrower bases and project forward for upper fullness. The article says they give the fullest and most rounded results, which may look less natural on some patients, and that they are often ideal for petite women with a narrow chest wall.

Here's how they compare:

![Comparison card of moderate and high profile breast implants. Moderate: wider base, medium projection, natural slope. High: narrower base, more projection, upper-pole fullness.](IMAGE:profile-compare)

| | Moderate Plus | High Profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base width | Wider | Narrower |
| Projection | Less | More |
| 400 cc in Mentor's catalog | 13.1 cm wide, 4.0 cm forward | 12.2 cm wide, 5.0 cm forward |
| Look, from Dr. Craven's article (moderate profile, not moderate plus) | Tends to be the most natural-looking | Fullest and most rounded |

Across the sizes in the table above, the high profile is about 1 cm narrower and about 1 cm further forward. That same article also says moderate implants are typically ideal for smaller or narrower chests, so a narrow chest alone doesn't pick the profile. Matching the profile to your measurements is what counts, and your surgeon recommends the profile that suits your anatomy and goals. Profile is a separate choice from placement, which our comparison of [implants over or under the muscle](/blog/breast-implants-over-or-under-the-muscle) covers.

## Consultation Tips and Miami Considerations

Some practices offer sizers or 3D simulation; ask whether yours does. Sizers are trial implants worn in a bra, and the 2025 paper describes previewing the result with both. [A 2024 paper in Aesthetic Surgery Journal](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38377409/) calls the Vectra XT 3D simulation program a valuable adjunct, warns that relying on it solely may lead to inaccuracies, and says its results "underline the superiority of clinical judgment to simulation programs". So a preview can help, and your measurements and your surgeon's judgment decide.

Bring photos of looks you like and looks you don't. Discuss your lifestyle openly, including exercise habits and clothing preferences. Ask to see your breast base width and what it means for the implant's diameter. [The FDA suggests](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/breast-implants/breast-implant-surgery) asking a surgeon, "What is your reoperation rate?" Before surgery, your provider must also review the FDA-required patient decision checklist with you, and you must have the chance to initial and sign it.

In South Florida the sun is year-round, so new scars need real protection. [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000273.htm), writing about cosmetic breast surgery in general, says to use sunblock of SPF 30 or higher whenever you are out in the sun. Ocean, bay and pool water wait at least about a month, [an article on the ASPS website by Dr. Cindy Wu](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/considering-breast-augmentation-for-beach-season-heres-when-you-should-plan-your-surgery) says, and until your incisions are fully closed and your surgeon agrees, as [our swimming guide explains](/how-long-after-breast-augmentation-can-i-swim). ## Frequently asked questions

### How do implant profiles affect the final breast shape?

Profile sets how far the implant comes forward and how wide it sits. Low profiles are flatter, moderate profiles give a gentler slope and high profiles give the fullest, roundest look. At the same volume, Mentor's high profile is about 1 cm narrower and 1 cm further forward than its moderate plus. Discuss your anatomy with your surgeon to find the profile that fits your frame.

### What factors should I consider when choosing breast implant size?

Start with the width of each breast, how much tissue covers the implant, your overall frame and your lifestyle. Then add the look you want, subtle or full. Think long term too: in Sientra's 10-year study, wanting a different size or style was the most common reason implants came out after a first augmentation.

### How does chest width influence the choice of implant size?

It is really breast width that counts. The base width of each breast guides the implant's diameter, and a wider breast can take a wider implant. Narrow chests often suit narrower, higher profile implants, which project forward instead of spreading out. A mismatch with your tissue can show up as rippling or implant edges showing through thin tissue.

### What are the benefits of choosing a moderate profile implant?

A moderate profile sits on a wider base, so it gives a gentler slope with less forward projection. An article on the ASPS website says moderate profiles tend to give the most natural-looking results. They suit women who prefer a softer look, though how an implant blends always depends on your tissue and the width of your breast.

### Are high profile implants riskier than moderate ones?

We found no study comparing complication rates by profile, so we can't tell you one is riskier. What the literature does warn about is a mismatch: an implant that doesn't suit your tissue can lead to skin stretch, visible rippling and bottoming out. Your surgeon matches the profile to your measurements.

### What is the most common breast implant size?

In ASPS's 2025 survey, most member surgeons ranked 300 to 400 cc among the implants they use most, with 200 to 300 cc second. By the 130 to 150 cc rule, 300 to 400 cc adds about 2 to 3 cup sizes. The right size for you still depends on your anatomy and goals.

### How accurate are breast implant size calculators?

They are rough. A band-and-cup calculator can only use your bra size, and cups vary between brands. It can't measure your breast width, skin or tissue, which are what published sizing systems use. The study behind ours looked at augmentation patients, so it does not apply after a mastectomy, when sizing is a different conversation with your reconstructive surgeon.

### How many cc is a cup size?

About 130 to 150 cc for one cup size, according to a 2017 study that measured bras from three brands: roughly 130 cc on narrower bands and 150 cc on wider ones. It is a rough rule, because the study also found no standardization between bra makers. Treat any single number as a starting point for your consultation.
