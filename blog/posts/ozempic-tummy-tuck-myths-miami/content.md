## Can you get a tummy tuck after Ozempic?

Yes, when your weight has stopped changing and loose skin is what's left. A tummy tuck is not a way to keep losing weight: [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck) says it is "not a substitute for weight loss" and advises anyone planning substantial weight loss to postpone it, and it [describes a good candidate](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck/candidates) as physically healthy and at a stable weight. Your surgeon decides at consultation, and the page explains [who is a good candidate for a tummy tuck](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#candidate).

Taking a GLP-1 doesn't rule you out. An [article on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/move-over-mommy-makeover-the-ozempic-makeover-may-be-the-hottest-new-trend) says you may continue the medication to maintain your weight, and that the time to consider surgery is when you are at or near your goal weight. It also says you will need to stop the drug before surgery, though the anesthesia guidance below suggests most patients continue. In this post, GLP-1 means the whole drug family, including [Ozempic, Wegovy, Saxenda and Trulicity](https://www.asahq.org/about-asa/newsroom/news-releases/2024/10/new-multi-society-glp-1-guidance), the drugs many people call weight loss jabs.

## Why does GLP-1 weight loss leave loose belly skin?

Because fast weight loss doesn't give skin time to adjust. That ASPS website article says gradual loss gives skin and other tissues time to adjust and rapid loss doesn't, which can leave loose skin, especially around the arms, legs and belly. Kristy Hamilton, MD, quoted there, says this excess skin "cannot be 'exercised' away."

Liposuction doesn't solve it either. A video on [ASPS's tummy tuck page](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck) says "your skin can only contract so much with the liposuction procedure." So the question is mostly skin or fat. Read the comparison of [tummy tuck vs liposuction](/blog/tummy-tuck-vs-liposuction), or, if fat is your main concern, [liposuction after Ozempic](/blog/liposuction-recovery-ozempic-miami).

## Tummy tuck after weight loss: how long should you wait?

Until you have been close to your goal weight for 6 to 12 months, says [Paul Vitenas, MD, on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/five-things-to-consider-before-getting-a-tummy-tuck), because gaining or losing more can undo the shape surgery creates. Hamilton puts it this way: be at or near the end of your weight loss, or you may look "a little 'loose' again."

Research on semaglutide adds a different clock, counted from when you start the drug rather than from when you reach your goal. A [2025 review of 12 studies by Garbaccio and colleagues](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40835770/) suggests weight stabilizes after 8 to 12 months of use, at which point surgery may be appropriate, though it urges caution because of weight regain after stopping.

The drug adds two more timing points, from the [2024 guidance by the American Society of Anesthesiologists and four other societies](https://www.asahq.org/about-asa/newsroom/news-releases/2024/10/new-multi-society-glp-1-guidance):

- **Your dose has stopped rising.** That phase typically lasts 4 to 8 weeks, and elective surgery should wait until it has passed.
- **Your stomach has settled.** Nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, shortness of breath or constipation should clear before elective surgery.

![Timing card on warm stone titled When a tummy tuck fits after a GLP-1. Weight: close to your goal for 6 to 12 months. Dose: not while it is still rising, a phase that typically lasts 4 to 8 weeks. Stomach: nausea, vomiting, pain or constipation cleared first. The drug: agree a plan with your surgeon, anesthesia team and prescriber.](IMAGE:timing)

## Do you have to stop Ozempic before a tummy tuck?

Not always, and the people who advise on this disagree, so never decide alone. These drugs slow digestion, so food can stay in your stomach, and under anesthesia it can come up into your lungs, which can cause a serious, potentially fatal pneumonia. That risk is why your surgeon, anesthesia team and prescriber need to agree a plan.

| Who | What they say | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [American Society of Anesthesiologists and four other societies](https://www.asahq.org/about-asa/newsroom/news-releases/2024/10/new-multi-society-glp-1-guidance) | Suggests most patients continue. Those at highest risk of stomach side effects should follow a liquid diet for 24 hours before surgery, or other measures | October 2024 |
| [The same guidance, as published](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11666732/) | If a hold is chosen, how long is unknown. It suggests the day of surgery for daily drugs and a week before for weekly ones | October 2024 |
| [Anna Steve, MD, quoted on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/understanding-how-medications-interact-with-anesthesia-and-when-to-stop-before-plastic-surgery) | Most anesthesia doctors require stopping at least 2 to 3 weeks before general anesthesia | June 2024 |
| Kristy Hamilton, MD, quoted on the ASPS website | Asks patients to stop one month ahead, so they can work on their nutrition | March 2025 |

Don't stop or restart on your own. The guidance warns that stopping has its own risks, such as higher blood sugar if you take the drug for diabetes. That guidance is about anesthesia, not healing: Hamilton's reason for a pause is nutrition, and the studies further down look at wounds.

Tell your team:

- **Which drug and dose,** and whether you take it weekly or daily. The guidance names higher doses and weekly dosing as raising the risk of stomach problems.
- **When your dose last went up,** and the date of your last dose.
- **Any stomach symptoms,** such as nausea, vomiting, belly pain or constipation.
- **Other conditions that slow your stomach,** such as gastroparesis.
- **How much you are eating,** including protein.

Sources: multi-society guidance, 2024; ASPS website article, 2025.](IMAGE:tell-your-team)

## What is recovery like after a tummy tuck if you've used a GLP-1?

No source we read gives a separate timeline for GLP-1 users, so start from [tummy tuck recovery, week by week](/how-long-to-recover-tummy-tuck) and ask your surgeon what may change. Two things may differ, and the second is still unsettled.

The first is food. The ASPS website article says that without enough calories and food, these drugs can deplete vitamins, minerals and nutrients needed for healing. Both surgeons it quotes recommend plenty of protein, and [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar) suggests asking your provider or a registered dietitian how to get the nutrition you need.

The second is wound healing, where studies conflict:

| Study | Who it looked at | What it found |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Lee, Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery, 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41364427/) | Matched tummy tuck patients in a health records database | Wound problems within 30 days: 9.8% on a GLP-1, 3.6% not on one |
| [Lewis, Aesthetic Surgery Journal, 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39665435/) | Body contouring after bariatric surgery, no diabetes | Wounds reopening: 5.19% on semaglutide, 2.78% not on it |
| [Bruno, Aesthetic Plastic Surgery, 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42286330/) | 80 patients, split into four groups, having a tummy tuck with liposuction | Complications: 45% kept taking semaglutide, 30% stopped 2 weeks before, 10% stopped 4 weeks before, 10% never took it |
| [Liang, Aesthetic Surgery Journal, 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39960861/) | 72 GLP-1 users and 143 non-users having body contouring at one institution | Similar outcomes, and stopping time did not predict wound problems |
| [Albanese, Aesthetic Plastic Surgery, 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42716982/) | 196 patients having a tummy tuck with liposuction | No significant difference, though the authors say this doesn't prove the two are equal |

These studies look back at past records, and none can prove the drug caused the difference. A [2026 study by Boukind and colleagues](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41403207/) also linked recent GLP-1 use to thick, raised scars, with a risk 1.79 times that of non-users. Ask your surgeon how they read this evidence.

Restarting is also a question for your surgeon and prescriber, because no source we read sets a date. Anna Steve, MD, notes that some surgeons ask patients to hold off after surgery so there is enough nutrition for wound healing, and a [2026 study of 91 women by Ahmedov and colleagues](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42892081/) linked early restarting to more short-term complications.

## Tummy tuck, fleur-de-lis or liposuction after big weight loss?

It depends on how much loose skin you have and where it is. Your surgeon decides at the exam, and the page sets out [which tummy tuck suits which body](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#which-tummy-tuck).

- **Extended tummy tuck.** For a lot of excess, sagging skin on your abdomen and flanks, says [Samir Rao, MD, on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/understanding-the-different-types-of-tummy-tucks).
- **Fleur-de-lis.** [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar) says it is usually for a large amount of excess skin due to major weight loss or multiple pregnancies, and it adds a second vertical scar from your lower breastbone to your pubic bone.
- **Panniculectomy.** [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/procedures/11017-tummy-tuck) says it removes hanging skin and fat from the lower abdomen but doesn't tighten muscles, move your belly button or address fullness in your upper abdomen.

## Will your results last if you keep taking a GLP-1?

They can, if your weight stays steady. ASPS says a good result can be greatly diminished by significant weight fluctuations, and the ASPS website article warns that more significant weight loss after surgery can alter your result. No source we read measured how long results last in people who stay on the drug.

Stopping matters too. In the 2025 review, the two studies that followed people after they stopped semaglutide found they regained 62.7% and 74.3% of the weight they had lost, and its authors urge caution until more is known. So if you plan to come off the drug, say so before you set a surgery date. The surgeons in the ASPS website article recommend protein and, when your surgeon clears it, strength training to protect your muscle and your result.

## What are the risks to weigh?

A tummy tuck has a higher rate of major complications than other cosmetic surgery, and adding procedures raises it. In [an insurance database of 25,478 tummy tucks](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26505716/), major complications followed 3.1% of those done alone, 3.8% with liposuction, 4.3% with a breast procedure, 6.8% with another body-contouring procedure and 10.4% with liposuction and body contouring. The same study found higher risk at age 55 or older and with a BMI of 30 or more. Its data are from 2008 to 2013 and don't look at GLP-1 use.

If you want several areas treated, ask about staged surgery: Hamilton asks patients to choose up to two areas at a time. The page has more on [tummy tuck safety](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#safety).

If you are still planning, ASPS says [most health insurance plans do not cover a tummy tuck](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck/cost). You can compare [the five types of tummy tuck and their prices](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#pricing), which your surgeon confirms at consultation, and [financing is offered](/plastic-surgery-financing-miami).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I take Ozempic after a tummy tuck?

Ask your surgeon and the doctor who prescribes it, because no source we read sets a restart date. Anna Steve, MD, on the ASPS website, notes that some surgeons ask patients to hold off for a period after surgery so there is enough nutrition for wound healing. Once you restart, keep your weight steady: losing a lot more can loosen the skin again.

### Is it worth getting a tummy tuck after Ozempic weight loss?

It can be, when loose skin is what's left and your weight has held steady for months. Kristy Hamilton, MD, quoted on the ASPS website, says that excess skin can't be exercised away. If you still plan to lose a lot more, wait, because further weight loss can loosen the skin again. Your surgeon decides at consultation whether surgery fits.

### Does Ozempic affect healing after a tummy tuck?

It may, but studies conflict. A 2026 database study found wound problems in 9.8% of tummy tuck patients on a GLP-1 and 3.6% of those not on one, while a 2025 study at one institution found similar outcomes. Eating too little on these drugs can also deplete nutrients you need to heal, so tell your surgeon how much you are eating.

### What is an "Ozempic makeover"?

It is a catchy name, in the words of an article on the ASPS website, for plastic surgery procedures that address changes after rapid or significant weight loss. It may include a tummy tuck, breast lift or buttock lift. Kristy Hamilton, MD, quoted there, asks patients to choose up to two areas at a time and stages the operations.

### Do I need a fleur-de-lis tummy tuck after major weight loss?

Not always. Cleveland Clinic says a fleur-de-lis is usually for people with a large amount of excess skin due to major weight loss or multiple pregnancies, and that it adds a second vertical scar from the lower breastbone to the pubic bone. An extended tummy tuck is another option for skin on the flanks. Your surgeon decides at the exam.
