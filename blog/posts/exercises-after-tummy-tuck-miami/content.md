## When can you exercise after a tummy tuck?

You can walk right away, but harder exercise waits for weeks. [Cindy Wu, MD, on the website of the American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS)](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/answers-to-common-tummy-tuck-questions) says you can walk on the day of surgery, and that most surgeons encourage walking three times that day. [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/tummy-tuck/aftercare-recovery) says to be up and walking the day after, and to take it slow. Your surgeon will tell you which applies to you.

Strenuous exercise waits 4 to 6 weeks, according to both [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/procedures/11017-tummy-tuck) and The Aesthetic Society, and Wu says to avoid running and lifting for 6 weeks. These are general ranges, so your surgeon's clearance always comes first. Our tummy tuck page explains [how recovery goes and when you can get back to normal life](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#recovery).

## Post-tummy tuck exercise plan, week by week

Build up in this order: walking, daily tasks, strenuous exercise, then running and lifting. No source we read dates ab work, so ask your surgeon about it. Move to each step only when your surgeon agrees.

| When | What usually happens | Who says so |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day of surgery | Short walks, bent at the waist | Wu |
| First 7 to 10 days | Stay bent at the waist, keep walking | Wu |
| After 1 to 2 weeks | Standing tall; cooking or shopping are often manageable; driving too, once your surgeon clears it | ASPS; Salemy |
| First few weeks | No squatting, no picking up children | Salemy |
| Weeks 4 to 6 | Strenuous exercise may restart, once you are cleared | Cleveland Clinic; The Aesthetic Society |
| Week 6 | Running and lifting, once you are cleared | Wu |
| Weeks 4 to 8 | Heavy lifting, strenuous exercise, stretching and sex, once you are cleared | Cleveland Clinic |
| Around week 8 | Most people begin to feel more normal | Salemy |
| Around 3 months | Swelling keeps easing until about now; full recovery | Wu; Rao |

The ranges overlap because these sources don't all say the same thing. We give no minutes, step counts or pound limits, because none of them gives one. The table draws on [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck/results), [Shahram Salemy, MD, on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/what-you-need-to-know-about-your-tummy-tuck-recovery), [Cleveland Clinic's scar page](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar) and [Samir Rao, MD, on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/understanding-the-different-types-of-tummy-tucks).

The type of surgery changes the plan. Rao says most people resume more vigorous activities about 5 to 6 weeks after a mini. After an extended tummy tuck, Rao describes about 4 to 6 weeks of downtime before returning to work and about 12 weeks more before strenuous activities. For everything beyond exercise, see [tummy tuck recovery, week by week](/how-long-to-recover-tummy-tuck).

## Can you walk too much after a tummy tuck?

You can overdo it, but don't stop walking: it is the exercise you are encouraged to do. The Aesthetic Society says walking discourages swelling and helps prevent blood clots in the legs, and it says to take it slow. No source we read sets a limit on distance or steps.

![A woman in her early 30s with fair freckled skin and auburn hair tied back walks slowly along a bright arched corridor with a limestone floor, leaning forward slightly at the waist, in a loose oat-colored sweatshirt and wide joggers.](IMAGE:walking)

Use your body as the guide. If your stomach swells more after a longer walk, or a mild pull turns sharp, go back to shorter walks and call your surgeon. A pulling feeling on its own is common: Salemy says numbness or a pulling sensation for weeks or months is typically normal and often fades. There is more on that in our guide to [tightness after a tummy tuck](/how-to-reduce-tightness-after-tummy-tuck).

Thinking of a treadmill? We found no source that gives it a date. It is the same movement as walking, but Wu says you stay bent at the waist for 7 to 10 days, so wait until you can stand straight and ask your surgeon first.

## When can you run after a tummy tuck?

About 6 weeks at the earliest, and only once your surgeon clears you. Wu says running or lifting should be avoided for 6 weeks after the procedure. After an extended tummy tuck, Rao's timeline is much longer, so ask about your own type.

When you do start, go shorter and slower than you did before surgery, and stop if the incision pulls or you swell more afterward. That is common advice, not something a study has tested.

## When can you do ab exercises after a tummy tuck with muscle repair?

Wait for your surgeon's clearance. No source we read gives crunches, planks or twists a date of their own. The closest guide is Cleveland Clinic's scar page, which says to avoid heavy lifting, strenuous exercise and stretching until your provider says it is safe, usually 4 to 8 weeks after the procedure.

The reason to wait is simple. In a full tummy tuck, the surgeon repairs the paired six-pack muscles that may have separated, as Wu puts it, and ab work pulls on those same muscles. Not sure whether yours were repaired? Ask your surgeon, or read about [a tummy tuck with or without muscle repair](/what-is-a-tummy-tuck-without-muscle-repair-called).

Research doesn't help much here. A [2024 scoping review of rehabilitation around tummy tuck surgery](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39489057/) found 12 studies, mostly small trials, and concluded that data on its effects are lacking. Its abstract gives no date for ab work.

Toning your abs later is fine once you are cleared. ASPS says a [tummy tuck](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck) "is not a substitute for weight loss or an appropriate exercise program", and Cleveland Clinic names exercising as one way to maintain your results. When you get the go-ahead, ask which core moves to start with, and go from easy to hard.

## What about squats, weights, yoga and Pilates?

Judge each one by what it asks of your stomach, and ask your surgeon before you restart it. None of the sources we read gives these their own schedule, except where noted here.

- **Squats.** Salemy strongly advises against squatting for the first few weeks. No source we read gives squats a later date, so ask your surgeon before you restart them, with or without weights. Cleveland Clinic's 4 to 8 weeks for strenuous exercise and heavy lifting, with clearance, is the closest guide.
- **Weights.** Wu says no lifting for 6 weeks, and Cleveland Clinic says heavy lifting usually waits 4 to 8 weeks, until you are cleared. No source gives a pound limit, so be wary of any number you read online.
- **Yoga and Pilates.** Stretching is on Cleveland Clinic's list, usually 4 to 8 weeks with clearance. Ask your surgeon about poses that stretch or load your stomach.
- **Swimming.** [Cleveland Clinic's drain page](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/21104-jackson-pratt-jp-drain) says to avoid soaking in a tub or pool if you have a drain. Beyond that, the common advice is to wait until your incisions have fully closed and your surgeon agrees.

If your tummy tuck was part of a [mommy makeover](/procedures/mommy-makeover-miami), ask about arm work too, because breast surgery has its own limits.

## How do you exercise in the South Florida heat?

Walk early or late in the day, or indoors where it is cool, and carry water. That applies anywhere hot, and it is common sense more than research, though Wu does say adequate hydration helps lower the risk of blood clots.

Keep your scar out of the sun. Cleveland Clinic says it is extremely important to stay out of the sun as your wound heals, and to wear sunscreen with an SPF of 30 for 12 to 18 months if you have to be in the sun. If you feel dizzy or sick to your stomach, stop and call your surgeon: The Aesthetic Society lists both among the reasons to call.

## What are the signs you are doing too much?

Soreness or tiredness that settles with rest usually means you should ease off. Swelling that keeps growing, bleeding, a fever, fluid or an opening incision means you should call your surgeon. Leg pain, swelling or warmth needs medical care right away. Sudden shortness of breath or chest pain is an emergency. Call 911.

| What you notice | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Soreness or tiredness that settles with rest | Ease off, go back to the last level that felt fine |
| Numbness or a pulling feeling | Typically normal and often fades (Salemy); mention it at your next visit |
| More swelling, pain, redness, drainage or bleeding | Call your surgeon right away (The Aesthetic Society) |
| A fever of 100.4°F or more | Call your surgeon right away (Cleveland Clinic) |
| Fluid or pus leaking from an incision, severe pain or excessive bleeding | Call your surgeon right away (Cleveland Clinic) |
| An incision that opens | Call your surgeon right away |
| Dizziness, nausea or vomiting | Call your surgeon right away (The Aesthetic Society) |
| Leg pain, swelling or warmth | Get medical care right away; it can be a sign of a blood clot ([Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/17400-pulmonary-embolism)) |
| Sharp chest pain, sudden shortness of breath, a fast heartbeat | Call 911 (Cleveland Clinic) |

## Frequently asked questions

### Is walking enough exercise after a tummy tuck?

For the first few weeks, yes. Walking is the one exercise the sources we read encourage early. Cindy Wu, MD, on the ASPS website says it improves circulation and keeps the risk of blood clots in the legs low, and The Aesthetic Society says it discourages swelling. Go at an easy pace, and add distance only when you feel ready.

### When can I do squats after a tummy tuck?

Not for the first few weeks. Shahram Salemy, MD, on the ASPS website strongly advises against squatting then. No source we read gives squats their own date. The closest guide is Cleveland Clinic, which says strenuous exercise and heavy lifting usually wait 4 to 8 weeks, until your surgeon clears you, so ask before you restart them.

### When can I do planks and crunches after a tummy tuck?

Only after your surgeon clears ab work. No source we read gives planks or crunches their own date. Cleveland Clinic says strenuous exercise and stretching wait until your provider says it is safe, usually 4 to 8 weeks, so that is the earliest window to ask about. If you had muscle repair, ask which core moves to start with.

### When can I lift my kids after a tummy tuck?

Line up help first. Shahram Salemy, MD, on the ASPS website strongly advises against picking up children for the first few weeks, and The Aesthetic Society lists it as heavy lifting to avoid for 4 to 6 weeks. It also says that if you have small children, someone else must take full charge of their care for at least 2 weeks.

### Can exercising too soon ruin a tummy tuck?

It can set you back, though no source we read measures how often. The Aesthetic Society says strenuous exercise too early can trigger fluid retention in the treated areas, and Cleveland Clinic lists avoiding lifting and strenuous exercise among the steps that help your incision heal. Follow your surgeon's timeline even when you feel ready, and call your surgeon about new swelling.

### Do I need physical therapy after a tummy tuck?

Ask your surgeon, because the evidence is thin. A 2024 review by Gasparini and colleagues found 12 studies of preparation and rehabilitation around tummy tuck surgery, mostly small trials, and called for more research. Lymphatic drainage was the most common treatment studied, but the review doesn't show which approach works, so your surgeon's advice for your own recovery matters more.
