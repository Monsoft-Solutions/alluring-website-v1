## What is more painful, a BBL or a tummy tuck?

Usually a tummy tuck, especially one with muscle repair, though we found no study that measures the two side by side. The first week tends to be harder because you use your abdominal muscles to sit up, stand and get out of bed. BBL soreness tends to sit in the liposuction areas, and the hardest part is often not being able to sit.

That comparison is our reading of sources that each describe one surgery, and everyone feels pain differently, so ask your surgeon. For the full picture, see [what tummy tuck recovery looks like, step by step](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#recovery) and [what BBL recovery looks like, stage by stage](/procedures/brazilian-butt-lift-bbl-miami#recovery).

## How do BBL and tummy tuck procedures differ?

A BBL is surgery with a small but serious risk. The most serious is a fat embolism: fat injected into or below the gluteal muscle can enter a blood vessel, which can be fatal, as [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23308-brazilian-butt-lift) explains. [Safety guidance](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/documents/Patient-Safety/BBL/Gluteal-Fat-Grafting-Safety-Advisory_Jul18.pdf) says to keep fat under the skin, and [Florida's rule for office surgery](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/458.328) requires it. You can read [how BBL risk is reduced](/procedures/brazilian-butt-lift-bbl-miami#safety) in more detail.

The surgery itself is liposuction to collect fat, then fat transfer to the buttocks. At Alluring it takes 3 to 5 hours under general anesthesia, and you go home the same day.

A tummy tuck "removes excess fat and skin and, in most cases, restores weakened or separated muscles," [according to ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck). The scar usually runs from hip bone to hip bone, just above the pubic area, [Cleveland Clinic says](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar).

So the two hurt in different places. A BBL is sore where the fat was taken and limits how you sit. A tummy tuck is sore along the incision and across the repaired muscles, which you use every time you move. If you are still choosing between them for your goals, [our comparison of the two surgeries](/blog/tummy-tuck-vs-bbl-miami) covers that.

## Which surgery causes more post-op pain?

Expect the tummy tuck to feel harder in the first week. The harder part of a BBL is often the sitting limits, which last about 8 weeks.

### What tummy tuck pain feels like

[The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/tummy-tuck/aftercare-recovery) says "The first two days are the most uncomfortable" and that discomfort typically drops to a "nagging" level by 5 to 7 days. It also says the first week will be the worst, and that you will still feel like you are recovering for about 2 to 3 weeks.

[Cindy Wu, MD, writing on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/answers-to-common-tummy-tuck-questions), says most patients manage "with just a few days of pain medications," and that you are bent at the waist for 7 to 10 days to protect the incision. That pulling feeling is often what people notice: here is [why a tummy tuck feels so tight](/how-to-reduce-tightness-after-tummy-tuck) and what helps.

### What BBL pain feels like

A [plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/six-things-you-need-to-know-about-recovering-from-a-brazilian-butt-lift) says most people need some pain medication for the first 4 to 5 days, and that many find the liposuctioned areas "to be tender, but not overly sensitive."

Cleveland Clinic says "The first several days after your Brazilian butt lift procedure are often the most painful," and that pain should decrease after 1 to 2 weeks. You also can't sit or lie on your buttocks for at least 2 weeks, and you can't sit without a BBL pillow for about 8 weeks. Our guide to [BBL recovery, week by week](/how-long-to-recover-from-bbl) goes through it in full.

## What affects pain after cosmetic surgery?

Your own pain tolerance is part of it, and so is what was actually done:

- **Muscle repair.** You use these muscles every time you sit up or stand, so they are a big part of why a full tummy tuck feels hard at first. A [tummy tuck without muscle repair](/what-is-a-tummy-tuck-without-muscle-repair-called) leaves the muscles as they are, and Samir Rao, MD, [on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/understanding-the-different-types-of-tummy-tucks), says a mini "requires less recovery time than a full tummy tuck."
- **Where the fat comes from.** BBL soreness tends to sit in the areas where fat was removed, so ask your surgeon which areas are planned.
- **Doing more than one procedure.** More surgery means more areas healing at once, and more risk. In an insurance database of 25,478 tummy tucks, major complications followed 3.1% of those done alone and 3.8% of those combined with liposuction ([Winocour and colleagues, Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery, 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26505716/)).
- **Staying on schedule.** The Aesthetic Society says to stick to your medication times, because the first 2 days of a tummy tuck are the hardest.

## How should you manage post-surgery pain?

Your surgeon's instructions come first. These are the steps the sources support:

1. **Take your medicine as prescribed.** Cleveland Clinic says some people take "either a prescribed pain medication or OTC acetaminophen" after a BBL, and that you shouldn't drive while taking a prescription pain medication.
2. **Walk a little, starting early.** Cindy Wu, MD, says, "You can walk the day of surgery," after a tummy tuck, and that it helps lower the risk of blood clots. After a BBL, The Aesthetic Society says you should be able to get up and walk after the second day.
3. **Rest in the position your surgeon shows you.** That means bent at the waist after a tummy tuck, and on your stomach or sides after a BBL, as Cleveland Clinic advises. Our guide to [sleeping after a BBL](/how-to-sleep-after-bbl) covers the setup.
4. **Wear your compression garment as directed.** Cindy Wu, MD, says it is worn for 4 to 6 weeks after a tummy tuck. Your surgeon tells you how long to wear yours.
5. **Line up help at home.** The Aesthetic Society says that after a tummy tuck, "you must put someone else totally in charge" of small children for at least 2 weeks.

## How long does recovery take after each surgery?

Both usually get you back to a desk in about 2 weeks. After that, a tummy tuck limits lifting and a BBL limits sitting.

## When is pain a reason to call your surgeon?

Pain should ease as the days go by. After a tummy tuck, The Aesthetic Society says to call your surgeon immediately if you notice an increase in swelling, pain, redness, drainage or bleeding, or if you develop a fever, dizziness, nausea or vomiting. After a BBL, it says, "If the pain is extreme or long-lasting, contact your surgeon."

Blood clots in the legs are a known risk. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/16911-deep-vein-thrombosis-dvt) lists swelling of your leg on one side and pain or tenderness in your leg as signs, and says not to wait to see if they go away.

Breathing trouble, chest pain or an unusual heartbeat mean you should seek medical attention immediately, so call 911.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a tummy tuck painful?

Yes, for the first days, especially with muscle repair. The Aesthetic Society says the first 2 days are the most uncomfortable and that discomfort drops to a nagging level by 5 to 7 days. You stay bent at the waist for 7 to 10 days, and most people work again in about 2 weeks, according to Cindy Wu, MD, on the ASPS website.

### How long does pain last after a BBL?

Most people need some pain medication for the first 4 to 5 days, a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS says, and Cleveland Clinic says pain should decrease after 1 to 2 weeks. Not sitting on your buttocks for at least 2 weeks is often the harder part.

### Is BBL recovery harder than tummy tuck recovery?

They are hard in different ways. A tummy tuck limits standing straight and lifting for weeks, and a BBL limits sitting. Desk work comes back after 10 to 14 days for a BBL and about 2 weeks for a tummy tuck. Cleveland Clinic puts full BBL recovery at 2 to 3 months, and up to 6; Samir Rao, MD, puts a tummy tuck at around 3 months.

### Does having both in one surgery make the pain worse?

We found no study that measures it. It does mean a longer operation and two recoveries at once, and combined procedures raised the risk of complications in a large database (Winocour and colleagues, 2015). Your surgeon tells you whether to do both in one surgery, and [our comparison of the two surgeries](/blog/tummy-tuck-vs-bbl-miami) covers it.

### What helps with pain after a tummy tuck or a BBL?

Take your medicine on the schedule your surgeon gives you, walk a little as soon as you are told you can, wear your garment as directed and rest in the position your surgeon shows you. Pain that gets worse instead of better, or that comes with a fever, needs a call to your surgeon. Swelling, pain or warmth in one leg needs medical care right away.
