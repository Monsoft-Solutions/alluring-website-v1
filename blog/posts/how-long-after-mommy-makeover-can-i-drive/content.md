## When can I drive after a tummy tuck or mommy makeover?

When you can drive again depends on the surgery. After a tummy tuck alone, driving is often manageable after 1 to 2 weeks, according to [Shahram Salemy, MD, on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/what-you-need-to-know-about-your-tummy-tuck-recovery). After a mommy makeover, [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/mommy-makeover/aftercare-recovery) puts driving at 2 to 3 weeks. Either way, your surgeon clears you.

| Surgery | What the source says | Who says so |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tummy tuck alone | Driving often manageable after 1 to 2 weeks | Shahram Salemy, MD, on the ASPS website |
| Breast lift alone | No driving during the first 2 weeks | The Aesthetic Society |
| Mommy makeover | Driving in 2 to 3 weeks, or when all sutures and drains are out and you no longer take pain medication | The Aesthetic Society |

The 1 to 2 weeks you may see quoted for driving after a mommy makeover is the tummy tuck figure. [What a mommy makeover includes](/procedures/mommy-makeover-miami) explains the gap: The Aesthetic Society says the combination [typically focuses on the breasts and abdomen](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/mommy-makeover) and might include a tummy tuck, a breast lift, augmentation or reduction, and liposuction, so you heal from more at once. For the tummy tuck on its own, see the [stages of tummy tuck recovery](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#recovery). Still choosing between the two? [Compare a tummy tuck and a mommy makeover](/blog/tummy-tuck-mommy-makeover-miami).

## How long after a tummy tuck can I drive?

After a tummy tuck alone, also called an abdominoplasty, driving is often manageable after 1 to 2 weeks, says Shahram Salemy, MD, on the ASPS website. That is one surgeon's view, not a society's rule. The [ASPS recovery page](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck/recovery) and [The Aesthetic Society's aftercare page](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/tummy-tuck/aftercare-recovery) for tummy tucks give no driving date.

Three things move your date:

- **How you stand and sit.** [Cindy Wu, MD, on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/answers-to-common-tummy-tuck-questions), says you stay bent at the waist for 7 to 10 days after surgery, to protect the incision.
- **Where the seat belt sits.** [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar) says a standard tummy tuck scar usually runs from hip bone to hip bone, just above the pubic area. A lap belt sits close to that line.
- **Drains, if you have them.** Wu says a drain usually comes out in 1 to 2 weeks. Our guide to [tummy tuck drains](/blog/tummy-tuck-drains-what-they-are-how-long-they-stay) explains what to expect.

## How long after a mommy makeover can I drive?

After a mommy makeover, The Aesthetic Society says you may be comfortable enough to drive in 2 to 3 weeks, or when all your sutures and drains are out and you no longer take pain medication. It also says that by the end of the first week, you will still be unable to drive.

The rest of its guidance points the same way: the first week will be the worst, you may still feel like you are recovering for 2 to 3 weeks, and recovery takes at least 2 weeks and can be longer, depending on your combination of procedures. Traditional sutures usually come out within 1 to 2 weeks, and absorbable ones don't need removing.

The parts in your plan decide where you land in that range, so ask at your consultation. See the [mommy makeover recovery overview](/procedures/mommy-makeover-miami#recovery) and the [week-by-week recovery timeline](/blog/mommy-makeover-recovery-signs).

## Can I drive after a breast lift or breast augmentation?

After a breast lift alone, The Aesthetic Society says you must not lift, drive, do laundry or clean during the first 2 weeks. When a lift or implants are part of a mommy makeover, plan on the mommy makeover driving figure of 2 to 3 weeks instead.

Breast augmentation on its own has a separate guide: [driving after breast augmentation](/how-long-after-breast-augmentation-can-i-drive).

## Who drives the kids while I can't drive?

Plan for another adult to do all the driving for 2 to 3 weeks after a mommy makeover, or 1 to 2 weeks after a tummy tuck alone. The Aesthetic Society also says someone else must be totally in charge of small children for at least 2 weeks, and no picking up your children for 4 to 6 weeks.

That last limit changes the school run. Being cleared to drive doesn't clear you to lift a toddler into a car seat, and The Aesthetic Society's lifting limit runs longer than its driving figure. A child who climbs in and buckles up alone is a different plan, so ask your surgeon.

| What to hand off | For how long | Who says so |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The ride home, and an adult staying with you | The day of surgery and at least the first night after an outpatient tummy tuck; several days if you go home the same day after a mommy makeover | [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck/preparation); The Aesthetic Society |
| The ride to your first follow-up | Usually within 5 days of surgery | The Aesthetic Society |
| Care of small children | At least 2 weeks | The Aesthetic Society |
| School runs, errands and all other driving | 2 to 3 weeks after a mommy makeover; 1 to 2 weeks after a tummy tuck alone | The Aesthetic Society; Shahram Salemy, MD |
| Lifting a child into a car seat | 4 to 6 weeks after a mommy makeover; the first few weeks after a tummy tuck alone | The Aesthetic Society; Shahram Salemy, MD |

Work can end before driving does. The Aesthetic Society says to plan about 2 weeks off, depending on your job, so if you drive to work, line up a ride until you are cleared. Put a name against each job before you choose a surgery date: [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/mommy-makeover/preparing-your-procedure) asks for a "no-fail support system for the full recovery period."

## How do I know I'm ready to drive again?

You are ready to drive after surgery when five things are true: you no longer take pain medication, you can turn to check a blind spot without pain, you can brake hard without holding back, the seat belt sits comfortably, and your surgeon has cleared you. Try the movements in a parked car, and stop if your incision pulls.

| Check | How to test it, parked with the engine off |
| --- | --- |
| You no longer take pain medication | You have stopped taking it, not just skipped a dose to drive |
| You can turn to check a blind spot without pain | Look over each shoulder, as you would to change lanes |
| You can brake hard without holding back | Press the pedal hard and fast a few times |
| The seat belt sits comfortably | Buckle up over what you wear during the day, and sit for a few minutes |
| Your surgeon has cleared you | Ask at your follow-up, with any drains and removable sutures out |

The pain medication check comes from The Aesthetic Society. The other checks are common practice, not studied, so your surgeon has the last word. An abdominal binder or [compression garment](/blog/mommy-makeover-compression-garments-guide) sits under the belt too, so test with yours on.

![Card titled Five checks before you drive again. You no longer take pain medication. You can turn to check a blind spot without pain. You can brake hard without holding back. The seat belt sits comfortably. Your surgeon has cleared you. The pain medication check comes from The Aesthetic Society. The other checks are common practice, not studied.](IMAGE:five-checks)

Pain medicine is also a legal question in Florida. The state's [DUI statute](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0300-0399/0316/Sections/0316.193.html) covers "any substance controlled under chapter 893" when your normal faculties are impaired, and [chapter 893](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0800-0899/0893/Sections/0893.03.html) lists oxycodone and hydrocodone in Schedule II. [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/druginfo/meds/a682132.html) says oxycodone may make you drowsy and tells you not to drive until you know how it affects you.

## How can I make the first drives easier?

Keep the first drives after surgery short, familiar and in daylight, outside rush hour, with another adult in the car who can take over. Set the seat and mirrors before you start the engine. If you feel pain or hesitate at a stop, pull over and hand over the keys.

Keep the seat belt on: [Florida law](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0300-0399/0316/Sections/0316.614.html) requires it of drivers and front-seat passengers. Two common habits may help, though neither has been studied, so check with your surgeon:

- **Padding.** Some people put a small folded towel between the belt and the incision. Ask first, because anything thick can move the belt off its normal path.
- **Twisting less.** Use your mirrors and a backup camera if your car has one, but only once you can still check a blind spot.

## Can I ride in a car after a tummy tuck or mommy makeover?

Yes, as a passenger, from the day of surgery. ASPS advises arranging for someone to drive you to and from an outpatient tummy tuck, and The Aesthetic Society says someone should drive you home and stay for several days if you go home the same day after a mommy makeover.

<Figure src="IMAGE:passenger" alt="A woman with dark wavy hair and a loose cream linen shirt rides in the front passenger seat of a car with her seat belt on, looking out of the window in soft morning light, while another adult drives." width="1600" height="1067" caption="Model shown. Not a patient." />

[A 2020 review in Aesthetic Plastic Surgery](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31858207/) found that blood clots occur more often after a tummy tuck than after any other cosmetic surgery, and more often still when liposuction is added. Wu says walking on the day of surgery keeps the risk of clots in the legs low, and [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000156.htm) advises moving your legs often on long car trips. Ask your surgeon how soon you can take a long ride and how often to stop.

MedlinePlus lists swelling, pain or tenderness, warmth and redness in a leg or arm as signs of a deep vein clot, so call your surgeon about any of them. Chest pain, coughing up blood, difficulty breathing or fainting mean going to the emergency room or calling 911.

If you live elsewhere in Florida or in another state, you also need rides to your follow-ups. Ask how many nights to stay in Miami before you are cleared to go home, and see how a [fly-in consultation](/fly-in-consultation) works.

## What should I ask my surgeon about driving before I book?

Before you book surgery, ask your surgeon which part of your plan sets your driving date, whether you will have drains or sutures to remove, when you can expect to stop pain medication, and who clears you to drive. Those answers tell you how many weeks of driving help to line up.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I drive one week after a tummy tuck?

Sometimes, but don't count on it. Shahram Salemy, MD, on the ASPS website, says driving is often manageable after 1 to 2 weeks, while Cindy Wu, MD, says you stay bent at the waist for 7 to 10 days. If you still take pain medication or can't brake hard, wait and ask your surgeon.

### Can I drive one week after a mommy makeover?

No. The Aesthetic Society says that by the end of the first week, you should be walking around the house frequently, but you will still be unable to drive. Its driving figure for a mommy makeover is 2 to 3 weeks, or when all your sutures and drains are out and you no longer take pain medication.

### Do I have to stop all pain medicine before I drive?

The Aesthetic Society ties driving after a mommy makeover to no longer taking pain medication, and it doesn't separate prescription from over-the-counter medicine. Opioid medicine such as oxycodone is the clear line, because of drowsiness and Florida's DUI statute. Ask your surgeon about anything you are still taking before you get behind the wheel.

### Can I skip the seat belt if it presses on my incision?

No. Florida law requires a seat belt for drivers and front-seat passengers. Its medical exception covers a person certified by a physician as having a condition that makes a belt inappropriate or dangerous. If the belt hurts too much to wear, you aren't ready to drive yet, so ask your surgeon about padding and timing.

### How long after a panniculectomy can I drive?

We found no driving date for a panniculectomy. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/panniculectomy) says the incision usually runs across the lower abdomen from hip to hip, and that light daily activities, including desk work, return after 1 to 2 weeks. A lap belt sits close to that line, so use the same five checks and ask your surgeon to clear you.

### How long after Lipo 360 can I drive?

We found no driving date for liposuction or [Lipo 360 recovery](/procedures/liposuction-miami#recovery). [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/liposuction/aftercare-recovery) says someone must drive you home if you leave the same day, and that you can usually resume most normal activities within 10 days or less. As part of a mommy makeover, The Aesthetic Society's driving figure is 2 to 3 weeks. Use the five checks, and ask your surgeon to clear you.

### When can I drive after a tummy tuck and BBL?

We found no date for the two together. Alone, driving after a tummy tuck is often manageable after 1 to 2 weeks (Salemy). [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/butt-lift/aftercare-recovery) says you can drive 10 to 14 days after a BBL, sitting on a pillow. Plan around the longer one and your surgeon's rules on [how long after a BBL you can sit](/how-long-after-bbl-can-i-sit).
