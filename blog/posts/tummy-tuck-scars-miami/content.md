## Where is a tummy tuck scar?

After a full tummy tuck, you have a long, low scar across your lower belly and usually a small one around your belly button. A mini leaves a shorter scar and the longer types reach further, so the place to start is [which tummy tuck you need](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#which-tummy-tuck).

Every type leaves a scar. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar) says you can't completely prevent one, and that while small or fine scars may fade completely after about a year, most people will still have a visible scar. It also says the scar is usually thin, though it may be raised, recessed, slightly pink or lighter than the skin around it. So the realistic goal is a thin line that sits where your clothes cover it.

## Where does the scar sit for each type of tummy tuck?

The size and position depend on the type. Lengths appear here only where a source gives one.

| Type | Where the scar sits | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mini | Low in the pubic area, generally with no cut around the belly button | About the length of a C-section scar, 3 to 6 inches (Cleveland Clinic) |
| Full | Hip bone to hip bone, just above the pubic area, often with a scar around the belly button | Hip bone to hip bone |
| Extended | Longer and often higher than a full or mini scar, stretching up and around the flanks | None in the sources we read |
| Fleur-de-lis | Across the abdomen, plus a second vertical scar from the lower breastbone to the pubic bone | None in the sources we read |

The extended row comes from [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/tummy-tuck/incisions-and-scars) and a [post by Samir Rao, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/understanding-the-different-types-of-tummy-tucks), on the website of the American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS). The Aesthetic Society gives a wider range for a mini, from a few inches to the entire span of the abdomen, so ask about your own.

For a full tummy tuck, [Jonathan Weiler, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/your-guide-to-a-tummy-tuck-before-during-and-after), on the ASPS website, says the incision should be straight and as low as possible so underwear or swimsuit bottoms easily conceal the scar. The Aesthetic Society says your surgeon sets its shape and length from your preferences and the correction you need, so bring the underwear or swimsuit cut you usually wear and ask where the line would fall.

Alluring's price list also has an extended mini, described as a hip-to-hip incision without muscle repair. We found no outside source on that type, so ask your surgeon to show you where it would sit.

## How does a tummy tuck scar fade over time?

Slowly, over a year or more. [Cindy Wu, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/answers-to-common-tummy-tuck-questions), in a post on the ASPS website, says scars become thinner and lighter in color at around 12 to 18 months. [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck/results) says the scar may take several months to a year to fade as much as it will. [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/tummy-tuck/aftercare-recovery) gives the widest range: fading and flattening typically take 3 months to 2 years.

None of the sources we read gives a week-by-week color chart, so treat any you see as a rough guess. Here is what they do say:

| When | What the sources say |
| --- | --- |
| First weeks | Surgical glue, tape or bandages can reduce scarring by holding the incision together and keeping it moist (Cleveland Clinic) |
| Usually 4 to 8 weeks | Heavy lifting, strenuous exercise, sex and stretching wait until your surgeon says it's safe (Cleveland Clinic) |
| Several months | The scar is still visible, and typically fades over time (Jonathan Weiler, MD) |
| About 1 year | Small or fine scars may fade completely, but most people still have a visible scar (Cleveland Clinic) |
| 12 to 18 months | Thinner and lighter in color (Cindy Wu, MD) |
| Up to 2 years | The late end of fading and flattening (The Aesthetic Society) |

![Timeline card for a fading tummy tuck scar. Several months: still visible. About 1 year: small or fine scars may fade completely, but most people still have a visible scar. 12 to 18 months: thinner and lighter in color. 3 months to 2 years: fading and flattening.](IMAGE:timeline)

## What helps a tummy tuck scar heal well?

A well-healed incision with little strain on it. The Aesthetic Society says your scars also depend on the surgical technique and on any underlying conditions or genetic tendencies. These parts are in your hands:

- **Before surgery.** Cleveland Clinic says quitting smoking several months ahead can help your wound heal, and its tummy tuck page gives, as an example, at least 1 month before surgery and 2 weeks after. Your surgeon sets yours. Tell your surgeon if you take corticosteroids or have a condition such as diabetes, since both can affect healing.
- **In the first weeks.** Follow your surgeon's wound care, and hold off on the activities in the table above until you are cleared.
- **At home.** [An article on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/worried-about-surgery-scars-here-are-some-tips) says everyday cooking, cleaning, laundry and lifting can increase tension on the incision and sometimes even cause wound separation, so line up help. Our [week-by-week tummy tuck recovery guide](/how-long-to-recover-tummy-tuck) shows what to plan for.

## Does a C-section scar go away with a tummy tuck?

It may. ASPS says that in women who have had a C-section, the existing scar may be incorporated into the new one. It also says previous abdominal surgery may limit the results of a tummy tuck, so ask what your own scar means for you.

Kristy Hamilton, MD, quoted in [an article on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/post-pregnancy-plastic-surgery-what-can-you-do-now-and-what-needs-to-wait), describes waiting at least 6 months after delivery, longer if breastfeeding, and advises against having a tummy tuck during a C-section. The [section on who is ready for a tummy tuck](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#candidate) covers the rest.

## Do scar creams and silicone work on a tummy tuck scar?

Silicone has the most evidence behind it, though reviews disagree on how good that evidence is. Onion extract and vitamin E have much less. In a PubMed search on 3 November 2026 we found no controlled trial of silicone on tummy tuck scars specifically, so what follows comes from other kinds of scars.

- **Silicone gel or sheets.** A [2013 Cochrane review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24030657/) of 20 trials and 873 people found "weak evidence" that sheeting helps prevent abnormal scars in high-risk people, and improvements in thickness and color when it treats them, but called the trials poor quality and highly susceptible to bias. A [2020 review in Dermatologic Surgery](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32932267/) of 34 trials, 16 of them on silicone sheets, was more positive, reporting that silicone gels and sheets significantly improved scar outcomes, and still asks for better long-term studies.
- **Onion extract and vitamin E.** The 2020 review found only a limited number of studies supporting them. A [2016 review of topical vitamin E in Aesthetic Surgery Journal](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26977069/) looked at 6 studies: 3 showed improvement, 2 of those using vitamin E with other treatments, 3 did not, and 2 reported reactions such as a rash or itching. It found the evidence not yet enough to justify widespread use on its own.
- **Scar massage.** Cleveland Clinic says you may consider it about 2 to 3 weeks after surgery, once you have talked to your provider, and a [2016 review in Seminars in Plastic Surgery](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4961501/) says not to start until the wound has closed completely. A [2012 review in Dermatologic Surgery](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22093081/) called the evidence weak, though 27 of 30 surgical scars improved with massage, and a [2018 review on burn scars](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28669442/) called its evidence preliminary and of poor quality. Neither is specific to tummy tucks, so we can't say how much it helps after one.

Schedules differ too. Michelle Roughton, MD, in the article on the ASPS website, says a year of silicone is ideal and 3 to 6 months more realistic. The Seminars in Plastic Surgery review suggests wearing sheets at least 12 hours a day for 3 to 4 months, and no more than 6 months in total. Follow your surgeon's plan, and ask before you pay for expensive creams: that review says the benefit of ointments seems to come from the massage and the moisture.

## How do you protect a tummy tuck scar from the sun?

Keep it out of the sun. Cleveland Clinic says ultraviolet light can make a scar discolored or thicker, and advises sunscreen with an SPF of 30 for 12 to 18 months after your tummy tuck if you have to be in the sun. Dr. Roughton explains that sun on a new scar can leave it too dark, and that a burn can leave it too light.

Clothing is often enough for a scar it covers. The belly button scar is the one people forget: Dr. Roughton says patients back in two-piece swimsuits need either a one-piece for a while longer or plenty of sunscreen there.

## When should you ask about a thick scar or a revision?

Ask early if a scar is getting thicker, and call right away about signs of infection. Revision surgery usually comes much later.

| Usually expected | Ask your surgeon |
| --- | --- |
| A thin line, perhaps slightly raised, recessed, pink or lighter than your skin | A scar that is getting thick, raised or red |
| Still visible after several months | A scar that grows past the edges of the incision |
| Fading slowly over a year or more | Bleeding, pus or foul-smelling drainage, a swollen or very painful incision, swelling, pain or redness that is increasing, or a fever: call right away |

Cleveland Clinic lists those incision signs, and The Aesthetic Society says to call your surgeon immediately about an increase in swelling, pain, redness, drainage or bleeding, or a fever.

The Seminars in Plastic Surgery review explains the difference between the two kinds of thick scar. A hypertrophic scar is raised but stays within the incision, most often starts around the first month, and many improve on their own. A keloid grows beyond the incision, doesn't go away by itself, and is more common on darker skin. If you have ever had a thick scar or a keloid, tell your surgeon at your consultation.

Cleveland Clinic says steroid injections and laser resurfacing may help, and that both are usually kept for abnormal scarring, though a [2022 Cochrane review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36161591/) of 15 trials found "insufficient evidence to support or refute" lasers for hypertrophic and keloid scars. In scar revision, a surgeon cuts the scar out and closes the wound with stitches. Cleveland Clinic says most people wait 12 to 18 months for it, when the scar is about as good as it is going to get. Lasers can cause swelling, blistering and redness, and revision may cause infection, bleeding or more scarring.

## Can a tummy tuck remove stretch marks?

Only those on the skin that comes off. [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck) says a tummy tuck cannot correct stretch marks, although they may be removed or somewhat improved if they sit on the excess skin being cut away. Stretch marks elsewhere on your belly stay, so ask your surgeon which of yours would go.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a tummy tuck with no scar?

No. Removing loose skin takes an incision, and Cleveland Clinic says you can't completely prevent the scar. A mini leaves a shorter one but treats less. If your concern is more fat than loose skin, compare your options in our guide to [a tummy tuck versus liposuction](/blog/tummy-tuck-vs-liposuction).

### Can a bikini hide a tummy tuck scar?

The low line, usually. Dr. Weiler says the incision goes as low as possible so swimsuit bottoms cover it, and The Aesthetic Society says it may be shaped so a bikini hides it. The scar around the belly button may still show in a two-piece. Extended and fleur-de-lis scars run longer or higher, so ask about those.

### When does a tummy tuck scar stop being red?

No source we read gives a date for redness alone. Cleveland Clinic says a healed scar may be slightly pink or lighter than your skin, and Dr. Wu says it turns lighter in color at around 12 to 18 months. If a scar stays red, thick or raised, mention it at a follow-up, since treatments exist.

### What does a tummy tuck scar look like after 1 year?

Usually fainter, but still there. Cleveland Clinic says small or fine scars may fade completely after about a year and that most people still have a visible scar. Dr. Wu says scars keep getting thinner and lighter until around 12 to 18 months, and The Aesthetic Society says fading can take up to 2 years.

### Can a tummy tuck scar be revised?

Yes, in some cases. Cleveland Clinic says scars such as hypertrophic or keloid scars may need surgical treatment, in which a surgeon cuts the scar out and closes the wound with stitches. Most people wait 12 to 18 months. Revision may cause infection, bleeding or more scarring, and the Seminars in Plastic Surgery review says more conservative treatments should come first.

### Does a mini tummy tuck leave a scar?

Yes. Cleveland Clinic says it is about the length of a C-section scar, 3 to 6 inches, and generally has no cut around the belly button. The Aesthetic Society says it can run longer. At Alluring the mini doesn't include muscle repair, so read [what a tummy tuck without muscle repair can do](/what-is-a-tummy-tuck-without-muscle-repair-called) first.
