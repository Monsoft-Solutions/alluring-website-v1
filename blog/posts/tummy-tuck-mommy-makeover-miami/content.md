## Do I need a tummy tuck or a mommy makeover?

Look at where the changes are. If they are all in your abdomen, such as loose skin, extra fat or separated muscles, a tummy tuck is the surgery that treats them. If your breasts changed too, that points to a mommy makeover, a combination of surgeries. Your surgeon confirms which one fits after examining you.

A mommy makeover treats more in one plan, and more surgery can mean more to heal and more risk. Read [how a tummy tuck is done](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#procedure), and when you are ready, see [what a mommy makeover costs, part by part](/procedures/mommy-makeover-miami#pricing).

Here is what each one treats, according to the American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS) and The Aesthetic Society:

- **Tummy tuck:** The abdomen. Loose skin, extra fat, separated muscles.
- **Mommy makeover:** The breasts and abdomen. A tummy tuck, a breast lift, augmentation or reduction, and liposuction can all be part of it.

## What is the difference between a tummy tuck and a mommy makeover?

A tummy tuck is one operation on the abdomen. A mommy makeover is a plan built from several operations, with the goal, [ASPS says](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/mommy-makeover), of restoring the shape and appearance of a woman's body after childbearing. ASPS says it is typically performed as a single-stage procedure. A tummy tuck can be one of its parts.

Tummy tuck and mommy makeover, side by side:

| Compare | Tummy tuck | Mommy makeover |
|---|---|---|
| What it treats | The abdomen | The breasts and abdomen |
| Who it suits | Healthy, at a stable weight, bothered by the abdomen | Healthy, at your ideal body weight, finished with childbearing |
| Scars | Hip bone to hip bone, and possibly around the belly button | In the bikini line and under the bra |
| Time off work | About 2 weeks | About 2 weeks |
| Driving | 1 to 2 weeks | 2 to 3 weeks |
| Final result | Up to 3 months | 6 months to a year |
| Major complications | 3.1% | 4.6% with liposuction and a breast procedure |

## What does a tummy tuck treat, and what does a mommy makeover add?

A tummy tuck treats the abdomen: [ASPS says](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck) it removes excess fat and skin and, in most cases, restores weakened or separated muscles. A mommy makeover adds what else changed. [The Aesthetic Society says](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/mommy-makeover) it typically focuses on the breasts and abdomen and might include a breast lift, augmentation or reduction, and liposuction.

About a tummy tuck:

- **Muscle repair depends on the type.** A mini tummy tuck at Alluring does not include it, so if separated muscles bother you, ask which type you are being offered.
- **A C-section scar can be part of it.** [ASPS says](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck/results) the existing scar may be incorporated into the new one.

What a mommy makeover adds:

- **A breast procedure.** If you choose implants, plan for more surgery someday: the [FDA says](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/breast-implants/risks-and-complications-breast-implants) they are not considered lifetime devices, and The Aesthetic Society says you will need to replace them over time.
- **Liposuction.** The Aesthetic Society lists the legs, arms, hips and waist as areas it might treat.

## Who is a tummy tuck right for, and who is a mommy makeover right for?

A tummy tuck suits you if your abdomen is what bothers you and, as [ASPS describes candidates](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck/candidates), you are physically healthy, at a stable weight and a nonsmoker. A mommy makeover suits you if your breasts bother you too and, [ASPS adds](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/mommy-makeover/candidates), you are at your ideal body weight and finished with childbearing. Your surgeon decides at consultation.

Two things apply to both:

- **Your weight.** Be close to your goal weight for 6 to 12 months before a tummy tuck, [Paul Vitenas, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/five-things-to-consider-before-getting-a-tummy-tuck), advises on the ASPS website. "These are not weight loss surgeries," says Nneamaka Nwubah, MD, [quoted by ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/mommy-makeovers-what-women-should-know-about-postpartum-surgical-procedures).
- **Smoking.** [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/mommy-makeovers) says you will need to quit smoking for at least 6 weeks before a mommy makeover.

Read more about [who is a good candidate for a mommy makeover](/procedures/mommy-makeover-miami#candidate) and [who a tummy tuck suits](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#candidate).

## How is recovery different after a tummy tuck and a mommy makeover?

Time off work looks similar, about 2 weeks for either surgery, though surgeons differ: after a tummy tuck, Cindy Wu, MD, says about 2 weeks, and Shahram Salemy, MD, says most people opt for up to a month. What changes is how much of your body is healing at once.

The Aesthetic Society says mommy makeover recovery takes at least 2 weeks and could be longer depending on your combination, and that the tummy tuck part is the most uncomfortable.

| Milestone | Tummy tuck alone | Mommy makeover |
|---|---|---|
| Young children | No picking them up for the first few weeks (Shahram Salemy, MD) | Someone else in charge of their care for at least 2 weeks (The Aesthetic Society) |
| Driving | Often manageable after 1 to 2 weeks (Shahram Salemy, MD) | 2 to 3 weeks, or when sutures and drains are out and you are off pain medication (The Aesthetic Society) |
| Heavy lifting | 4 to 8 weeks ([Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar)) | 4 to 6 weeks (The Aesthetic Society) |

These figures come from different authors, not from one study comparing the two surgeries, which is why the lifting ranges overlap. Expect the limits of every surgery in your plan to apply together, and follow your surgeon's schedule.

## Is a mommy makeover riskier than a tummy tuck?

One large database study says somewhat, yes. In an insurance database of 25,478 patients, major complications followed 3.1% of tummy tucks done alone and 4.6% of those with liposuction and a breast procedure, the closest line to a mommy makeover (Winocour and colleagues, Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery, 2015). These are 2008 to 2013 averages, not any surgeon's rate.

| Surgery in the study | Result |
|---|---|
| Tummy tuck alone | 3.1% with major complications |
| With liposuction | 3.8% with major complications |
| With a breast procedure | 4.3% with major complications |
| With liposuction and a breast procedure | 4.6% with major complications |

Of those major complications, 31.5% were hematomas (bleeding under the skin), 27.2% infections and 20.2% suspected or confirmed blood clots. The same study found higher risk at age 55 or older, with a BMI of 30 or more, and when several procedures were combined. Those are risk factors, not cutoffs.

- **Blood clots.** In 129,007 patients having cosmetic surgery, blood clots within 30 days followed 0.20% of combined procedures and 0.04% of single ones ([Winocour and colleagues, Aesthetic Surgery Journal, 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28207041/)). The authors call the overall rate relatively low.
- **A newer review is more reassuring.** A 2026 review of 8 studies found that a tummy tuck combined with breast surgery carries a complication profile comparable to a tummy tuck alone and higher than breast surgery alone, and suggested it is generally safe in carefully selected patients ([Skorochod and Wolf, Aesthetic Plastic Surgery, 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42089979/)). It rates its own evidence as moderate to very low certainty.
- **Length of surgery.** In a study of complex plastic surgery at one US medical center, each extra hour of surgery raised the odds of a complication by 21% ([Hardy and colleagues, Aesthetic Surgery Journal, 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24696297/)). It was not limited to mommy makeovers, but it is why the length of your plan matters.

In a doctor's office, [Florida's rule](https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=64B8-9.009) caps the planned length of all procedures in one cosmetic surgery at 8 hours. When liposuction is combined with a tummy tuck in the same office operation, it allows at most 1,000 cc of supernatant fat, the fat alone once the fluid separates out. Hospitals and licensed surgery centers fall outside these limits. More in [what to know about tummy tuck safety](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#safety).

## Which one should I choose?

Start with what bothers you most, then check your timing. If only your abdomen bothers you, start with a tummy tuck. If your breasts and abdomen both do, ask about a mommy makeover. If you may want more children or plan to lose a lot of weight, consider waiting. Your surgeon decides at consultation.

| If this sounds like you | It points to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your abdomen bothers you and your breasts don't | A tummy tuck | It treats skin, fat and, in most cases, muscle |
| Your breasts and abdomen both changed | A mommy makeover | It combines a breast procedure with surgery on the abdomen |
| Loose skin on the abdomen, plus fat on your hips or waist | A tummy tuck with liposuction | Ask how much liposuction fits in one surgery |
| Your breasts changed and your abdomen didn't | A breast procedure alone | See the [breast lift](/procedures/breast-lift-miami) and [breast augmentation](/procedures/breast-augmentation-miami) pages |
| Extra fat, but little loose skin | Possibly liposuction alone | See [how a tummy tuck compares with liposuction](/blog/tummy-tuck-vs-liposuction) |
| You may want more children, or plan to lose a lot of weight | Waiting | ASPS advises postponing a tummy tuck |

These are starting points, not a diagnosis. If you are also weighing a BBL, read [how a tummy tuck compares with a BBL](/blog/tummy-tuck-vs-bbl-miami).

## How do I find out which one fits me?

A consultation settles it. Your surgeon examines your abdomen and breasts, reviews your health, your weight and your pregnancy plans, and tells you which surgeries fit and whether they belong in one operation. Bring these questions, so you leave knowing what each part of the plan adds to your recovery and your risk.

1. Which type of tummy tuck would I need, and does it include muscle repair?
2. Does my plan fit in one surgery, and how long will it take?
3. Where will the surgery be done? Before office surgery under sedation or general anesthesia, Florida requires the surgeon to name, in writing, the hospital where they have privileges or a transfer agreement.
4. Will I have drains, and which garments will I wear, for how long?

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a tummy tuck be part of a mommy makeover?

Yes. ASPS lists a tummy tuck among the possible procedures in a mommy makeover, along with breast augmentation, a breast lift, buttock augmentation, labiaplasty and liposuction. A plan doesn't have to include one, and your surgeon may suggest something smaller after examining you.

### Is a mommy tuck the same as a mommy makeover?

Not always. We found no medical society that defines mommy tuck, so treat it as an informal name, not a surgery of its own. Some people mean a tummy tuck after pregnancy, and others mean a full mommy makeover. Ask exactly which operations a plan includes. If it treats only the abdomen, it is a tummy tuck.

### Is it better to do everything at once or one surgery at a time?

There is no single right answer. ASPS says a mommy makeover is typically performed as a single-stage procedure, which means one recovery. The studies above found more complications with combined procedures and longer surgery, though the 2026 review was more reassuring. Your surgeon decides whether to split your plan.

### How long after having a baby can I have either one?

An ASPS article says there is no one steadfast rule. Kristy Hamilton, MD, quoted there, tells patients to wait at least 6 months after delivery, longer if their body is still changing, and to have stopped breastfeeding for at least 6 weeks, preferably 3 months. Nneamaka Nwubah, MD, also quoted, calls 1 year the sweet spot.

### Can I get pregnant after a tummy tuck or mommy makeover?

Yes, though it can change your result. A 2023 review of 17 studies and 237 patients who became pregnant after a tummy tuck found no deaths of mothers or babies and concluded that pregnancy should not be ruled out ([Karunaratne and colleagues, Aesthetic Plastic Surgery, 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37266593/)). The Aesthetic Society still says pregnancy may compromise your results.
