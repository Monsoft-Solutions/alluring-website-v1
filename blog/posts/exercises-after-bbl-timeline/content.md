## When can I exercise after a BBL?

Start walking within a day or two, hold off on anything strenuous for about the first month, and go back to normal exercise at about 8 weeks, once your surgeon clears you. That outline comes from [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/butt-lift/aftercare-recovery), [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23308-brazilian-butt-lift) and an [ASPS article by Kamran Azad, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/recovering-from-a-brazilian-butt-lift). Your own surgeon may move these dates, and their instructions come first.

| When | What you can do | Who says it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 or 2 | Short walks | The Aesthetic Society, ASPS article by Kamran Azad, MD |
| First month | No heavy lifting or strenuous activity | Cleveland Clinic |
| After 1 month | Light activity such as fast-paced walking | ASPS article by Kamran Azad, MD |
| First 4 to 6 weeks | Nothing strenuous, including lifting | The Aesthetic Society |
| At least 8 weeks | No direct pressure on your buttocks | The Aesthetic Society |
| After 8 weeks | Normal activities and exercise, once your surgeon clears you | The Aesthetic Society |

![Timeline card titled BBL exercise timeline. Day 1 or 2: short walks. First month: no heavy lifting or strenuous activity. After 1 month: light activity such as fast-paced walking. After 8 weeks: normal activities and exercise, once your surgeon clears you. Sources: The Aesthetic Society, Cleveland Clinic, ASPS.](IMAGE:timeline)

The sources don't match exactly. [ASPS's recovery page](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/buttock-enhancement/recovery) says you can generally resume exercise after six to eight weeks. Cleveland Clinic describes only light exercise at about the 8-week mark. Read week 8 as the point where you start building back, not the day you pick up your old weights.

## How soon can you walk after a BBL?

Within a day or two, and you should. The Aesthetic Society says you should be able to get up and walk after the second day, which helps prevent blood clots. The Azad article says most people are up and walking a day or two after surgery. For the first four to five days, a [plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/six-things-you-need-to-know-about-recovering-from-a-brazilian-butt-lift) keeps it simple: around the house or, at most, the neighborhood.

No source we read sets a target in minutes or steps. Walk often, stay on flat ground and rest between walks. Here is the order things come back in:

- **First:** walking
- **Next:** fast-paced walking
- **Last, once your surgeon clears you:** squats, lunges and running

## Can you use a bike or seated machines after a BBL?

Not until your surgeon clears you, which the sources put at about 8 weeks. A bike seat or weight bench is direct pressure. The Aesthetic Society says to avoid direct pressure on the buttocks for at least 8 weeks. That is why seats wait.

![Card titled Why seats wait, with a simple bench icon. A bike seat or weight bench is direct pressure. The Aesthetic Society says to avoid direct pressure on the buttocks for at least 8 weeks. Source: The Aesthetic Society.](IMAGE:seats)

None of our sources names a bike, a rowing machine or a leg press, so this is our plain reading of the pressure rule, not a line from a study. The surgeon interviewed by ASPS also keeps any sitting to about 10 minutes at a time for the first six weeks, which rules out a spin class. Our post on [how long to wait before you sit](/how-long-after-bbl-can-i-sit) explains why pressure matters.

What about your upper body? No source sets a date. Cleveland Clinic says heavy lifting waits for one month, and The Aesthetic Society says nothing strenuous, including lifting, for four to six weeks. After that, if your surgeon agrees, light work done standing keeps the pressure off.

![A woman in a long-sleeved top and loose joggers stands in a bright room, holding a light resistance band at chest height.](IMAGE:standing)

## When can you do squats and train your glutes after a BBL?

After your surgeon clears you, at about 8 weeks at the earliest, and start light. Cleveland Clinic says you may have to add lower body exercises that focus on your butt, including leg lifts, lunges and squats, but it gives no date for them.

Running and jumping wait as well. The Azad article says to avoid high impact activities, "especially any type of bouncing," and that more vigorous activity such as jogging or running can resume once the fat cells have stabilized. It doesn't say when that is, so ask.

Ask your surgeon before you restart:

- Squats, lunges and leg lifts
- Hip thrusts and heavy deadlifts
- Running and jumping rope
- Stationary bikes and rowing machines
- Seated weights and leg presses

![Checklist card titled Ask your surgeon before you restart: squats, lunges and leg lifts; hip thrusts and heavy deadlifts; running and jumping rope; stationary bikes and rowing machines; seated weights and leg presses.](IMAGE:restart)

Can training grow your result? No source we read says glute work makes grafted fat grow, or that it shrinks it. What the sources do link to your result is weight: Cleveland Clinic says to maintain a consistent weight, and [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/buttock-enhancement/procedure) says the transferred fat can grow or shrink as you gain or lose weight. If you go back to hard cardio, watch that your weight holds steady.

## What is still unknown about exercise after a BBL?

More than most timelines admit. Here is what we couldn't find:

- **Tested dates.** We found no study comparing exercise timelines after a BBL. Week 8 is society guidance, not a trial result.
- **Limits for intensity.** No source we read sets a heart rate, a weight limit or a number of minutes. If you see those figures online, ask where they come from.
- **The cost of going early.** No source measures how much fat, if any, early exercise costs.
- **Swimming.** No source we read gives a date, and your incisions have to heal first. Ask your surgeon.

## When should you stop and call your surgeon?

Stop if something hurts more, not less, and call for any of the signs below. Trouble breathing, chest pain or an unusual heartbeat is an emergency, whether or not you were exercising: ASPS says to seek medical attention immediately.

| What you notice | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| More pain or swelling that doesn't settle after activity | Stop, rest and call your surgeon |
| Pain that is extreme or long-lasting | Call your surgeon |
| A lump that hurts or seems to grow | Call your surgeon |
| Fever above 101°F, spreading redness or discharge | Call your surgeon the same day |
| Leg pain, swelling or warmth | Seek medical care right away |
| Trouble breathing, chest pain or an unusual heartbeat | Seek medical attention immediately. Call 911 |

The lump signs come from [Cleveland Clinic's page on fat necrosis](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/24187-fat-necrosis), the infection signs from its [page on surgical wound infection](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/surgical-wound-infection), and the leg and breathing signs from its [page on pulmonary embolism](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/17400-pulmonary-embolism).

## Frequently asked questions

### Will exercise ruin my BBL results?

The sources encourage the right kind at the right time: The Aesthetic Society says walking after the second day helps prevent blood clots. The concern is pressure and bouncing too early. No study we found measures how much early exercise costs in fat, so stick to walking and light activity until your surgeon clears you.

### Can I use seated machines after a BBL?

Not until your surgeon clears you. A seat is direct pressure on your buttocks, and The Aesthetic Society says to avoid that for at least 8 weeks. That covers stationary bikes, rowing machines, leg presses and seated weights. No source names these machines, so this is our reading of the pressure rule. Ask your surgeon.

### When can I start doing squats after a BBL?

At about 8 weeks at the earliest, once your surgeon clears you. The Aesthetic Society says normal activities and exercise can resume after 8 weeks, and Cleveland Clinic describes light exercise at about that point. Start with low weight or none, and build up. No source gives a week-by-week squat plan.

### Can I work out in my compression garment?

For the first two months you will probably be in it anyway: a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS advises day and night wear in month 1 and at least 12 hours a day in month 2, or as your surgeon directs. No source we read covers workouts specifically, so ask your surgeon what to wear.

### When can I go back to the gym after a BBL?

The Aesthetic Society says normal activities and exercise can resume after 8 weeks, and ASPS's recovery page says generally after six to eight weeks. Both assume your surgeon has cleared you. Start with standing work and lighter weights than you left with. If your surgeon clears you for walking sooner, a flat treadmill may work. Ask first.

### What exercises help maintain a BBL shape?

No study we found tests one exercise against another for this. Cleveland Clinic says you may have to add lower body exercises such as leg lifts, lunges and squats, and it says to maintain a consistent weight to preserve your result. Steady weight is the part the sources are clearest on. Start once your surgeon clears you.
