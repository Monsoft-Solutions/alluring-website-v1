This vital network collects excess fluid from your body's tissues and returns it to your bloodstream, according to [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/21199-lymphatic-system).

## What Safety Tips Should You Follow for BBL Massages?

While lymphatic massages can help with swelling, proper safety protocols are critical to prevent complications.

Ask your surgeon which areas are off limits.

Maintaining strict hygiene standards is essential. Massage sessions require clean hands and sanitized equipment to minimize infection risks.

Notify your qualified healthcare provider immediately if you experience discomfort, so they can make appropriate adjustments to the treatment protocol.

Focus on gentle movements that support lymphatic drainage without traumatizing surgical sites. The aim is to try to reduce swelling and facilitate better drainage.

Complement these sessions with a healthy diet and proper hydration.

Before your first session, ask your surgeon:

- Do you want me to have lymphatic massage?
- How many sessions, and when do they start?
- Which areas are off limits?
- Who do you trust to do it?
