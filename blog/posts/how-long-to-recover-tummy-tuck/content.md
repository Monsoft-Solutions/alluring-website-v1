## How long does it take to recover from a tummy tuck?

Plan on about 2 weeks off work and around 3 months before you feel fully recovered. [Cindy Wu, MD, on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/answers-to-common-tummy-tuck-questions), says patients typically return to work in about 2 weeks, and [Samir Rao, MD, also on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/understanding-the-different-types-of-tummy-tucks), says a full recovery takes around 3 months. A mini tummy tuck is usually quicker, and an extended one slower. Treat 3 months as a guide: [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/tummy-tuck/aftercare-recovery) says recovery occurs over 6 months or more and varies greatly among individuals.

The first week is the hardest. The Aesthetic Society says it will be the worst, and that you will still feel like you are recovering for about 2 to 3 weeks. Your age, general health, type of tummy tuck and post-surgery care all change your dates, says [Shahram Salemy, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/what-you-need-to-know-about-your-tummy-tuck-recovery), so your surgeon gives you the timeline that applies to you. If you smoke, stop well before surgery: [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/procedures/11017-tummy-tuck) gives, as an example, at least 1 month before and 2 weeks after, and Salemy says to avoid smoking and alcohol for at least 3 months after. Your surgeon sets your dates.

## What is the recovery time for a mini tummy tuck?

Most people work again in 1 to 2 weeks and return to more vigorous activity in about 5 to 6 weeks, according to Samir Rao, MD. The Aesthetic Society agrees that recovery is typically longest after a traditional tummy tuck rather than a mini.

Rao calls a mini less invasive: it treats only the area below the belly button, through a smaller incision. Cleveland Clinic puts [the scar](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar) at about the length of a C-section scar, 3 to 6 inches, and says there is generally no cut around the belly button. The trade-off, Rao says, is that the result is limited to the lower abdomen. If you are weighing it up, the page sets out [which type of tummy tuck suits which body](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#which-tummy-tuck) and [the price of each type](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#pricing).

## Full and extended tummy tuck recovery

A full tummy tuck takes a couple of weeks before non-strenuous work and around 3 months to recover fully. An extended one takes longer: the more extensive the surgery, the longer recovery may take, says Salemy.

|  | Mini | Full | Extended |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Back to work | 1 to 2 weeks | A couple of weeks, non-strenuous work | 4 to 6 weeks |
| Harder activity | About 5 to 6 weeks | Not stated by Rao; Wu says 6 weeks for running or lifting | About 12 weeks more after that |
| Fully recovered | Not stated | Around 3 months | Not stated |

Except where it names Wu, the table comes from Rao, the only source we found that splits time off by type. Read it as one surgeon's guide, not a rule.

## When can you go back to work, drive and pick up your kids?

Many people work again in about 2 weeks and can often drive after 1 to 2 weeks, but lifting your kids waits at least 4 to 6 weeks. Your surgeon clears each one.

### Going back to work

Sources range from 1 week to a month, so plan around your job.

- **At least 1 week.** Cleveland Clinic's average.
- **About 2 weeks.** Cindy Wu, MD, and The Aesthetic Society, which adds that it depends on the physical demands of your job.
- **Up to a month.** Shahram Salemy, MD, says most people opt for this, and that a physically demanding job needs plenty of time off.

If you sit at a desk, 2 weeks is a fair plan. If you lift, stand all day or care for others at work, ask your surgeon before you promise your employer a date.

### Driving

Salemy says driving, cooking and shopping are often manageable after 1 to 2 weeks, and your surgeon tells you when you are clear to drive. If your tummy tuck is part of combined surgery, read [how long after a mommy makeover you can drive](/how-long-after-mommy-makeover-can-i-drive). For the gym, see [exercise after a tummy tuck, week by week](/blog/exercises-after-tummy-tuck-miami).

### Picking up your kids

This is the part to plan first. The Aesthetic Society says that if you have small children, you must put someone else totally in charge of their care for at least 2 weeks, and that heavy lifting, such as picking up your children or baskets of laundry, waits 4 to 6 weeks. Salemy strongly advises against squatting or picking up children for the first few weeks, Wu says lifting should wait 6 weeks, and [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar) says heavy lifting usually waits 4 to 8 weeks, until your provider says it is safe. So if your child still needs carrying, line up help for those weeks too, not just the first 2.

None of these sources gives a weight limit, so ask your surgeon what is too heavy, and say how much your child weighs.

![Kids and work card on warm stone. Small children: someone else in charge for at least 2 weeks. Heavy lifting, including your kids: at least 4 to 6 weeks, sometimes 8. Driving: often manageable after 1 to 2 weeks. Work: about 2 weeks, longer for a physical job. The sources we read set no weight limit, so ask your surgeon.](IMAGE:kids-work)

These steps come from common sense, not studies:

- **Line up the school runs, baths and nights** before surgery, not after.
- **Teach older kids** to climb into the car seat on their own, and to sit beside you rather than on your lap.
- **Keep what you need at waist height,** so you don't have to bend or squat.
- **Cook and freeze meals ahead,** so the first weeks don't depend on you.

### How long you need help at home

Plan for more than the minimum. ASPS says someone should drive you home and stay for at least the first night. The Aesthetic Society says to arrange help around the house and with your medication for at least the first 3 days, and to keep plenty of help on hand, because you won't feel like yourself for at least 7 to 10 days. [Paul Vitenas, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/five-things-to-consider-before-getting-a-tummy-tuck), adds chores and children to the list.

## How should you sleep after a tummy tuck?

Slightly bent at the waist, with your upper body raised, until your surgeon says you can lie flat. Cindy Wu, MD, says you stay bent at the waist while standing or lying down for 7 to 10 days, to protect the incision and let your skin adapt. Shahram Salemy, MD, says you rest at an angle for the first few days, and Cleveland Clinic says your surgeon will tell you how to sit or lie down with the least pain.

At home, that usually means sleeping on your back, propped on pillows or a wedge with a pillow under your knees, or in a recliner. That setup is common practice rather than something these sources spell out. We found no source that gives a date for sleeping on your side or stomach, so ask at your follow-up.

## How long does swelling last after a tummy tuck?

Most of it goes in the first weeks and more by about 3 months, though some can linger longer. Sources differ.

- **Weeks.** The Aesthetic Society says redness, bruising and swelling usually subside in 1 to 3 weeks, and that swelling should subside within 5 weeks.
- **About 3 months.** Wu says swelling keeps going down until about 3 months, and Salemy says it can take up to 3 months to go down completely.
- **Longer.** Vitenas says residual swelling can linger for 6 months or longer.

Swelling low on your belly has its own guide: [pubic swelling after a tummy tuck](/how-to-reduce-pubic-swelling-after-tummy-tuck). So does the pulling feeling that Cleveland Clinic says will go away: [tightness after a tummy tuck](/how-to-reduce-tightness-after-tummy-tuck).

## Does liposuction or muscle repair make recovery longer?

Adding other procedures usually does: The Aesthetic Society says recovery is typically longest for people who combine a tummy tuck with other surgery. For muscle repair, we found no source that times recovery with and without it. [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck) says a tummy tuck restores weakened or separated muscles in most cases, and Wu and Rao both describe a full tummy tuck with repair, so their figures above already include it. See [a tummy tuck with or without muscle repair](/what-is-a-tummy-tuck-without-muscle-repair-called).

On risk, the evidence about liposuction is mixed:

- **Slightly higher.** In an insurance database of 25,478 tummy tucks, major complications followed 3.1% of those done alone and 3.8% of those combined with liposuction ([Winocour and colleagues, 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26505716/)).
- **Not higher.** A review of 17 studies and 14,061 patients found no evidence that adding liposuction raised complications ([Xia and colleagues, 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30511162/)).
- **Blood clots.** A review found they occur more often after a tummy tuck than after any other cosmetic surgery, and more often still with liposuction ([Mittal and colleagues, 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31858207/)).

In a doctor's office in Florida, liposuction combined with a tummy tuck may not exceed 1,000 cc of supernatant fat, meaning the fat alone once the fluid has separated out ([Rule 64B8-9.009](https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=64B8-9.009)). The page has more on [tummy tuck safety](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#safety). If you have used a GLP-1, read about [a tummy tuck after Ozempic](/blog/ozempic-tummy-tuck-myths-miami).

## Tummy tuck recovery myths, checked

- **"You'll be on bed rest for weeks."** No. Wu says you can walk the day of surgery, and The Aesthetic Society says you should be up and walking the day after. Moving helps prevent blood clots.
- **"You can't lift your kids for a year."** No. Heavy lifting waits 4 to 6 weeks, according to The Aesthetic Society, 6 weeks, according to Wu, or usually 4 to 8 weeks, according to Cleveland Clinic.
- **"The pain stays bad for weeks."** Usually not. The Aesthetic Society says discomfort drops to a nagging level by days 5 to 7, and Wu says most patients manage with just a few days of pain medication.
- **"Drains stay in for a month."** Usually not, and you may not have them. Wu says usually 1 to 2 weeks, The Aesthetic Society says 3 to 14 days, and Vitenas says most come out in 7 to 10 days but some stay 2 weeks or longer. See [what tummy tuck drains are and how long they stay](/blog/tummy-tuck-drains-what-they-are-how-long-they-stay).
- **"The scar never fades."** Partly true. Wu says it turns thinner and lighter at around 12 to 18 months, but Cleveland Clinic says most people still have a visible scar. [Jonathan Weiler, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/your-guide-to-a-tummy-tuck-before-during-and-after), says it is placed low so underwear or swimsuit bottoms conceal it.
- **"You can never get pregnant after."** Not true. A 2023 review of 17 studies and 237 patients found no deaths of mothers or newborns and concluded pregnancy should not be ruled out, though it did record complications and followed women for 8.5 months on average ([Karunaratne and colleagues](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37266593/)). A 2024 study linked a past tummy tuck to higher odds of preterm delivery ([Cheng and colleagues](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38447678/)). ASPS still advises women who may want more children to postpone a tummy tuck, and Wu says a pregnancy may change your result.

## When should you call your surgeon?

Call right away for a fever, an increase in swelling, pain, redness, drainage or bleeding, dizziness, nausea or vomiting, or pain and swelling in one leg. Sudden shortness of breath, chest pain, an unusual heartbeat, coughing up blood or fainting is an emergency.

| What you notice | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Soreness that eases by days 5 to 7 | Normal. Take your medicine on schedule |
| Bruising, numbness, tightness or pulling | Common, and it usually fades. Mention it at your follow-up |
| Walking bent over for the first 7 to 10 days | Normal |
| Fluid collecting and swelling at the surgical site (a seroma, seen in 4.1% of patients in Xia's review) | Not an emergency, but tell your surgeon. If it is getting worse, call right away |
| Fever of 100.4°F (38°C) or more, or pus or smelly fluid from an incision | Call your surgeon right away |
| Swelling, pain, redness, drainage or bleeding that is getting worse | Call your surgeon right away |
| Dizziness, nausea or vomiting, past the first few hours after anesthesia | Call your surgeon right away |
| Swelling, pain or warmth in one leg | Call your surgeon right away or go to the emergency room: it can be a blood clot |
| Sudden shortness of breath, chest pain, an unusual heartbeat, coughing up blood or fainting | Call 911 |

The list draws on The Aesthetic Society and on Cleveland Clinic's pages on [the tummy tuck](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/procedures/11017-tummy-tuck), [surgical drains](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/21104-jackson-pratt-jp-drain) and [blood clots in the leg](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/16911-deep-vein-thrombosis-dvt). Your surgeon's own instructions come first.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the worst week of tummy tuck recovery?

The first. The Aesthetic Society says the first two days are the most uncomfortable, that discomfort drops to a nagging level by days 5 to 7, and that you will still feel like you are recovering for about 2 to 3 weeks. Taking your medicine on schedule and having help at home make those days easier.

### How long do you wear a compression garment after a tummy tuck?

Usually 4 to 6 weeks, according to Cindy Wu, MD, on the ASPS website. Jonathan Weiler, MD, describes wearing it all the time for the first 2 weeks, then as much as possible for another month. It helps control swelling and supports your skin as it heals. Your surgeon tells you when you can stop.

### How long is recovery from a tummy tuck with muscle repair?

Plan on a couple of weeks before non-strenuous work and around 3 months to recover fully, according to Samir Rao, MD, on the ASPS website, whose standard tummy tuck includes muscle repair. Cindy Wu, MD, whose description also includes repair, says running or lifting should wait 6 weeks. We found no source comparing recovery with and without repair.

### When will I see my final tummy tuck results?

Cleveland Clinic says it can take up to 3 months to see the final result, and ASPS says swelling can hide it at first. Paul Vitenas, MD, on the ASPS website, says complete results can take a full year. The scar keeps improving for up to 1 year, Cleveland Clinic says, and Cindy Wu, MD, says it turns thinner and lighter at around 12 to 18 months.

### How long does a tummy tuck last?

Cleveland Clinic calls a tummy tuck permanent, but says weight fluctuations can affect the result over time. Cindy Wu, MD, says results stay stable with a stable weight, and that weight changes or a pregnancy may change them. ASPS also advises women who may want more children to postpone surgery.

### Can I fly home after a tummy tuck?

Ask your surgeon before you buy a ticket. Alluring confirms your surgery, pre-op and follow-up dates in writing, and your surgeon tells you how many nights to stay in Miami before you are cleared to fly home. Blood clots are one reason to ask: a 2020 review found they occur more often after a tummy tuck than after any other cosmetic surgery.

### When can I shower after a tummy tuck?

Ask before you go home, because no source we read sets one date for everyone. The Aesthetic Society says your surgeon should give you detailed instructions on managing drains, changing bandages and showering, and ASPS lists "How will I bathe?" among the questions to ask.
