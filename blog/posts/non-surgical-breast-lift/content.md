## Is there a non-surgical breast lift?

Not one that does what surgery does. A surgical lift cuts away stretched skin and moves the nipple higher, and treatments sold as non-surgical don't remove skin. The closest alternatives heat tissue under the skin through small incisions, so they are less surgical rather than non-surgical, and their evidence is a few small studies that reviewers disagree about. Creams and exercise haven't been shown to lift breasts, and a bra holds them up only while you wear it.

## What does a surgical breast lift do that other treatments can't?

A surgical lift takes away excess skin and repositions the nipple. [ASPS describes it](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-lift/procedure) this way: "Excess breast skin is removed to compensate for a loss of elasticity," and "The nipple and areola are repositioned to a natural, more youthful height." Our procedure page explains [how a lift reshapes the breast](/procedures/breast-lift-miami#what-is-a-breast-lift).

The [FDA defines non-invasive body contouring](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/non-invasive-body-contouring-technologies) as "non-surgical procedures that do not remove any tissue (fat or skin) from the body." So the real question for any alternative is how much it can tighten the skin that stays.

[ASPS lists](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-lift/candidates) nipples that fall below the breast crease when unsupported as a sign you may be a candidate for surgery, and our guide shows [how to check where yours sit](/blog/breast-lift-candidate-checklist).

## What are the non-surgical breast lift options, and what does the evidence say?

As of November 2026, most of the studies we found tested under-the-skin radiofrequency or threads on sagging breasts, and those are small. One [2025 clinic dataset](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40115616/) reports 231 women who had a "laser-assisted" lift, followed for 3 months.

| Option | What it claims to do | What we found | FDA status found |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Radiofrequency under the skin | Tighten skin, raise the nipple | 10 women, 12 months: nipple 1.6 to 2.1 cm nearer the neck notch ([Unger, 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36103661/)). Maker supported the study | Called off-label for breasts in a [2022 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35081544/) |
| Helium plasma radiofrequency under the skin | Tighten skin, raise the nipple | 15 women, 180 days: 1.2 cm nearer ([Sterodimas, 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40236884/)). Maker funded the study | Cleared for loose skin of the neck and under the chin, to coagulate tissue under the skin after liposuction for body contouring, and (a separate handpiece) for wrinkles. [The FDA's update](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/safety-communications/update-use-renuvionj-plasma-device-certain-aesthetic-procedures-fda-safety-communication) doesn't name the breast |
| Radiofrequency, laser or ultrasound on the skin | Firmer skin, no incisions | No study of these alone on sagging breasts found | [The FDA's body contouring page](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/non-invasive-body-contouring-technologies), mainly about fat, circumference and cellulite: no such device is cleared or approved for the breasts |

## Is radiofrequency a non-surgical breast lift?

Not strictly, because the radiofrequency treatments studied on sagging breasts were done under sedation or anesthesia, through small incisions.

- **A 2022 pilot study.** In [Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36103661/), 10 women had one treatment in an operating room through 3- to 4-mm incisions. At 12 months, the distance from the notch at the base of the neck to the nipple was 1.6 to 2.1 cm shorter, depending on the side and how it was measured. Two authors consult for the device maker, which supported the study.
- **A 2025 study.** In [Aesthetic Surgery Journal Open Forum](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40236884/), 15 women with A or B cups had helium plasma under sedation, through three incisions per breast. At 180 days that distance was 1.2 cm shorter. All 15 reported less sagging, though on the study's scale 9 stayed at mild and 6 reached none. The authors call it "not a surgical equivalent" and the results "subtle."
- **A critical review.** A [2026 review in Aesthetic Plastic Surgery](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41053477/) re-measured published photos from three studies and found that distance 2 mm shorter on each side, not a significant difference. Its author calls these devices "ineffective" and the studies "heavily affected by commercial bias." He worked from photos rather than patients, and he is a surgeon in private practice. He also wrote the 2022 review cited in this article, so these are one surgeon's assessments.
- **A more hopeful review.** A [2026 review in Cureus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42317893/) lists "energy-based mastopexy" among its "emerging front runners," while noting the evidence comes almost entirely from single-arm studies.

Our read: these treatments may make a modest difference for some women with mild sagging, the size of that difference is disputed, and we found no trial comparing them directly with a surgical lift.

Off-label means outside what a device was cleared for, and the author of a [2022 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35081544/) says inserting a heated probe under the skin of the breasts is off-label. The FDA's [May 2023 update on Renuvion/J-Plasma](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/safety-communications/update-use-renuvionj-plasma-device-certain-aesthetic-procedures-fda-safety-communication), a helium plasma system, says it warned against using it for skin contraction in March 2022, before clearing the specific uses in the table above.

## Do lasers or ultrasound on the skin lift breasts?

We found no study showing that they do on their own. The FDA's body contouring page, which describes radiofrequency, light-based and ultrasound devices mainly for reducing fat, circumference or the look of cellulite, says: "There are no FDA cleared or approved non-invasive body contouring devices for treating the breasts in males or females." A PubMed search in November 2026 found no study testing a laser on the skin as a lift by itself. The 2025 laser dataset above tracks its patients as postoperative, doesn't say how the laser was used, and gives no results in that paper.

[The Aesthetic Society's skin tightening page](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/skin-hair/skin-tightening), which doesn't mention breasts, says results are "less extensive and dramatic" than surgical body contouring and that you may need to repeat treatments every 18-24 months. For non-invasive body contouring devices used on the skin, the FDA says some complications may last a long time, become permanent or need surgery to correct. For body contouring procedures, the FDA says darker skin types can see darkening or lightening of the skin.

## Do thread lifts work on sagging breasts?

They may give a short-term lift in mild cases. A [2025 review in the Journal of Clinical Medicine](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40507620/) says the effect tends to fade within a year, though the papers it cites for that aren't breast studies, and the evidence is thin.

The one clinical report we found, a [2017 letter in the Journal of Cutaneous and Aesthetic Surgery](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29491661/), followed 10 women for 6 months. Their average sagging grade, on a scale where 3 is severe, fell from 2.3 to 0.7, with no comparison group. Another passage in the 2025 review cites up to two years. It lists asymmetry, dimpling, thread migration, granuloma formation and localized infections as risks.

## Can creams, exercises, bras or injections lift sagging breasts?

Nothing we found shows that any of them lastingly lifts the breast.

- **Creams and lotions.** We found no study of a cream lifting a sagging breast. The [FDA says](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/wrinkle-treatments-and-other-anti-aging-products) cosmetics don't need approval before sale, so "firming" on a label isn't evidence.
- **Exercise.** Breasts are glandular, connective and fatty tissue, [Cleveland Clinic explains](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/8330-breast-anatomy), with [the pectoral muscles beneath them](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/breast-massage). In a [study of 132 patients](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20354434/), a lack of regular upper body exercise wasn't a significant risk factor for sagging.
- **Bras.** "Wearing a bra doesn't prevent your breasts from sagging, and foregoing one doesn't cause your breasts to sag," says Cassann Blake, MD, a breast health specialist quoted by [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/going-braless). Cleveland Clinic's article adds that a bra "temporarily changes the shape of your breasts," so wearing one is an easy way to look lifted while you have it on.
- **Massage.** "No conclusive evidence shows that breast massage improves or prevents sagging breasts," [Cleveland Clinic says](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/breast-massage).
- **Injections.** The [FDA recommends against](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/dermal-fillers-soft-tissue-fillers) any injectable filler to increase breast size, and says silicone injections can cause serious injuries, including stroke and death. We found no study of an injection lifting a sagging breast. A [2026 review in Dermatologic Surgery](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42975435/) describes collagen-stimulating fillers being used off the face, including on the breasts and chest wall, and says most off-face uses remain off-label.

## Who might be satisfied with less than a surgical lift?

Women with mild sagging, smaller breasts and modest goals. These treatments were mostly studied in women like them, and the change is small. The 2025 authors say helium plasma is "most useful in a subset of patients with small breasts requiring mild lift." The 2022 authors say their treatment is "not ideal for patients with large, pendulous breasts desiring reduction, or patients with very severe ptosis or massive weight loss."

If you want a lasting change, ASPS says a surgical lift's results [will be long-lasting](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-lift/results), though breasts keep changing with age and gravity. If scars worry you, see [where lift scars sit and how they fade](/blog/breast-lift-scars-miami) and how the [types of breast lift](/blog/types-of-breast-lift) differ. If you'd like more fullness, read about [a lift without implants](/blog/breast-lift-without-implants). If you're planning more children, [timing matters](/blog/breast-lift-miami-post-pregnancy).

Doing nothing is a perfectly good choice too. Sagging is a normal change, and it isn't something you need to fix.

## What should you ask before a non-surgical breast treatment?

Ask what the treatment is, what it's cleared for and what change the evidence supports, before you pay. The FDA itself advises questions 1 and 4.

1. **Which device or product will you use?**
2. **Is it FDA-cleared for the breast, or off-label?**
3. **How much change should I expect, from which study, and who paid for it?**
4. **How many treatments will I need, how long will the effects last, and will I need more to keep them?**
5. **What can go wrong, and who treats me if it does?** The 2022 pilot authors list burns, sensation changes and pigmentation changes.
6. **Could it affect my mammograms or a lift later?** We found no study of a lift after these treatments, and the 2025 study's authors say long-term effects on breast tissue and imaging still need study.
7. **What is the total cost, with anesthesia and repeat sessions?**

After a treatment under the skin, [Cleveland Clinic's list for a surgical lift](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23298-breast-lift) is a sensible guide. Call the person who treated you immediately if a breast becomes red, hot, swollen or painful, or if you have bleeding, drainage, redness or swelling at an incision, dizziness, a fever higher than 100.5 degrees Fahrenheit, severe pain that medicine doesn't help, or nausea or vomiting. If you can't reach them, go to an emergency room. Call 911 or go to the emergency room for new or severe chest pain or trouble breathing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a non-surgical breast lift permanent?

No. The longest follow-up in the breast studies we read was 12 months, and a 2025 review says thread effects tend to decline within 6–12 months. A surgical lift isn't forever either, as breasts keep changing with age and gravity, but it does remove skin, which these treatments don't.

### How much does a non-surgical breast lift cost?

We found no society average for treating the breasts, so we can't give you a fair number. The author of a 2022 review says one under-the-skin radiofrequency treatment of the breasts costs "about the same as a mastopexy," the medical name for a lift. Our [breast lift cost guide](/blog/breast-lift-cost) covers surgery.

### Is there a breast lift without scars?

Not a surgical one. ASPS says incision lines are permanent, though in most cases they fade and improve significantly over time. Under-the-skin radiofrequency uses much smaller incisions, but those are still incisions. Our [gallery of real before and after photos](/gallery/breast-lift) shows surgical lifts.

### Is a non-surgical breast lift safe?

It isn't risk-free, and we can't give a reliable rate. The radiofrequency breast studies reported no complications and the thread report only bruising, but the 2025 study's authors say their first check at 30 days likely came after minor effects had passed. Across body areas, a [2025 review of helium plasma](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40768710/) by the same critic counted 15 life-threatening events and 3 deaths in reports to the FDA, frequent full-thickness burns, and case reports of helium entering the chest and breast.

### Can you trust non-surgical breast lift before and after photos?

The 2025 study authors say arm position and camera angle varied in their own photos, and a 2022 review says some published photos didn't accurately depict the treatment result. Compare the same pose, lighting and distance, and ask how long after treatment the second photo was taken.

### Will losing weight or building muscle lift my breasts?

No source we read says so. In a review of 132 surgery patients, weight loss of more than 50 lbs and a higher BMI were both linked to more sagging, and a lack of regular upper body exercise wasn't a significant risk factor. None of this tested whether losing weight or exercising lifts breasts. For timing, see [a breast lift after weight loss](/blog/breast-lift-recovery-weight-loss-miami).
