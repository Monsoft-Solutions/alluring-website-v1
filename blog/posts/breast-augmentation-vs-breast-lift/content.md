## Do I need a breast lift or a breast augmentation?

You likely need a lift if your breasts have dropped and you like their size, and an augmentation if they sit where you want but you'd like more size or fullness. The [American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS)](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-lift) says a lift "does not significantly change the size of your breasts or round out the upper part of your breast." It [also says](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-augmentation) augmentation "does not correct severely drooping breasts." If you want your breasts both higher and fuller, you may need both.

These signs come from ASPS's candidate pages for [a lift](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-lift/candidates) and [an augmentation](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-augmentation/candidates). The last two rows are our own reading of the two lists. Use this to start the conversation, not to diagnose yourself: your surgeon decides at consultation.

| What you notice | Points toward |
| --- | --- |
| Your nipples fall below the breast crease when unsupported | A lift |
| Your nipples and areolas point downward | A lift |
| Stretched skin and enlarged areolas | A lift |
| You feel your breasts are too small | Implants |
| The upper part of your breast looks empty | Implants |
| Shape and volume lost after pregnancy, weight loss or with age | Either one, depending on where your nipple sits |
| Nipples below the crease, and you also want more fullness | A lift with implants |

For the mirror check, the pencil test and what they can't tell you, read [how to tell if you need a breast lift](/blog/breast-lift-candidate-checklist). The procedure page explains [who a breast lift suits](/procedures/breast-lift-miami#candidate).

## What does a breast lift do, and what doesn't it do?

A lift raises the breast and moves the nipple up, but it doesn't add size. ASPS says it "raises the breasts by removing excess skin and tightening the surrounding tissue," and it can reduce an areola that has enlarged. Cleveland Clinic's health library says a lift usually takes 1–2 hours, while [its surgery center](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/cosmetic-plastic-surgery/procedures/breast-lift) says 2.5 to 3. The library adds that [most people go home the same day](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23298-breast-lift). The procedure page explains [what a breast lift involves](/procedures/breast-lift-miami#what-is-a-breast-lift), and our guide to [a breast lift without implants](/blog/breast-lift-without-implants) covers who it suits on its own.

## What does a breast augmentation do?

An augmentation increases breast size with implants or fat transfer, and ASPS says it can "restore breast volume lost after weight reduction or pregnancy." The [FDA says](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/breast-implants/breast-implant-surgery) implant surgery "can last from one to several hours depending on the procedure and personal circumstances." The [FDA approves](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/breast-implants/types-breast-implants) saline implants for cosmetic breast surgery from age 18 and silicone gel implants from age 22. See [how breast augmentation is planned](/procedures/breast-augmentation-miami) on the procedure page.

## Can implants alone lift sagging breasts?

Not when the breast has really dropped. Lawrence Iteld, MD, [writing on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/what-are-the-benefits-of-combining-breast-augmentation-and-a-breast-lift), says "augmentation on its own isn't enough to address sagging breasts." ASPS's own wording is "severely drooping," so if your sagging is mild, ask whether an implant alone could be enough.

One risk of an implant alone on a sagging breast is a double contour, also called a waterfall deformity, where the implant and the breast tissue read as two separate shapes. In a [study of 200 patients](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20224340/) having a first cosmetic augmentation with silicone implants, it appeared in 7%, most of them minor (de Chardon and colleagues, Annals of Plastic Surgery, 2010). Implant placement matters too: the authors tie it most to under-the-muscle placement in sagging breasts.

## Can you have a lift and implants in the same surgery?

Often yes, though some surgeons split them. [ASPS says](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-lift/safety) implants "can be placed at the same time or as a second procedure, depending on the experience of the surgeon." Jaime Schwartz, MD, [also on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/misconceptions-about-breast-augmentation-and-lift), says that depending on your goals and how much you sag, "it may be safer to stage the procedures to allow for your body to recover in between operations."

The numbers depend on how long patients were followed:

- **First 30 days.** In a national surgical database, complications within 30 days followed 2.5% of breast lifts alone and 1.5% of lifts with implants, and reoperations were similar, 1.4% for a lift alone against 1.5% with implants ([Ali and colleagues, Annals of Plastic Surgery, 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34699429/)).
- **Longer follow-up.** A [2014 review of 23 studies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25347628/) of a lift with implants in one surgery found complications of any kind in 13.1%, most often the breast sagging again (5.2%). In the 13 studies that reported it, 10.7% had another operation. Most of its studies followed patients for under 1 year (Khavanin and colleagues, Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery, 2014).

Ask your surgeon whether one surgery or two is safer for you. Our guide to [a breast lift with implants](/blog/breast-lift-implants-miami) goes deeper.

## What changes after pregnancy?

Pregnancy can change both volume and position, so either surgery, or both, may be the answer. [ASPS says](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-lift/results) "changes that occur in the breasts during pregnancy can minimize or reverse the improvement a breast lift provides." Shahram Salemy, MD, [writing on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/better-breasts-after-pregnancy-and-breastfeeding), suggests waiting up to 6 months after breastfeeding stops, so the breasts settle into their final shape. Our guide covers [when to have a breast lift after pregnancy](/blog/breast-lift-miami-post-pregnancy).

## How do the scars compare?

A lift usually leaves more scarring, because it removes skin. [ASPS lists](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-lift/procedure) three patterns: around the areola, down to the breast crease, and along the crease as well. It says incision lines "are permanent, but in most cases will fade and significantly improve over time," and most of that fading comes in about 1 year, [Cleveland Clinic says](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/cosmetic-plastic-surgery/procedures/breast-lift).

An augmentation's incision, [ASPS says](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-augmentation/procedure), can sit along the areolar edge, in the fold under the breast or in the armpit, and "over time the incision lines will fade." See [where breast lift scars sit and how they fade](/blog/breast-lift-scars-miami).

## How do recovery and upkeep compare?

The first weeks are close, and the bigger difference is long term: implants need follow-up, and a lift adds no device to monitor.

| | Breast lift | Breast augmentation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First days | Stiff and sore for 2 to 5 days ([The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/breast/breast-lift/aftercare-recovery)) | Acute pain usually eases after one to five days ([ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-augmentation/recovery)) |
| Lifting | No lifting, driving, laundry or cleaning for the first 2 weeks (The Aesthetic Society) | No heavy lifting for at least 4 weeks ([Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/11024-breast-augmentation)) |
| Exercise | May not be cleared for up to 6 weeks (Cleveland Clinic) | Minimize hard activity for at least the first few weeks ([The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/breast/breast-augmentation/aftercare-recovery)) |
| Physical jobs | Ask your surgeon; no source gives a number | At least 3 weeks (Cleveland Clinic) |

For implants, the FDA says breast implants are not lifetime devices: [the longer you have them](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/breast-implants/things-consider-getting-breast-implants), the more likely you are to need them removed or replaced, and that cost may not be covered by insurance. With silicone gel implants, the [FDA recommends](https://www.fda.gov/media/131885/download) a first ultrasound or MRI 5–6 years after surgery, then every 2–3 years, even without symptoms. Since 2021, breast implants carry an FDA boxed warning, and your health care provider must review the FDA patient decision checklist with you, you get the chance to sign it, and the implanting doctor must sign it.

Call your surgeon right away for any of these, from Cleveland Clinic's pages on each surgery:

- A breast that becomes red, hot, swollen or painful, or one or both breasts getting larger or firmer with pain and pressure
- Bleeding, drainage, pus or redness at an incision, or stitches that open
- A fever (the lift page says higher than 100.5°F)
- Severe pain not helped by medicine
- Dizziness, nausea or vomiting

Call 911 or go to the emergency room for new or severe chest pain, trouble breathing, or pain, redness or swelling in one leg, which [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000132.htm) lists among the symptoms of a blood clot in the lung.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will a breast lift make my breasts smaller?

Not significantly, but they can look smaller. ASPS says a lift does not significantly change breast size. Lee Daniel, MD, [writing on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/whats-the-difference-between-a-breast-reduction-and-a-breast-lift), explains that because a lift repositions the breasts they "can appear smaller." If you want them smaller as well as higher, read [breast lift vs breast reduction](/blog/breast-lift-vs-reduction-miami).

### Is a lift without implants enough after pregnancy?

It can be, if you like your size and mainly want your breasts higher and firmer. ASPS says a lift doesn't round out the upper part of the breast, so if you miss fullness at the top, it suggests considering a lift with an augmentation. Your surgeon can talk through both options at the same exam.

### What are the benefits of combining a lift and implants?

You get position and fullness from one plan. Lawrence Iteld, MD, writing on the ASPS website, says combining them "means that patients only have to undergo one surgery and one recovery period," and that patients may not need as much implant volume because the lift reshapes the breast.

### How long do the results of a lift and implants last?

No source we read gives a number of years for either. ASPS says the results of a lift are long-lasting, though your breasts can keep changing with aging and gravity, and Cleveland Clinic says they should last for several years. The FDA says implants are not lifetime devices, and the pages we read give no set time for replacing them.

### Can I breastfeed after a breast lift or implants?

Often, but no one can promise a full supply. The [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/breastfeeding-special-circumstances/hcp/illnesses-conditions/breast-surgery.html) says most mothers who have had breast surgery can make some milk, though maybe not a full supply, and that implants below the muscle usually affect production less than implants above it. [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/breast/breast-lift/detailed-procedure-information) says a lift leaves the nipple attached to underlying tissue to help preserve breastfeeding.

### Does insurance cover a breast lift or implants?

Usually not, when they are cosmetic. [ASPS says](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-lift/cost) most plans do not cover a breast lift or its complications, and [also says](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-augmentation/cost) most plans will not cover cosmetic breast augmentation. It adds that some carriers exclude breast diseases in patients who have implants, so read your policy before surgery.
