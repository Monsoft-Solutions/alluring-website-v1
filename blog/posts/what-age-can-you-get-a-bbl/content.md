## What age can you get a BBL?

You must be at least 18. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23308-brazilian-butt-lift) sets that minimum and adds that you should be finished developing, because some people don't complete puberty until after 18, and that can affect your result. No source we rely on names an ideal age or an upper age limit. What decides it is your health, a stable weight and whether you have enough fat to move.

## Can you get a BBL under 18?

No. Cleveland Clinic says you must be at least 18, and Alluring doesn't perform cosmetic surgery on anyone under 18, even with a parent's consent.

A parent's consent isn't the whole test anyway. [ASPS's paper on plastic surgery for teenagers](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/briefing-papers/briefing-paper-plastic-surgery-for-teenagers) doesn't discuss BBLs, but it says parental consent is required for any plastic surgery on someone under 18, that "certain milestones in growth and physical maturity must be achieved," and that teens must show emotional maturity.

If you are under 18 and thinking about a BBL, there is no rush. You can read, ask questions and come back to it once you are 18.

## Can you get a BBL at 18 to 25?

Yes, if you meet the rest of the list. Being 18 gets you past the minimum, but it doesn't make you a candidate on its own. Cleveland Clinic also asks that you be finished developing, in good physical health, with realistic expectations and enough excess fat to transfer.

Two things are worth weighing when you are younger:

- **You may not have much fat to move.** If you don't have enough, Cleveland Clinic says you may be a good candidate for butt implants instead. A leaner patient may also suit a smaller transfer, described in the [BBL page's options](/procedures/brazilian-butt-lift-bbl-miami#options).
- **Your body will keep changing.** [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/butt-lift/results) says weight fluctuations, pregnancy and aging can affect your result, and Cleveland Clinic says you may need another BBL during your life.

You may also read that the mid-20s is the ideal time. We didn't find a source for that. Your surgeon looks at you, not your birth year.

## Can you get a BBL over 40 or over 50?

Age alone doesn't rule you out. No source we rely on sets an upper limit, and the questions are the same as at any age: your health, your weight, whether you smoke and how much fat you have.

We also found no study of BBL results or complications by age, so we can't give you a figure for your age group. The closest evidence is about a different surgery: in a [large insurance database study of tummy tucks](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26505716/), age 55 or older was one of the risk factors for major complications, along with a BMI of 30 or more and several procedures at once. That isn't a BBL finding, and a risk factor isn't a cutoff. It is a reason for a careful exam.

Two more points can matter after 40:

- **A BBL adds volume. It doesn't remove loose skin.** [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/buttock-enhancement/candidates) describes a separate buttock lift for significant looseness in that area, so ask which one fits what you see.
- **Your result ages with you.** The Aesthetic Society says the fat that survives stays for life and will grow and age with you.

## What matters more than age?

Five things decide whether a BBL is right for you, and age is only the first gate.

| What your surgeon looks at | What the source says |
| --- | --- |
| Age | At least 18 and finished developing (Cleveland Clinic) |
| Health | Good physical health (Cleveland Clinic), and a nonsmoker ([a plastic surgeon interviewed by ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/seven-things-you-need-to-know-about-a-brazilian-butt-lift)) |
| Weight | Relatively close to your ideal body weight (the same surgeon), and a weight you can keep, since Cleveland Clinic advises a consistent weight to preserve your result |
| Fat | Enough excess fat to transfer, from other parts of your body (Cleveland Clinic) |
| Expectations | Realistic expectations (Cleveland Clinic) |

We found no society cutoff for BMI before a BBL. Our [BMI guide](/bmi-calculator#bbl) shows what we recommend, and the [BBL page's candidate section](/procedures/brazilian-butt-lift-bbl-miami#candidate) explains how your surgeon weighs it. Your surgeon decides at consultation.

The sources we read tie a BBL's most serious risk, a fat embolism, to where the fat is placed. None ties it to age. The [BBL page's safety section](/procedures/brazilian-butt-lift-bbl-miami#safety) explains that risk and the questions to ask any surgeon.

## What should I ask at my consultation?

Whatever your age, these questions get you a straight answer:

1. Am I finished developing, and how can you tell?
2. Do I have enough fat, and where would it come from?
3. How does my health history change my risk?
4. Should my weight change before surgery?
5. If a BBL isn't right for me, what would you suggest instead?
6. How is my price set? The [pricing section of the BBL page](/procedures/brazilian-butt-lift-bbl-miami#pricing) explains it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get a BBL at 18?

You meet Cleveland Clinic's minimum at 18, as long as you are also finished developing, in good physical health, with realistic expectations and enough excess fat to transfer. Being 18 doesn't settle it on its own. Your surgeon examines you and decides at consultation.

### Can I get a BBL at 16 with a parent's consent?

No. Cleveland Clinic says you must be at least 18, and Alluring doesn't perform cosmetic surgery on anyone under 18, even with a parent's consent. The same goes for 17. ASPS's paper on teenagers says physical and emotional maturity matter too, so consent alone isn't the whole test.

### Am I too old for a BBL at 50?

Not because of your age alone. No source we rely on sets an upper age limit. Your surgeon looks at your general health, whether you smoke, your weight, how much fat you have and what you want to change, and decides at consultation. Ask how your own health history changes your risk.

### Does age change BBL recovery?

We found no source that gives a different BBL recovery by age, so plan on the same timeline: not on your buttocks for at least 2 weeks and back to work in 10 to 14 days, Cleveland Clinic says. The Aesthetic Society says good health, not smoking and help after surgery all support healing. The [BBL recovery guide](/how-long-to-recover-from-bbl) has the full timeline.

### How do I know if I have enough fat for a BBL?

Only an exam can tell you. Cleveland Clinic says you need enough excess fat in other parts of your body, and no source we read gives a minimum amount. If you are lean, your surgeon may discuss a smaller transfer, or Cleveland Clinic says butt implants may suit you better. Your surgeon tells you at the exam.
