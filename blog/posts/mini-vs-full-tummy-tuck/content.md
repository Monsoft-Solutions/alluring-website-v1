## What is the difference between a mini and a full tummy tuck?

A mini works only on the area below your belly button, through a short, low incision. A full works on your whole abdomen, above and below the belly button, through a longer incision, and in most cases it repairs separated muscles too. So the question is where your loose skin sits and whether your muscles have separated. If you're comparing costs, see [tummy tuck prices by type](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#pricing).

## Mini vs full tummy tuck, side by side

Here is how they compare, with the source for each row. Sources describe a mini differently in places, and we say where.

| | Mini | Full |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Area treated | Below the belly button only (Samir Rao, MD) | Above and below the belly button (Dr. Rao) |
| Scar | About the length of a C-section scar, 3 to 6 inches (Cleveland Clinic) | Hip bone to hip bone, just above the pubic area (Cleveland Clinic) |
| Belly button | Generally no cut around it (Cleveland Clinic) | You may also have a scar around it (Cleveland Clinic) |
| Muscle repair | Varies by surgeon. Not included on our price list | In most cases (ASPS) |
| Often suits | A pooch or loose skin only below the belly button, close to your ideal weight (ASPS article) | Loose skin above and below, or separated muscles (Dr. Rao) |

Cleveland Clinic's lines come from its [scar page](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/24004-tummy-tuck-scar) and its [tummy tuck page](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/procedures/11017-tummy-tuck). Dr. Rao's lines are from [his post on the website of the American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS)](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/understanding-the-different-types-of-tummy-tucks), so they are his views and not the society's. The muscle line is from [ASPS itself](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck), and the rest from [an article on its website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/theres-more-than-one-type-of-tummy-tuck).

One range is wider elsewhere. [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/tummy-tuck/incisions-and-scars) says a mini incision can run from a few inches to the entire span of the abdomen, so ask to see where yours would sit.

## Which tummy tuck do you need?

Most people need a full, according to Michelle Roughton, MD, an ASPS member surgeon quoted in that article. A mini tends to fit a smaller problem that sits low on your belly. Start with these questions, and read [who a tummy tuck suits](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#candidate):

- **Where is the loose skin?** Cleveland Clinic says a mini is for a small amount of excess fat and tissue beneath the belly button. If the skin above it is loose too, a mini won't reach it, because Dr. Rao says its results are limited to the lower abdomen.
- **Have your muscles separated?** Dr. Roughton says the muscles are usually stretched out by pregnancy or by having been significantly overweight. Read [how to check for diastasis recti](/blog/tummy-tuck-diastasis-recti-miami).
- **Is your weight stable?** The ASPS article says a mini suits people close to their ideal body weight.

This isn't something to settle at home. Your surgeon decides at your exam, and the type you need may not be the one you came in asking for.

## What about an extended mini or extended tummy tuck?

These sit between and beyond the two, and sources describe them differently.

- **Extended mini.** On Alluring's price list, this is a hip-to-hip incision without muscle repair. We found no outside source that describes it, so ask your surgeon to show you where the scar would sit.
- **Extended.** Dr. Rao says it suits a lot of excess, sagging skin on the abdomen and flanks, through a longer incision that stretches up and around the flanks. The Aesthetic Society says its scars are longer and often higher than in a full or mini. On our price list, the extended has a hip-to-hip incision, so ask your surgeon how far yours would run.

Our page answers [which tummy tuck fits, across all the types](/procedures/tummy-tuck-miami#which-tummy-tuck).

## Does a mini recover faster than a full?

Generally yes, though only one source we read splits recovery by type. [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/body/tummy-tuck/aftercare-recovery) agrees in general terms that recovery is typically longest for a traditional tummy tuck rather than a mini. These figures are from Dr. Rao's post, so treat them as one surgeon's view.

| | Mini | Full | Extended |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Back to work | 1 to 2 weeks | A couple of weeks, for non-strenuous work | 4 to 6 weeks |
| More vigorous activity | About 5 to 6 weeks | No figure in his post | About 12 weeks more after returning to work |
| Fully recovered | No figure in his post | Around 3 months | No figure in his post |

For a full tummy tuck, [Cindy Wu, MD](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/answers-to-common-tummy-tuck-questions), also on the ASPS website, says running or lifting should be avoided for 6 weeks after a tummy tuck that repairs the muscles. Our [week-by-week recovery guide](/how-long-to-recover-tummy-tuck) has the detail, and your surgeon sets your own schedule.

## What to ask at a consultation

If you are still planning, take these questions with you:

- Which type would you recommend for me, and why not the other?
- Would it include muscle repair?
- Where would the scar sit, and how long would it be?

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a mini tummy tuck fix a pooch?

Sometimes. The ASPS article says a mini is for a pooch or loose skin and fat only below the belly button. But [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22346-diastasis-recti) lists a pooch above or below the belly button as a sign of separated muscles, and skin removal alone won't close that gap. Read [how diastasis recti is repaired](/blog/tummy-tuck-diastasis-recti-miami), and ask your surgeon which one you have.

### Is a mini tummy tuck enough after having kids?

It can be, if your loose skin is only below the belly button and your muscles haven't separated. Many women need more, though. Dr. Roughton says most people need a full, and that the muscles are usually stretched out from pregnancy. If you may have more children, read [what a later pregnancy can change](/blog/pregnancy-after-tummy-tuck) first.

### Does a mini tummy tuck repair muscles?

Not at Alluring: our mini and extended mini don't include muscle repair. Elsewhere it varies. Dr. Rao says a mini strengthens muscles in the lower stomach, and the ASPS article says the surgeon may tighten them. Read [what a tummy tuck without muscle repair can and can't do](/what-is-a-tummy-tuck-without-muscle-repair-called), and ask any surgeon exactly what theirs includes.

### Which leaves a smaller scar, a mini or a full?

Usually a mini. Cleveland Clinic says a shorter procedure usually results in a smaller scar, and a mini generally has no cut around the belly button. Every type leaves a permanent line, so ask where yours would fall against the underwear you wear. Read [where a tummy tuck scar sits and how it fades](/blog/tummy-tuck-scars-miami) for more.

### Can I upgrade from a mini to a full tummy tuck later?

No source we read sets out how or when to do that, so ask your surgeon before you choose. [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/tummy-tuck/results) says that in some situations a single surgery may not achieve an optimal result, and another may be necessary. It makes more sense to decide on the right type with your surgeon up front than to count on a second operation.
