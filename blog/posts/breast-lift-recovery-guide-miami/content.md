## How long does breast lift recovery take?

About 1 to 2 weeks, with some limits lasting longer. [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/breast/breast-lift/aftercare-recovery) says it typically takes that long to recover from a breast lift, but bras, exercise and scars each run on their own schedule. These are published figures. Your surgeon's plan comes first.

## What happens week by week after a breast lift?

Recovery moves from rest, to light activity, to being cleared for exercise and a regular bra, and your scars keep fading after that.

| When | What's typical | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surgery day | Home the same day for most people | Cleveland Clinic |
| Days 1 to 5 | Chest stiff and sore for 2 to 5 days | The Aesthetic Society |
| About 1 week | Desk work and gentle activity | Cleveland Clinic |
| About 2 weeks | Discomfort, swelling and bruising ease | Cleveland Clinic |
| First 2 weeks | No lifting, driving, laundry or cleaning | The Aesthetic Society |
| Up to 6 weeks | Lifting, exercise and sex may not be cleared | Cleveland Clinic |
| About 8 weeks | A regular bra | Cleveland Clinic |
| A few months | Shape and position settle | ASPS |
| About 1 year | Most scar fading | Cleveland Clinic |

What each stage feels like:

- **Surgery day and the first night.** [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23298-breast-lift) says most people go home the same day and should have someone drive them and stay the first night. You may have bandages or a surgical bra, and possibly a thin tube near an incision to drain fluid.
- **Days 1 to 5.** Your chest may feel stiff and sore for 2 to 5 days, The Aesthetic Society says. Avoid straining, bending and lifting, which can increase swelling or cause bleeding. The Aesthetic Society says to get out of bed for short periods the day after surgery. After several days, you should be able to move around more comfortably. Cleveland Clinic says your team may ask you not to raise your arms above your head, and that any drains come out a few days after surgery.
- **Weeks 1 to 2.** Many women go back to desk work and gentle activities, such as walking, about 1 week after a breast lift, Cleveland Clinic says. The Aesthetic Society says stitches generally come out in stages over about 3 weeks, starting about 1 week after surgery.
- **Weeks 2 to 6.** After the first 2 weeks, The Aesthetic Society says to stay extremely gentle with your breasts for at least the next month.
- **Months 2 to 3.** Women can usually wear a regular bra at about 8 weeks, Cleveland Clinic says. The Aesthetic Society says side effects such as bruising, itching and numbness fade over the first few weeks, with some lasting up to 3 months.
- **Up to a year.** Cleveland Clinic says incisions may take several months to heal fully. Scars keep fading for about a year.

Our [breast lift page's recovery section](/procedures/breast-lift-miami#recovery) has the same timeline in short form.

## How do I prepare for breast lift recovery?

Stop nicotine, check your medicines with your surgeon, and set up help and your home before surgery day. Most of this list comes from [ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-lift/preparation) and [The Aesthetic Society](https://www.theaestheticsociety.org/procedures/breast/breast-lift/preparing-your-procedure):

- **Nicotine.** ASPS lists stopping smoking. Heather Furnas, MD, [writing on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/how-nicotine-sabotages-plastic-surgery), says your surgeon's advice may be to quit 3 to 6 weeks before surgery through 3 to 6 weeks after.
- **Medicines.** ASPS says to avoid aspirin, anti-inflammatory drugs and herbal supplements because they can increase bleeding. Give your surgeon a full list, and don't stop anything prescribed without asking.
- **Tests.** You may be asked to get lab testing or a medical evaluation, plus a baseline mammogram before surgery and another one after.
- **Alcohol.** The Aesthetic Society says to keep it to less than 2 to 3 drinks a week.
- **Help.** Arrange a driver and someone to stay at least the first night, as ASPS advises. If you have young children, arrange for someone to care for them for at least the first week, The Aesthetic Society advises.
- **Clothes.** Pick tops that open in front and slip-on shoes, so you don't have to raise your arms or bend.
- **Your home.** Keep what you need at counter level, with your phone and charger by the bed. Stock high-protein, low-sodium food, and set up wedge pillows or a recliner.

For the day itself, read [what happens during a breast lift](/procedures/breast-lift-miami#procedure).

## What is normal, and when should I call my surgeon?

Soreness, bruising, itching and some numbness are normal in the first weeks, while fever, a hot red breast or bleeding are reasons to call right away. The normal column below comes from The Aesthetic Society and Cleveland Clinic. The call column comes mainly from Cleveland Clinic, with wounds that reopen from Daniel Maman, MD, [writing on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/how-to-maintain-minimal-scarring-after-breast-augmentation) about augmentation, and leg symptoms from [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000132.htm).

| Usually normal | Call your surgical team right away |
| --- | --- |
| Chest stiff and sore for 2 to 5 days | Either breast becomes red, hot, swollen or painful |
| Bruising | One breast suddenly swells much more than the other |
| Swelling that gets better over time | A nipple or areola changes color or turns dark |
| Skin that feels tight | Bleeding, drainage, redness or swelling at an incision, or an incision that opens up |
| Numbness or changes in nipple sensitivity | Pain, redness or swelling in one leg |
| Itching around the incisions | Chest pain or dizziness |
| Firmness or fullness in the breast | Fever higher than 100.5°F |
| Incisions that stay red or pink for many months | Severe pain not helped with medicine |
| Mild, periodic discomfort in the first few weeks | Nausea or vomiting |

Normal swelling affects both breasts and slowly gets better. Swelling that comes on suddenly, is much worse on one side, or comes with heat or redness is a reason to call.

Chest pain, sudden shortness of breath or those leg symptoms can also be signs of a blood clot in the lung. MedlinePlus says to go to the emergency room or call 911 for those symptoms, so don't wait for a call back. Our [breast lift safety section](/procedures/breast-lift-miami#safety) covers the wider risks.

## How should I sleep after a breast lift?

On your back, propped up. The Aesthetic Society says to rest and sleep on your back in an inclined position, at a 25- to 45-degree angle, for the first days or if you have significant swelling. Wedge pillows or a recliner both work. Cleveland Clinic adds that some surgeons prefer you to sleep on your back for a few weeks, so check with yours.

## What bra should I wear after a breast lift?

A surgical or support bra first, and a regular bra usually at about 8 weeks, Cleveland Clinic says. [Its surgery center](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/cosmetic-plastic-surgery/procedures/breast-lift) has patients wear a surgical bra for the first week, then a support bra for several weeks. That's one center's protocol, and your surgeon sets yours.

Underwire waits longer. Karen Horton, MD, [quoted on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/articles/finding-comfort-after-breast-surgery-a-guide-to-compression-bras-and-postsurgical-garments), advises no underwire for at least 3 months, because a firm wire can press on an incision in the breast fold. She's speaking of breast surgery generally, and surgeons differ: Kelly Killeen, MD, in the same article, says most women return to normal bras in about 1 month, and to avoid bras without proper support for 3 to 6 months.

## When can I go back to work after a breast lift?

After about 1 week if you work at a desk. The Aesthetic Society says you should be able to return after a week or so, depending on your job. If you drive to work, arrange a ride: The Aesthetic Society says not to drive for the first 2 weeks. We found no published figure for physical jobs. The limits on lifting and vigorous activity still apply, so ask your surgeon what your job allows.

## What happens to swelling, numbness and scars over the first year?

Most swelling goes within weeks, numbness usually within months, and scars keep fading for about a year. Discomfort, swelling and bruising last about 2 weeks, Cleveland Clinic says.

Feeling in the nipples and breasts gradually returns over a few weeks or months; some people are numb for up to 1 year, and rarely the loss is permanent, Cleveland Clinic's surgery center says. The Aesthetic Society says it can take more than a year.

Incisions start red or pink and stay that way for many months, The Aesthetic Society says. Breast lift scars are permanent but fade a lot, most of it in about 1 year, Cleveland Clinic says. Our guide to [how breast lift scars fade](/blog/breast-lift-scars-miami) covers what helps.

[ASPS](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/breast-lift/results) says the results are visible right away, and the final shape appears over the next few months. You can see results in our [breast lift photos](/gallery/breast-lift); ask how long after surgery each was taken. Our timing guide covers [how long a lift lasts](/blog/breast-lift-miami-post-pregnancy).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is breast lift recovery painful?

Expect some pain when the anesthesia wears off, then soreness. The Aesthetic Society says your chest may feel stiff and sore for 2 to 5 days, with mild, periodic discomfort during the first few weeks. Take pain medicine as your surgeon prescribes it. If pain is severe or long-lasting, contact your surgeon right away.

### When can I shower or swim after a breast lift?

Ask your surgeon about showers, because it depends on your dressings and any drains. The Aesthetic Society says to avoid hot showers, hot tubs and saunas for 2 to 3 weeks. Baths, hot tubs and swimming wait until your incisions have fully healed: [UW Health's sheet on breast reconstruction incisions](https://patient.uwhealth.org/healthfacts/8525) says 4 to 6 weeks, and your surgeon clears it.

### When can I sleep on my side after a breast lift?

When your surgeon clears it. You start on your back, propped up, and Cleveland Clinic notes some surgeons prefer back sleeping for a few weeks. No source we read gives a set week for a lift. Our post on [sleeping on your side after breast surgery](/when-can-i-sleep-on-my-side-after-breast-reduction) explains how to ease into it.

### When can I sleep without a bra after a breast lift?

Ask your surgeon when you move to a regular bra, which Cleveland Clinic says is usually about 8 weeks after surgery. We found no separate published figure for going without one at night. Our post on [sleeping without a bra after breast surgery](/when-can-i-sleep-without-a-bra-after-breast-augmentation) covers the stages and the options at night.

### When can I drive after a breast lift?

Not in the first 2 weeks. The Aesthetic Society advises no lifting, driving, laundry or cleaning for the first 2 weeks, and your surgeon clears you to drive. Our post on [how to tell you're ready to drive](/how-long-after-breast-augmentation-can-i-drive) is written for augmentation, so use the longer 2-week figure for a lift.

### When can I work out after a breast lift?

You should be up for short periods from the day after surgery, The Aesthetic Society says. Many women are back to gentle activities such as walking about a week afterward, Cleveland Clinic says, and lifting, exercise and sex may not be cleared for up to 6 weeks. Ask your surgeon before chest work, and read [our post on push-ups and chest exercises](/when-can-i-do-push-ups-after-breast-augmentation), which is written for augmentation, so use the lift figure above.

### When can I pick up my child after a breast lift?

Not in the first 2 weeks, since The Aesthetic Society advises no lifting during that time. It also advises arranging care for young children for at least the first week. No source we read gives a weight limit for a lift. The nearest is for augmentation: Daniel Maman, MD, [writing on the ASPS website](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/blog/how-to-maintain-minimal-scarring-after-breast-augmentation), says to avoid lifting more than 5 to 10 pounds in the weeks after surgery. Ask your surgeon when lifting a toddler is safe for you.

### How long should I stay before flying home after a breast lift?

It depends on your surgery, so we won't give a number here. At Alluring, your surgeon, Dr. Victoria Karlinsky, MD, FACS, tells you in writing how many nights to stay in Miami before you're cleared to fly, along with your surgery, pre-op and follow-up dates. Ask about it at your consultation, so you can plan your time away.
